# Design records

- `worker-redesign-spec.md`: the final design for self-chosen mine sites, the fenceless woodland and real-water
  fishing, as agreed before implementation (written at commit f90ec43).
- `notes/step1.md` … `notes/step6.md`: what each implementation step did, its deviations from the spec and its
  review. `notes/scouts.md`: the scouts' structure reports and monster evasion. `notes/fixes.md`: the 17 problems
  found by the adversarial review of the redesign, the problems seen in campaigns, and how each was fixed.
  `notes/stuck-pits.md`: NPCs trapped in a ravine beside a settlement's wall, a miner pacing at its mine's landing
  and NPCs pacing in a gateway (a 15-day campaign), with causes, fixes, the GameTest and the live results.

These are historical working notes. Log files and world copies they mention lived in a temporary folder and are
gone. Where the notes disagree with the code, README.md or BALANCE.md, those win.

The Wither - "Queen of Three Skulls"
Female | wither | marksman | blight | tyrant | legendary | boss hero
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 1, 2026-11-18). She is one of the two boss
heroes: not among the 200, never a faction leader, and she joins a faction only through her token
(see "HOW SHE JOINS A FACTION"). She fights the same against players and NPCs and never yields. All
numbers are first values, tuned after play. Design: docs/heroes-v2/BOSS_HEROES.md section 4.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
The Wither is a weapon that learned to want things. She was built, not born: soul sand, three
skulls and a will forced into one body, and she woke into the world in an explosion. She is a cold,
proud queen flanked by two floating skulls, her side heads: one sneers, one hungers, and both obey
her. She fights like the vanilla boss: in the air she rains black skulls on everything at once;
at half health she grows an armour that turns arrows and comes down to tear at you; nearly beaten,
she sets her heads loose; and when you finally kill her, she summons herself again at a third of
her strength. Bound to the faction that carried her token, she mines for them with hands that
wither stone, draws the withering out of her own people, and makes rivals think twice.

LOOK
- Body: female, adult (her one body for good), slim, drawn a little tall [scale 104%: 1.89 blocks].
  Her feet never touch the ground: she floats a hand's breadth up, and her gown's hem trails into
  black smoke and soul-blue sparks.
- Hair: very long straight charcoal hair [#2A282D], the lowest strands ash grey [#8E8A93], fraying
  at the tips like smoke.
- Outfit (modest): a high-collared charcoal bodice whose bone-white stays look like a ribcage, long
  fitted sleeves, a floor-length smoky gown, a tattered charcoal cape lined in soul blue and split
  down the back to show a ridge of seven bone vertebrae, bone bracers with short spines.
- Head: a low crown of three charcoal-bone prongs, the middle one set with a soul-blue gem.
- Her two side heads: two floating wither skulls, charcoal with bone-white teeth and glowing
  soul-blue eye sockets, one on each side of her head at eye height, bobbing and turning with her.
- Weapon: a slender black sceptre topped by a wither rose with a blue glow at its heart.
- Colours: bone-pale skin [#E3DFDA], glowing soul-blue eyes [#9FE8FF], primary wither charcoal
  [#232127], secondary bone [#E6E0D2], accent soul fire [#3CC6E8], trim ash [#5E5A63], glow
  [#7FE2FF].
- Face: sharp, upturned eyes with glowing irises and no pupils, a dark shadow under each eye like a
  skull's sockets, thin arched brows, a small mouth. She blinks slowly and rarely [blink calm]. Her
  "happy" is a thin half-lidded smile; when angry her eyes blaze brighter.
- Idle quirks: her skulls bicker (they turn and snap at each other), she turns a wither rose in her
  fingers, she drifts in a slow half-turn with her arms folded.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 her rib stays, crown gem and hem glow soul blue and a faint blue shimmer
  hangs on her outline (the wither armour). In phase 3 the skulls are gone from her side (they are
  out hunting) and her hair's ash tips burn with soul fire. Reborn, her gown and cape lining blaze
  blue-white, the colour of the vanilla Wither's spawn charge.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is in plain health points (2 points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid
4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy, area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its
whole wind-up and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light
moves show only the animation. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits [16 for a move, 30 for the ultimate,
at the level-1 reference]: there is no extra damage to players and no protection from a one-hit
kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes may gang up on one target, and
any number of enemies on her.
Her fighting: focus style (the sceptre; skulls are her projectiles); duel style zoner in phase 1,
close and nasty from phase 2. She keeps about 10 blocks away in phase 1 [keep_distance 10, the
marksman default] and about 2 from phase 2 [the wither armour sets 2]; she goes for the weakest
enemy first (the marksman focus). Her element is blight (the Wither effect's own); she resists
blight strongly [+60], fire somewhat [+30], and takes 20% more from radiant damage. She can never
be given the Wither effect. Her moves never break, place or burn a block.

  Basic attack
    A black wither skull thrown from her open hand: it flies straight out to 16 blocks, 4 blight
    damage every 1.5 s, and one hit in four also gives the Wither effect [Wither I, 3 s]. She also
    throws them from the air.

  Abilities
    SKULL VOLLEY (skull_volley) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: three black skulls in a fan; each that hits you deals blight damage and
      withers you. She can cast it in the air.
      The tell: a quick lift of the sceptre while her crown gem flares (a light move: the
      animation only).
      Counter: step sideways out of the fan or put a block between you; a shield blocks it.
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; volley of 3, 10 degrees apart, range 18; 4 blight + Wither I
      3 s each; frames 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.4; cooldown 6 s]

    RUIN SKULL (ruin_skull) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: the dangerous blue skull. It flies slowly and bursts where it lands, hurting,
      withering and pushing everyone within 3 blocks. It never breaks blocks. Her answer when you
      eat (60%) and when three or more enemies crowd her (40%).
      The tell: a blue skull gathers between her palms for 1 s with a rising glassy hum; a strip
      on the ground shows its line.
      Counter: it is slow: sidestep it, and do not stand in a group.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; shot at 60% of a shot's speed, range 20, burst radius 3; 10
      blight + Wither II 4 s + knockback 1; frames 1.0 / 0.2 / 0.8; cooldown 14 s]

    WITHERING PULSE (withering_pulse) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: a wave of withering around her that hurts and pushes you away. Her answer to
      anyone who comes close (70%).
      The tell: she draws a breath and a disc grows under her for 1 s.
      Counter: step out of its 4 blocks during the wind-up.
      Numbers: [area, close; nova, radius 4; 4 blight + Wither I 3 s + knockback 2; frames 1.0 /
      0.1 / 0.8; cooldown 10 s]

    WITHER ROSE BLOOM (rose_bloom) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: a patch of black petals blooms where you stand and withers whoever stays in
      it for 4 s. Shields do not stop it; in phase 1 it is one of her answers to a raised shield
      (40%).
      The tell: she points the sceptre and a disc of petals grows under you for 1 s.
      Counter: walk out of it.
      Numbers: [area, mid; zone at the target, radius 3, 4 s, a pulse every 1 s; each pulse 2
      blight + Wither I 2 s; frames 1.0 / 4.0 / 0.8; cooldown 16 s]

    SOUL DASH (soul_dash) - boss phases 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: she hurls herself in a straight line 12 blocks long, hitting everyone on the
      way. If she ends against a wall she staggers. Her answer to a far target (50%).
      The tell: she leans back, her skulls shriek, and a strip grows along her line for 0.9 s.
      Counter: step off the line; better, stand in front of a wall so she slams into it.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; charge 12 at 180% speed, wall stun; 9 blight + knockback 2;
      frames 0.9 / 0.6 / 1.0; cooldown 12 s]

    GRAVE GRASP (grave_grasp) - boss phases 2, 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: three rakes of her withering hands; the last one withers you. It breaks a
      raised shield for 5 s, so from phase 2 it is her answer to blocking (50%).
      The tell: a quick string (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: back off out of her reach, or trade blows: her recovery is short but real.
      Numbers: [light, close; combo of 3; 3, 3 and 5 blight, the third + Wither I 3 s; breaks the
      guard; frames 0.45 / 0.6 / 0.4; cooldown 8 s]

    UNBOUND HEADS (unbound_heads) - boss phase 3 and the rebirth
      What it does: her two skulls leave her side and hunt on their own for 30 s, each after a
      different enemy (phase 3 starts by sending them; this move sends them again and re-forms a
      killed one).
      The tell: she tears the skulls from her side for 0.8 s, with a sound.
      Counter: kill them. Each has a tenth of her health and no armour (arrows work), and each one
      you kill breaks her crown: she takes 15% more damage for 6 s and loses poise.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; summons 2 skull familiars for 30 s; each floats 3 blocks from its
      enemy, at most 3 blocks above the ground, and spits a black skull every 2.5 s (3 blight +
      Wither I 2 s); health 10% of hers each; frames 0.8 / 0.2 / 0.8; cooldown 35 s]

    STRAY SKULLS (stray_skulls) - the rebirth only
      What it does: half-awake, her heads spit blue skulls at random all around her for 2.5 s,
      like the vanilla Wither's idle heads.
      The tell: her eyes roll back and her crown sparks for 1 s; a disc marks each impact.
      Counter: watch the discs and step between them, or stand more than 7 blocks away.
      Numbers: [area, any range; rain of 8 around herself, radius 7, 2.5 s, each impact 0.9 s
      after its disc appears; 6 blight + Wither I 3 s per impact; frames 1.0 / 2.5 / 1.0; cooldown
      18 s]

  Ultimates (both there from level 1; they share one energy bar)
    BIRTH BLAST (birth_blast)
      What it does: the Wither's spawn, all over again. She rises, turns the blue-white of the
      vanilla spawn charge and charges for 3 s, healing as she does; then she bursts and
      everything within 7 blocks is blasted, withered and thrown back. It never breaks blocks.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: a 1.6 s wind-up with a red disc of radius 7, her line
      ("Eleven heartbeats. Count them with me."), a wide sound, and the screen darkens for 2 s for
      the players near the fight; then the 3 s charge, eleven quickening heartbeats. She cannot be
      staggered during the wind-up.
      Counter: get more than 7 blocks away before the burst (you have 4.6 s), or break the charge:
      deal 15% of her maximum health while she charges (two hits or more: one hit is capped at
      12%) and the burst never comes, and she is open for 2 s.
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.6 / 3.0 charge + 0.2 / 1.8; heals 14 over the charge; nova
      radius 7: 24 blight + Wither II 5 s + knockback 3 up 1, softened near lava or a drop;
      channelled, breaks after 15% of her max health]

    SKULLFALL (skullfall)
      What it does: ten skulls rain on a circle around you for 3 s. She can call it from the air.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell ("Look up. All of you."), a red disc for each skull.
      Counter: read the discs and keep moving; leave the 6-block circle.
      Numbers: [ultimate, mid and far; rain of 10, radius 6 around the target, 3 s, each impact
      0.9 s after its disc; 4 blight + Wither I 3 s per impact; frames 1.6 / 3.0 / 1.5]

    Her ultimate energy and charge rate are set when she is built; the two ultimates are at least
    30 s apart.

  Passives
    THREE HEADS, THREE HUNGERS (three_hungers): while her skulls are at her side, every 3 s in a
    fight each skull spits a black skull at a different enemy within 20 blocks that it can see,
    not her own target when there is another (the vanilla Wither's side heads) [3 blight + Wither
    I 2 s each]. In a crowd, everyone near her is being shot at.
    UNDYING HUNGER (undying_hunger): in a fight she heals 1% of her maximum health every 5 s, and
    4 every time she kills something (the vanilla Wither regenerates and feeds on its kills).
    WITHERED BLOOD: the Wither effect never touches her. Everything else does: Slowness II still
    brings her down out of the air.

  The wither armour (phase 2 on, and the rebirth)
    Arrows, crossbow bolts, thrown tridents, wind charges and physical hero projectiles do nothing
    to her: they glance off with a blue spark, whoever shot them (you or an NPC archer). Magic
    projectiles, splash potions, melee and everything else hit as usual. While armoured she does
    not take to the air (only to reach someone on a pillar, and then arrows still knock her down),
    she closes to about 2 blocks, and she throws fewer volleys. It is the vanilla Wither's armour
    at half health: fight her close.

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: she is a boss in every fight, whether she fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with her until 10 s after the last one leaves.
  Her first fight against people each in-game day opens with her intro, and each player within 32
  blocks gets a title (her name, title and faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see her
  boss bar whenever she fights anyone: a star, "The Wither · Queen of Three Skulls", four phase
  pips (three phases and her rebirth, shown from the start), a lightning mark when an ultimate is
  ready, and a square for each artifact she wears. Her bar is meant to be purple, the vanilla
  Wither's.
- Health: her base health 28 x4.0 (the legendary scale; she never leads, so no leader bonus) x1.3
  for a marksman [health scale 400], always: it never changes with who or how many fight her.
  Level 1 / 10 / 20: 146 / 169 / 195, and her rebirth adds 30% more: 44 / 51 / 59. At level 10 a
  player with an iron sword needs about 37 s of landed hits before the rebirth and about 11 s after
  it (1.6 swings a second, half of them landing), plus what she heals: longer than any of the first
  eight heroes, as a legendary should be. Attackers add up: six iron soldiers who all reach her need
  about 7 s of blows, 10 s with the rebirth. No single hit, from anyone, takes more than 12% of her
  health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no attacker (lava, fire,
  drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 90. Every hit fills her poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6,
  mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8; arrows add nothing while
  she is armoured], and a parry (a shield raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands)
  adds 25. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full poise staggers her:
  2.5 s dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum grows 20%. No stagger
  during her intro, a phase change, her rebirth or her ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  and the rebirth (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes
  between two moves. A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too, so no move reaches you sooner than
  that wait plus its wind-up. During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from
  behind. In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100%: Skull Volley, Ruin Skull, Withering Pulse, Wither Rose Bloom; she takes to
      the air (below) and her skulls shoot at everyone.
    Phase 2, from 50%: the wither armour (arrows glance off; she comes down and fights close), Soul
      Dash, Grave Grasp. "Half of me is still more than all of you. Your arrows are nothing now."
    Phase 3, from 20%: her skulls are torn loose and hunt on their own (Unbound Heads); she moves
      15% faster and claws more. "Go, my heads. Feed."
    The rebirth: the first time in a fight that a blow would kill her, she does not die: she sinks
      into a T of soul sand and three skulls, glows blue-white, and stands again with 30% of her
      health, her skulls back at her side, adding Stray Skulls. "You think the Wither dies once? I
      was built to be summoned." Once per fight; the next killing blow is final.
  Each phase change takes 1.2 to 2 s: she takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered (a glint
  shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes everyone within 4 blocks back (no
  damage; softened near lava or a drop), she says the line, her look changes, and the bar marks
  the pip.
- In the air (phase 1 only): she rises at most 6 blocks over the ground or the highest enemy
  standing on a block, stays at most 8 s, then lands for at least 6 s; she spends at most 40% of
  any 30 s in the air and at most 4 s at a time out of everyone's melee reach. Up there she throws
  skulls and volleys (at most three volleys a flight) and may call Skullfall. Arrows bring her down:
  five arrows, from you or an NPC archer, fill her air poise and she falls, lies 2 s taking 20% more
  damage, and cannot take off for 12 s. On the ground she still floats about a block up, within
  reach of a sword.
- Weak points:
  - Smite: a weapon with the Smite enchantment hurts her as it hurts undead monsters [+2.5 per
    level], whoever wields it (an NPC soldier whose sword the faction's enchanter gave Smite
    counts the same). "Holy steel? How quaint. ...How it stings."
  - Channel: Birth Blast's charge breaks after 15% of her maximum health in damage and leaves her
    open for 2 s.
  - Charge into a wall: a Soul Dash that ends against a wall staggers her. "Walls... I hate walls."
  - Broken crown: each of her skulls you kill in phase 3 gives her 30 poise and makes her take 15%
    more damage for 6 s. "Careful with them. They are the only ones who listen."
  - Radiant damage hurts her 20% more.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; she reads an NPC the same way: a soldier
  raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating draw the same answers at the same
  chances):
  - You come close -> Withering Pulse (70%, at most every 10 s).
  - You raise a shield -> Wither Rose Bloom (phase 1, 40%) or Grave Grasp (from phase 2, 50%).
  - You draw a bow -> Skull Volley (phase 1, 50%; later her armour answers bows).
  - You eat -> Ruin Skull (60%).
  - You are above her or in the air -> Skull Volley (60%).
  - Three or more enemies engage her -> Ruin Skull (40%).
  - You are far away -> Soul Dash (from phase 2, 50%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever she fights: +15% damage, shorter waits (x0.7), poise
  maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and line ("Enough. Wither, all of you, wither!"). All
  her damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. She also enrages at once when a hero of her side
  dies within 24 blocks of her.
- Intro: she rises from a crouch in the air, arms folded; her skulls snap to her sides and her eyes
  ignite. "Who summoned me this time? ...You? Then kneel, and wither."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press her; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When she is losing: she fights to the death [aggression 80 + honour 45 - caution 35 = 90, well
  over 65; her file also says last_stand outright, so tuning her traits can never make her run]:
  the vanilla Wither never flees. At 10% of her health [a third of her retreat_below 34, kept 10
  under her last phase] she has her last-stand beat once per fight: "Run? The Wither does not run.
  The Wither ends things." Then her rebirth, then the end. At zero health after the rebirth she
  dies like anyone: nothing stops a killing blow, and the bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "Soul
  sand... remembers. I will be summoned again."
- Rewards: only when she dies, to whoever killed her (the side of the last player, NPC or hero
  that hurt her in her last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear, a hero
  crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day), and every artifact she wears drops
  where she fell, her Hollow Star included (it never despawns and survives fire and explosions).
  Soldiers of a winning faction pick it all up and carry it home.
- Revival: 10 minutes after her death, whoever killed her, at her home base: over the last 10 s a
  T of soul sand and three skulls assembles beside the reporting station (only a picture: no
  blocks, no damage) and she bursts out of it. She has no death ability of her own; a hero of her
  faction with the revival_rite talent would bring her back sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Phase 1 is the vanilla Wither in the air: shoot her down. Five arrows down her for 12 s, and on
   the ground she floats within reach of a sword. Spread out: her two skulls shoot at whoever she
   is not shooting.
2. From 50% your arrows are useless: bring a sword, an axe or a mace, ideally with Smite, and fight
   her close. A tunnel or a low ceiling suits you: she will not take to the air again anyway.
3. Never stand within 7 blocks when Birth Blast fires. You get 4.6 s of warning: run, or break the
   charge with two big hits (15% of her health) and enjoy 2 s of free hits.
4. Do not hide behind a shield: Wither Rose Bloom ignores it and Grave Grasp breaks it.
5. Stand with a wall at your back when she lines up a Soul Dash: if she slams into it, she staggers.
6. In phase 3, kill her skulls (a tenth of her health each, no armour): each one breaks her crown
   for 6 s.
7. She is not dead when she dies. Save something for the rebirth, and keep 7 blocks away when the
   blue skulls start raining.
8. Milk will not help you against her blood, but it cures the Wither effect she puts on you.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: flyer (ceiling III) and, uniquely, hover (ceiling II) on the ground. She has every
feature from level 1; the numbers grow from 60% at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20.
Mobility never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue
every NPC has is the one exception).
- She always floats: about a block above the ground or water, never trampling farmland, drifting
  over small gaps, always within reach of a sword. (Flyers cannot hover today: her design asks the
  format to allow it for her.)
- Take-off: 0.6 s (the tell: her gown lifts and smoke pours from the hem). Landing 0.4 s.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Stamina (seconds of powered flight)     36          / 47.4        / 60
    Cruise speed (blocks per second)        6.5         / 7.2         / 8.0
    Climb rate (blocks per second)          2.4         / 3.2         / 4.0
    Glide: sink, forward (b/s)              3.3, 7.7    / 2.5, 8.8    / 2.0, 10.0
    Glide time (s), gusts                   12, 2       / 15.8, 2     / 20, 2
    Take-off needs (stamina)                33%         / 25%         / 20%
    Stamina back on the ground (per s)      1.8         / 2.4         / 3.0
    Hover height (blocks), gap, step        0.6, 2, 1.3 / 0.8, 2, 1.4 / 1.0, 3, 1.5
- Out of fights: she flies the long legs of her trips and to her mine; in a thunderstorm she
  floats on foot.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): only phase 1 has flights (above); from phase 2
  only a reach flight up to someone on a pillar or a wall. If you stand where she cannot float to
  you, she flies up to you, and if she is walled in she breaks straight out (below).
- Raids: as a non-leader she joins her base's raids like a soldier [raids: joins]. She scouts the
  target first by circling it, crosses the wall from the air when she has a way out, and inside she
  breaches the wall for her soldiers when they are held outside, otherwise she shoots the
  defenders' archers. Defending, she patrols over her walls.
- She does not escape (she fights to the death).

WHAT SHE BREAKS, AND WHAT SHE NEVER DOES
- Her moves never place, break or burn a block: skulls, bursts, the dash and Birth Blast hurt and
  push only living things. A Wither defending her own base never cracks its walls.
- Withering hands: her bare hands break blocks as fast as an unenchanted diamond pickaxe (with
  the drops that tool gives, and no wear). Never bedrock, barriers, portal frames or reinforced
  deepslate (the vanilla Wither cannot break them either). She uses them:
  - at work (she is a miner);
  - when walled in during a fight: she breaks straight out at once (the old trick of boxing a
    Wither in does not hold her);
  - to reach someone she cannot get to, after 10 s, as every hero does;
  - on a raid, when her soldiers are stuck outside: she breaks a 2-wide, 3-high hole in the enemy
    wall beside the gate (about 2-4 s through stone bricks, much longer through obsidian), once
    per raid. She is the only hero who breaks a faction's walls. The defenders rebuild it later.
- Never her own side's blocks: nothing of her own faction's or an ally's buildings, and nothing
  inside her own bases except her mine. With the game rule mobGriefing off she makes no breach.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: miner (when she is not fighting). She mines with her withering hands (she presses a palm to
  the stone and it greys and crumbles) and answers every alarm.
- Proficiency (tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from 20): work speed +15 / 25 / 35%, backpack
  21 / 24 / 27 slots (a worker has 18), reach +0.5 / 1 / 1.5 blocks, work hunger -10 / 15 / 20%,
  work XP +10 / 20 / 30%; no tool wear at all (she uses no tool). Each level she gains rolls a
  miner's affix, as for any NPC.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is, not
  on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never the sum):
  - surveyor (her specialty): mine sites searched +8 / 16 / 24 blocks farther, the diamond layer one
    level sooner; when she mines herself, Fortune +1 / +2 / +3 on the ores and vein following one
    block wider.
  - purifier: allies at her base (NPCs, heroes and member players alike) lose poison, wither and
    hunger effects every 30 / 20 / 10 s: the queen of withering draws it out of her own people. At
    home it counts one tier higher, and she also lifts Slowness, Weakness and Mining Fatigue from
    allies within 8 blocks of her once every 60 / 45 / 30 s.
  - intimidation (a later milestone): rival war councils see her base's defence x1.15 / 1.3 / 1.45,
    and raiders who see her on the walls fall back 10 / 15 / 20% sooner.

HOW SHE JOINS A FACTION: THE TOKEN
- The first time a vanilla Wither dies in your world (since the mod was added), in any dimension
  and whoever kills it, a chest named "The Wither's Remains" appears on solid ground below where it
  died (never on the Nether roof, never in lava, never over the void). Inside lies the Wither Hero
  Token. Later Withers give no chest.
- The token glows, never despawns, and shrugs off fire, lava, explosions and cactus. If it falls
  into the void, sits in lava for 30 s, or is destroyed, a new one appears in the chest 2 minutes
  later. There is only ever one; any copy is a "Hollow Wither Token" that does nothing. It cannot
  go into an ender chest, a shulker box or a bundle, and a player in creative mode cannot hold it.
- Bring it inside the walls of one of your faction's bases (you, or any NPC or hero of your
  faction, carrying it) and it is used at once: in a 10 s show at the reporting station a T of soul
  sand and three skulls glows blue and bursts, harmlessly, and she steps out and joins your
  faction at level 1, wearing her Hollow Star. A token that simply lies inside a base for 5 s
  joins that base's faction.
- Whoever carries it drops it when they die, even with keepInventory on, so it can be stolen on
  the way home. Carriers trail soul particles; any NPC that sees a carrier recognises it, and
  enemies go for the carrier first.
- The NPC factions want it too: a faction learns of the chest only if its people saw the Wither die
  or its scouts see the chest's column of soul fire (from 64 blocks); then its council may send an
  expedition, intercept a carrier its scouts saw, and escort its own carrier home. Your own
  faction's NPCs do the same for you.
- If her faction ever loses its last base, she turns back into her token where she stands (or
  where its last heart fell, if she was dead), keeping her level, for the conquerors to take.

NOT A LEADER
She is never drafted as a faction leader and never founds a faction: she serves the faction that
carried her token. She does not count against the number of heroes a faction may have, and one
faction may hold both boss heroes.

HER ARTIFACT: HOLLOW STAR
A nether star burnt black with a soul-blue light inside, worn as a brooch at her collar (you can see
it on her). It cannot be crafted: it comes with her the first time she joins, and it drops where
she dies, for anyone to take. Only one exists; if it is destroyed it comes back to her.
- On any hero: ability power +12%, drain +2%, blight resistance +10%, health +5% (60% of that at
  level 1, all of it at 20), and Withering Brand: the first hit of each of its moves withers the
  enemy (Wither I, 3 s), and an enemy it withered that dies within 3 s heals it 3.
- On the Wither herself (her bond): also blight damage +6%, health +5%, drain +2%; her brand is
  Wither II and a withered enemy that dies heals her 4 and gives her 10 energy (Third Hunger);
  Skull Volley throws 4 skulls instead of 3, and Ruin Skull's burst is 4 blocks wide.

PERSONALITY AND VOICE
- Temperament: tyrant. Traits: aggression 80, honour 45, ambition 90, caution 35, greed 65,
  sociability 20, loyalty 55, volatility 45. Next to a typical tyrant she keeps her word better
  (honour 45 against 30: the bargain of whoever carried her token) and is more loyal (55 against
  40: she is bound).
- In a fight: bold (aggression 65 or more), so her attacking moves weigh a little more [x1.18;
  zoning and defensive x0.91]. She goes for the weakest enemy, and she chases a hero that runs from
  her (honour under 60).

Her own, on top of the shared library: one for each move (Skull Volley, Ruin Skull, Withering
Pulse, Wither Rose Bloom, Soul Dash, Grave Grasp, Unbound Heads, Stray Skulls); Birth Blast with a

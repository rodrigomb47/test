Ilsabet Frostmere - "The Rime Vow Knight"
Female | knight | vanguard | frost | righteous | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: she fights the same against
players and NPCs, and she never yields: see "When she is losing"). All numbers are first values,
tuned after play.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
A knight-commander who swore to keep the weak behind her shield. She never strikes a foe who runs
and never breaks her word, and she will hold a gate until the last of her people is inside. In a
one-on-one fight she is a counter fighter: patient, with a guard that answers your melee, so you
bait it out and hit after her swing. Out of battle she guards the gate; as a faction leader she is
the Knight-Commander of the Rime Vow.

LOOK
- Body: female, standard build, drawn a little large [scale 104%].
- Hair: ash-blonde [#D9CDB4], braided into a crown.
- Outfit: light steel plate with a long blue-and-white tabard bearing a frost-lily emblem, a short
  blue cape and greaves.
- Weapon: a longsword in her right hand and a kite shield with the frost-lily emblem in her left.
- Colours: skin [#F3D9C9], pale blue eyes [#7FB7D9], primary steel [#B8C7D6], secondary white
  [#EEF3F7], accent blue [#3D6FA8], trim gold [#CFAF5A], metal [#C9D1D9], glow ice blue [#A8E6FF].
- Face: almond eyes, light lashes [lashes 2], straight brows, a soft mouth. She blinks at a normal
  pace (every 2.5 to 6 s) [blink normal].
- Idle quirks: a shield tap, a salute, a glance back at her allies.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 a visored helm snaps down over her face; in phase 3 a planted standard
  appears on her back and the edge of her blade glows with frost.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of her attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, her face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where she stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3
or enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on her.
Her fighting: sword and shield; duel style counter. She closes to melee, protects her allies and
goes for the strongest enemy first (the righteous focus: the one with the most maximum health). Her
element is frost (players take it normally). What her effects do, to you and to an NPC alike: a stun
stops you for its seconds (you cannot walk, jump, attack or use items; the camera still turns), as
it stops a soldier, and nobody can be stunned again for twice as long; a freeze works as powder snow
does (slowed, frost on the screen, freeze damage), and leather armour stops it, as in vanilla; a
taunt turns monsters and enemy NPCs on her, and every taunted enemy, you included, deals 50% less
damage to anyone but her while it lasts and sees her glow. Cooldowns and how often she picks each
move are set when she is built.

  Basic attack
    A sword-and-shield string: cut, cut, shield bash, and she really blocks with her shield between
    swings. The design measures her boss health before her shield blocks, so her shield makes the
    fight longer. The damage of each swing comes from her attack power and her sword; how often she
    raises the shield and what it stops are set when she is built.

  Abilities
    SHIELD BASH (shield_bash) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she lunges 3 blocks behind her shield and slams it into you, stunning you and
      knocking you back.
      The tell: the shield goes up high for 0.8 s while a strip grows on the ground ahead of her.
      Counter: step aside (it is a short lunge), or raise your own shield just before it lands: a
      parry (+25 poise). After the hit she needs 0.8 s to recover.
      Numbers: [heavy, close; dash 3; 70% + stun 1 s + knockback 2; frames 0.8 (shield raised high)
      / 0.2 / 0.8]

    OATH STRIKE (oath_strike) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: two quick sword cuts; the second one freezes you.
      The tell: a short 0.45 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: back off after the first cut, or block both (light moves are blockable like any hit).
      Leather armour stops the freeze.
      Numbers: [light, close; combo of 2 strikes: 60%, then 80% + freeze 2 s; frames 0.45 / 0.35 /
      0.4]

    RIME WALL (rime_wall) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: her allies near her get a shield of golden hearts, and the enemies around her
      are taunted. She likes it best with two or more allies near her, and it is her answer to an
      allied hero under half health (80%).
      The tell: a 0.6 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: it does not hurt you, so use the moment to hit her or her helpers.
      Numbers: [light, any range; nova, radius 5: taunt 3 s; allies within 6: shield 6 for 10 s;
      prefers 2 or more allies near; frames 0.6 / 0.1 / 0.5]

    TURNING SWEEP (turning_sweep) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: her frost blade turns through a half-circle around her over one second. It is
      her answer to an enemy behind her, a player or a soldier (70%).
      The tell: a ground mark grows around her for 1 s (the design gives no mark shape for sweeps:
      set when she is built), then the blade turns through the half-circle for 1 s.
      Counter: get more than 4 blocks away, or put a block between you (the cut is stopped by
      blocks); then come back in for its 0.8 s recovery.
      Numbers: [area, close; sweep 4, 180 degrees, over 1.0 s (the sweep minimum); 70% frost; frames
      1.0 / 1.0 / 0.8]

    FROST LANCE CHARGE (frost_lance_charge) - boss phases 2, 3 (new in phase 2)
      What it does: she charges 14 blocks in a straight line, freezing and throwing back whoever she
      hits, and stops by planting her shield. It is her answer to a drawn bow (50%).
      The tell: she sets herself for 1 s while a long strip on the ground shows her path.
      Counter: step off the strip, ideally with a wall behind you: a charge that ends against a wall
      staggers her (see Weak points). A charge stops at walls, drops and water, and she skips it
      when water or lava lies on the path. Her 1 s recovery is your opening.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; charge 14, speed 170%, wall stun; 100% + freeze 2 s + knockback
      2; frames 1.0 / 0.8 / 1.0 (plants the shield to stop)]

    FROZEN STANDARD (frozen_standard) - boss phase 3 only (new in phase 3)
      What it does: she raises a frozen field around where she stands for 10 s: her allies inside it
      hit harder, and enemies inside it are slowed.
      The tell: a disc grows around her for 1.2 s.
      Counter: fight her outside the 6-block field, or pull her helpers out of it; it lasts 10 s.
      Numbers: [area, any range; zone at herself, radius 6, 10 s, a pulse every 1 s; allies inside
      +15% damage; enemies inside Slowness I; frames 1.2 / 0.1 / 0.8]

    CALL THE GUARD (call_the_guard) - boss phase 3 only (new in phase 3)
      What it does: she calls her soldiers: up to three real soldiers of her faction within 32
      blocks take her target. Nothing is spawned, so far from her people no one comes.
      The tell: a 0.6 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: fight her away from her base and her soldiers; near them, deal with the soldiers or
      keep moving.
      Numbers: [light, any range; on herself, call_guards 3; frames 0.6 / 0.1 / 0.4]

    BULWARK (state bulwark) - from boss phase 2
      At the phase 2 change her visor snaps down and she settles into Bulwark: her armour holds
      better and she is much harder to push, but her back becomes an even bigger weakness.
      Numbers: [armour +25%, knockback resistance +30, her back weakness doubles (see Weak points)]

  Ultimate
    VOW OF WINTER (vow_of_winter)
      What it does: she raises her sword while a ring of ice crystals grows on the ground around
      her, then frost bursts out, hurting and freezing every enemy within 6 blocks and shielding her
      allies within 8.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: the 1.6 s wind-up, a spoken line, a wide sound, a red ground
      mark with the ring of ice crystals, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the
      fight. She cannot be staggered during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when
      her ultimate is ready.
      Counter: get more than 6 blocks away from her during the wind-up; leather armour stops the
      freeze. Hit her in the 1.5 s recovery.
      Numbers: [ultimate; nova, radius 6: 14 frost + freeze 3 s; allies within 8: shield 10; frames
      1.6 / 0.2 / 1.5. Set when she is built: her ultimate line, her ultimate energy and charge
      rate, and the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    OATH KEEPER (oath_keeper): when an ally within 12 blocks drops below 35% health, that ally takes
    20% less damage for 6 s; at most once every 15 s (and once per ally every 20 s).

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: she is a boss in every fight, whether she fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with her (it hurt her in the last 15 s, or is
  her target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. Her first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with her intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which she takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and her intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (her name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever she fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, her name and title, her phase pips and a lightning
  mark when her ultimate is ready. (Her bar title is set when she is built.)
- Health: her base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.0 for a
  vanguard, always: it never changes with who or how many fight her. At level 10 that is 144 health
  (165 as a leader): about 31 s (36 s) of landed hits for a player with an iron sword at 1.6 swings
  a second, half of them landing, counted before her shield blocks; an iron-sword soldier needs
  about a fifth longer, and attackers add up (six soldiers who all reach her need about a sixth of
  one soldier's time). A whole duel lasts two to three times its hitting time once you dodge her
  wind-ups. No single hit, from anyone (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their arrows), takes
  more than 12% of her health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no
  attacker (lava, fire, drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 140 (the vanguard value). Every hit fills her poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x weapon:
  sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], and
  a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands)
  adds 25. A Frost Lance Charge that ends against a wall adds 40, and her charge_wall weak point
  makes it a stagger at once. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full
  poise staggers her: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its
  maximum grows 20% for the rest of the fight. No stagger during her intro, a phase change or her
  ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where she faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Shield Bash, Oath Strike, Rime Wall, Turning Sweep.
    Phase 2, from 60%: her visor snaps down; she enters Bulwark; adds Frost Lance Charge.
      "Then I will stand against you properly."
    Phase 3, from 25%: adds Frozen Standard and Call the Guard; the standard appears on her back and
      her blade glows.
      "To me, Rime Vow! Hold the line!"
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (her exact length is set when she is built): she takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), she says the line, her
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Back: the bonus for hitting her from behind (more than 120 degrees from where she faces) doubles
    [during a recovery, +30% more instead of +15%]. From phase 2, Bulwark doubles her back weakness
    again; the exact number is set when she is built.
  - Charge into a wall [charge_wall]: when her own Frost Lance Charge ends against a wall, she
    staggers (2.5 s kneeling, +25% damage taken).
  - The line or prop that hints at each weak point is set when she is built.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; their cooldowns are set when she is built).
  She reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You stand behind her -> Turning Sweep (70%).
  - You draw a bow -> Frost Lance Charge (50%; phases 2 and 3).
  - An allied hero falls under half health -> Rime Wall (80%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever she fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter
  waits between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All
  her damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. She also enrages at once when the heart of her base
  is attacked while she defends it. (The design gives 240 s for her as a leader; the 180 s otherwise
  is the design's default.)
- Intro: a salute with her blade. "Lower your weapon and I will lower mine. I keep my word."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press her; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When she is losing: she never escapes [last stand: aggression 50 + honour 90 - caution 60 = 80, 65
  or more: her vow is to stand between the enemy and her people]. Under 8% of her health [where an
  escaping hero would go: a third of her retreat_below 22%, raised to the 8% floor] she has her
  last-stand beat once per fight (her last-stand line and a determined face; nothing else changes:
  phase 3, Frozen Standard and the enrage are her escalation) and holds the line until her enemies
  are dead or gone or she dies. "My word is given. Not one step back." When her raid falls back she
  finishes the fight she is in, then walks home; when the heart of the base she defends falls, she
  fights on where she stands. The same against you, soldiers, another hero or monsters.
  - At zero health she dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow. The
    bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "Hold the line... hold..."
  - A hero that runs from her: with honour 90 she does not chase or strike it; she lets it go and
    takes another target.
- Rewards: only when she dies, to whoever killed her (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt her in her last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear (her kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero she comes back 10 minutes after her death, whoever killed her
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at her home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); her kit items vanish at death and return with her. She has no death ability of her own. A
  hero of her faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring her back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Wear at least one piece of leather armour. Oath Strike, Frost Lance Charge and her ultimate all
   freeze, and the freeze ignores anyone in leather armour (the vanilla powder-snow rule).
2. Work her back. Hits from behind during her recoveries pay double the extra, and from phase 2
   Bulwark makes her back weaker still. She answers you standing behind her with Turning Sweep
   (70%): get more than 4 blocks out when its mark appears, then come back in for its 0.8 s
   recovery.
3. From phase 2, make her charge into a wall. Stand at range with a wall at your back (drawing a bow
   invites the charge, 50%) and step off the strip when it appears: if she runs into the wall she
   staggers, which means 2.5 s of hits at +25% damage.
4. Parry her heavy hits and bring a heavy weapon. Raise your shield just before Shield Bash (her
   shield goes up high for 0.8 s first) or Frost Lance Charge lands (+25 poise each). Axes and maces
   fill her big poise bar (140) faster (x1.6 and x2.0).
5. Fight her away from her people. In phase 3 Call the Guard brings up to three real soldiers from
   within 32 blocks and Frozen Standard makes her allies hit harder, while Rime Wall and Oath Keeper
   protect them. Attacking her base's heart while she defends it enrages her at once.
6. Get out of the circles: leave Frozen Standard's 6-block field (it slows you), and be more than 6
   blocks from her when the red ring of Vow of Winter appears.
7. She will not run, and she will not let you through. Under 8% she holds her ground to the end,
   with her soldiers and her shields around her: keep leather on and keep working her back.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: charger (ceiling III) and snowstride (ceiling I). She has every feature from level
1; the numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20.
Mobility never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue
every NPC has is the one exception).
- From level 1: walking up full blocks without jumping [step 1.0]; the traversal charge (on a
  straight, clear stretch of path she breaks into a charging run; it stops at doors, fences, water
  and drops; travel only); cobwebs and sweet berry bushes never slow her, in fights too; harder to
  push back while travelling (off in every fight, against anyone); a longer safe fall; less slowing
  on soul sand, honey and other slow blocks. Snowstride I: she never freezes, and powder snow does
  not slow her (she still sinks into it). Her jump is a worker's: she has no leaping or climbing
  trait.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Traversal charge                        +36% every 20 s / +47% every 15 s / +60% every 12 s
    Knockback resistance while travelling   +0.18 / +0.24 / +0.30
    Safe fall (blocks)                      6.6  / 7.7  / 9.0
    Movement efficiency                     0.60 / 0.79 / 1.00
      (1.00 = soul sand, honey and other slow blocks do not slow her at all)
- Out of fights: on guard duty she holds the gate, and she is the first to meet an enemy hero coming
  over her walls.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): Frost Lance Charge is unchanged. In every fight
  her knockback bonus and traversal charge switch off (her Bulwark gives her footing instead); her
  step height, safe fall, web immunity and snow footing stay, so she fights in powder snow unslowed.
  If you stand where she cannot walk to you (a pillar, a pen, a ledge; an NPC archer on a tower the
  same), she uses her far-range moves, after 10 s digs and builds her way up at player speed, and
  after 30 s gives up with a taunt and resets.
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader she always goes on her base's raids and is picked first [raids:
  leads, the vanguard default]; as a leader she joins only joint raids and counter-attacks (below).
  Today's base walls are 5 blocks high and she can neither climb nor jump them, so as a charger she
  leads her soldiers through the gate or the breach. Inside she takes one goal: she opens the way
  when her soldiers are held outside, hunts a defending hero she sees inside, and otherwise goes for
  the heart with her party; a goal ends after 60 s without progress. Defending, she joins the two
  nearest free guards against an enemy hero on or inside her walls.
- Escaping: her charging run comes back on, and she may go wherever her legs take her, even where no
  pursuer can follow.
- Signature animation: none of her own in the design; she uses the shared charging run and the skid
  to a stop.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: guard (when she is not leading). She patrols with the guards, answers alarms and holds the
  gate; cobwebs never slow her, from level 1.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): as a guard she answers alarms from farther away instead of working faster [+8 / +12 / +16
  blocks]. She also has a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18], less wear on her
  gear [-10 / -20 / -30%], less hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 /
  +30%]. Each level she gains rolls a guard affix, as for any NPC.
- Presence (her specialty as a guard): rally and warden_of_walls count one tier higher while she is
  at her base (inside the walls or within 16 blocks) and not on a raid; at tier III they use the
  tier IV numbers. Her personal touch, from rally: when her faction pays for a recruit, two recruits
  may come instead of one [chance 15 / 25 / 35%; within the population caps; once per payment].
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is,
  inside the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never
  the sum; the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of her faction at tier I while
  she leads):
  - rally* (realm): recruits cost 10 / 15 / 20% less food [with Presence at home: 15 / 20 / 25%].
  - warden_of_walls: her base's defence alarm reaches farther and the repair check looks over more
    of the base [alarm radius +8 / +16 / +24 blocks, repair scan budget x1.5 / x2 / x2.5; with
    Presence at home +16 / +24 / +32 blocks and x2 / x2.5 / x3]. The talent table also gives it a
    personal effect (the hero repairs breaches herself with real blocks from storage, at 1.5x a
    builder's speed), but her own entry names only rally's; whether she does this, and how the
    talent's repair budget combines with her leader's repair budget, are set when she is built.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Knight-Commander of the Rime Vow.
- Faction names: "Order of the Rime Vow" (the first unused name is taken), then "The Frostmere
  Guard". Short name: "Rime Vow". Motto: "No one falls while we stand."
- Colours: primary frost blue [#5E9BD1], secondary white [#EEF3F7], accent gold [#CFAF5A]. The
  faction blue is lighter than her tabard's [#3D6FA8], which was too close to the Still Crane's
  indigo. They show in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, her sash and her NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: light blue, with a white diagonal cross (an X) and a yellow border [base light_blue; cross
  white; border yellow] (the knights' emblem). Real banners hang at the station and the gates, and
  members get a banner item. Her faction's wool, carpets and beds are light blue, with white as the
  second dye.
- Base style: frost_hall, a Hall: polished diorite walls, spruce log frames, spruce plank floors,
  dark prismarine roofs and stone bricks round the edge; signs read like "Rime Vow Hall". Culture:
  west (recruits get western names). Economy focus: defence (no civilian role moves up; one more
  home defender instead) and people (chefs and haulers are hired ahead of other civilians, and the
  recruitment interval is x0.9).
- Her day (court duty): a short stop at the station at dawn; by day a walk between the station, the
  gates and the heart, greeting member players (her first talent, rally, has no workplace); the
  heart at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by any alarm. She answers every alarm
  and guards the heart in a defence, says her founding line when the faction starts, and announces
  raids, attacks on her base, a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit milestones in her own voice
  (her own lines for these are not written yet; her temperament's fallback lines fill in).
- If she dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until she returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more); a raid she was on carries on without her [(caution + 1 - loyalty) / 2 =
  0.375, not over 0.5]. She never escapes, so a lost fight of hers ends in her death, at her own
  gate as often as not. If her faction's last base falls she becomes an exiled free hero, never a
  leader again in that campaign.
- How her personality steers the faction. Her traits (out of 100): aggression 50, honour 90,
  ambition 50, caution 60, greed 15, sociability 60, loyalty 85, volatility 25. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids at about the neutral pace: about every 31 minutes, or 1.56 in-game days [37440 ticks,
    interval x1.04; neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. Her honour and caution hold back the first
    one: not before 1.3 intervals [1].
  - Attacks only with an edge: attack odds needed 0.727 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.275 of the
    interval [0.25], at odds 1.58 [1.5].
  - She neither leads ordinary raids (that needs aggression 65 or more) nor stays home (caution 70
    or more, or aggression under 35): she joins joint raids and counter-attacks.
  - Her raid parties hold together a little longer: they fall back after losing 54% [50%].
  - Keeps 36% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 4 home defenders (3, plus 1 from her defence
    focus) and 3 home swordsmen [3, 3].
  - Soldier cap 13 [12]; ranged share 0.43 [0.40].
  - Trader hired at population 15 [14]; trader trips x0.92 apart [1]; amounts kept back from selling
    x1.29 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 10 [10]; recruitment interval x1.0 from her ambition, then x0.9 from her
    people focus [1]; food reserve x1.08 [1].
  - New house when free beds are 1 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 80.5% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 407 [384], before her warden_of_walls talent.
  - Miners want 10 gold and 5 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8].
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts in full [fd 1]; loot does not tempt her (greed 15);
    monster lairs (the Hollow's) are much more attractive targets [-500]. Mercy rule (honour 70 or
    more): she spares a faction that lost a base in the last in-game day.
  - A little random noise on every decision [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.05].
  - Righteous rule (a grudge against any conqueror; always joins coalitions): grudges need diplomacy
    (milestone 2), and in this milestone every surviving faction joins the coalition against a
    conqueror anyway (today's rule).
  - Next to a default righteous leader [aggression 60, caution 45] she is calmer and more careful.
    The slice's test run B has her as one of its four leaders (with a tsundere, an eccentric genius
    and a lone wolf); in run A she serves the Still Crane as a second hero.

PERSONALITY AND VOICE
- Temperament: righteous. Traits: aggression 50, honour 90, ambition 50, caution 60, greed 15,
  sociability 60, loyalty 85, volatility 25. Next to a typical righteous hero she is less aggressive
  (50 against 60), more careful (60 against 45), a little more loyal (85 against 80) and a touch
  less strict on honour (90 against 95).
- In a fight: steady (aggression between 35 and 65: she looks for enemies within 20 blocks and
  chases up to 56), so her attacking moves get no bonus [x1.0] and her zoning and defensive moves
  weigh a little more [x1.06]. She goes for the strongest enemy and protects her allies, and with
  honour 60 or more she never strikes a foe who runs: she lets a fleeing hero go. Her retreat point
  [retreat_below 22%] no longer makes her step back: she never leaves a fight, and 8% is where her
  last stand begins (see "When she is losing").
- Likes: shields, iron ingots, white tulips. Dislikes: wither roses. (Gifts come in a later
  milestone.)
- Quirk: "polishes her shield every night, whether it needs it or not."
- Voice: soft chime blips per syllable, a little lower than the usual female voice [pitch 1.15; the
  default is 1.25], measured [rate 3 ticks per syllable].
- Lines:
    Founding:  "By my oath, this is the Order of the Rime Vow. No one here will be left behind."
    Greeting:  "{player}. Are you well? Good. Stay that way."
    Intro:     "Lower your weapon and I will lower mine. I keep my word."
    Phase 2:   "Then I will stand against you properly."
    Phase 3:   "To me, Rime Vow! Hold the line!"
    Kill:      "It is done."
    Last stand: "My word is given. Not one step back."
    Fallen:    "Hold the line... hold..."
  Her phase 3 line names her own order even when she serves another faction (as in test run A);
  whether it changes there is set when she is built. Moments without a line of her own yet (her
  ultimate, enrage, stagger, raid announcements and others) use the righteous fallback lines until
  hers are written. (Her first sheet had a yield and a spared line; heroes no longer yield, so they
  are gone. She never escapes, so she needs no escape line.)

Benika Akagane - "The Iron Club Oni"
Female | oni | vanguard | fire | battle maniac | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: she fights the same against
players and NPCs, and she never yields: see "When she is losing"). All numbers are first values,
tuned after play.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
A laughing oni smith-warrior who wants one thing: an opponent worth breaking her club on. Her
settlement rings with hammers and glows with forge fire. In a one-on-one fight she is a grappler:
she grabs, throws and slams, so you stay out of her reach or punish a missed grab. Out of battle she
mines with a nose for ore; as a faction leader she is the Warlord of the Crimson Anvil.

LOOK
- Body: female, large (broad) build, drawn big [scale 108%]. In Rage she grows another 5%; her
  hitbox stops at 1.98 blocks so she still fits through 2-high doors, but the model is drawn at full
  size.
- Hair: a wild long black mane [#1B1717] with red tips [#C0392B].
- Oni parts: a single curved red horn on her forehead (it glows from phase 2); red stripe markings
  on her arms (glowing in phase 2).
- Outfit: an open crimson coat over chest wraps, a short black hakama with a gold rope belt, iron
  gauntlets, a gourd at her hip, wrapped feet in sandals.
- Weapon: an iron-studded club held in both hands and carried over her shoulder (in game terms, a
  mace).
- Colours: skin [#E8B89A], gold eyes [#F2C14E], primary crimson [#A3202A], secondary near-black
  [#1B1717], accent gold [#D4A23A], metal iron grey [#6D6E73], glow orange [#FF7A2A].
- Face: sharp eyes, a single lash [lashes 1], bold brows, a wide mouth with fangs on both sides. She
  blinks at a normal pace (every 2.5 to 6 s) [blink normal]. In Rage her face turns angry.
- Idle quirks: a shoulder roll, a swig from her gourd, the club resting on her shoulder.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 her horn and arm stripes glow; in phase 3 her eyes glow and steam rises
  from her horn.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of her attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, her face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where she stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3
or enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on her.
Her fighting: hammer (the club); duel style grappler. She closes to melee, protects her allies and
goes for the strongest enemy first (the battle maniac focus). Her element is fire: Fire Protection
reduces it, and Fire Resistance stops it completely (the vanilla fire rules); her basic club swings
are physical. Cooldowns and how often she picks each move are set when she is built.

  Basic attack
    A hammer string: overhead, side swing, slam, with her kit mace "Ironmaw" (Density I, the vanilla
    mace enchantment that adds damage for each block she falls before a hit). The damage of each
    swing comes from her attack power and the mace; its exact numbers are set when she is built. A
    raised shield blocks it like any hit from the front.

  Abilities
    OVERHEAD SLAM (overhead_slam) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she brings the club down on a narrow cone in front of her in a burst of fire
      that pops you up into the air.
      The tell: the club rises for a full second while a fan-shaped mark grows in front of her.
      After the hit the club is stuck in the ground for 1.2 s.
      Counter: step to the side (the cone is narrow) or back out of reach, then hit her while the
      club is stuck.
      Numbers: [area (a damaging cone is never lighter than area), close; cone 4, 40 degrees; 150%
      fire + knock-up 1 block; frames 1.0 (club rises) / 0.15 / 1.2 (club stuck)]

    SHOULDER CHARGE (shoulder_charge) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she drops her shoulder and runs straight through you, knocking you far back. It
      is her answer when you stand far away (60%) or start eating (70%).
      The tell: she sets herself for 0.9 s while a long strip on the ground shows her path.
      Counter: step off the strip, ideally with a wall behind you: a charge that ends against a wall
      staggers her (see Weak points). A charge stops at walls, drops and water, and she skips it
      when water or lava lies on the path.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; charge 10, speed 160%, wall stun; 90% + knockback 3; frames 0.9 / 0.6 /
      0.9]

    GROUND STOMP (ground_stomp) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she stomps and a ring of fire rolls outward along the ground, hurting you and
      setting you alight.
      The tell: a ring grows on the ground around her for 1 s.
      Counter: jump as the ring reaches you: it only hits what stands on the ground. Water puts the
      fire out.
      Numbers: [area, close and mid; wave, radius 8, speed 8 blocks a second, 1 wave; 6 fire +
      ignite 2 s; frames 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.8]

    GOURD SWIG (gourd_swig) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a long drink from her gourd that heals her and makes her hit harder for 8 s. She
      prefers it when she is under half health.
      The tell: the gourd comes up to her mouth (a light move: the animation only), and the drink
      takes 1.2 s before it works.
      Counter: it is a punish window: hit her while she drinks, then be careful for 8 s.
      Numbers: [light, any range; heal 6 + attack +15% for 8 s; prefers her own health under 50%;
      frames 1.2 / 0.1 / 0.6]

    GRAB AND THROW (grab_throw) - boss phases 2, 3
      What it does: she seizes you, holds you for a second and hurls you away in a burst of fire. It
      cannot be blocked. It is her answer to a raised shield (70%).
      The tell: she reaches out for 0.9 s over a purple mark (purple always means unblockable).
      Counter: get out of her reach during the wind-up. While held you can still turn and use items.
      A miss leaves her open for 1.2 s. A throw is softened when it would land you in lava, fire, a
      deep drop or the void.
      Numbers: [heavy, close; grab range 2.5, holds 1 s, throw 3, up 1; unblockable; 8 fire; frames
      0.9 / 1.0 / 1.2]

    METEOR CLUB (meteor_club) - boss phase 3 only (phase 3's new mechanic)
      What it does: she leaps high from up to 10 blocks away and lands club-first in a burst of
      fire, then always follows with a Ground Stomp.
      The tell: a landing disc grows on the ground where she will come down.
      Counter: get off the disc, then jump the stomp's ring. The stomp takes the place of the slam's
      recovery, so your opening comes after the stomp.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; leap 10, radius 3; 120% fire; followup Ground Stomp 100%; frames
      1.0 / 0.6 / 1.2]

    RAGE (state rage) - from boss phase 2
      At the phase 2 change she flies into a Rage: her horn glows, she grows, hits harder, is harder
      to push, and her face turns angry. A snowball hit ends it (see Weak points).
      Numbers: [attack +20%, knockback resistance +30, size 105%, mood angry. Whether and when she
      can enter Rage again after a snowball is set when she is built]

  Ultimate
    IRON MOUNTAIN CRASH (iron_mountain_crash)
      What it does: she raises the club high, shouts "Hold still, this'll be fun!", leaps and
      crashes down, and three rings of fire roll out from where she lands.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: the 1.8 s wind-up with the club raised, her line, a wide
      sound, a red ground mark, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the fight. She
      cannot be staggered during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when her ultimate
      is ready.
      Counter: leave the landing area, then jump each ring. Hit her in the 1.6 s recovery.
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.8 / 2.0 / 1.6; leap range 10 (the leap maximum), radius 4, then
      three fire waves "of 8". Set when she is built: whether "of 8" is the waves' reach or their
      damage, the crash's damage, the time between waves, her ultimate energy and charge rate, and
      the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    BATTLE JOY (battle_joy): every time she is hurt she gets a little stronger for 10 s, and it
    stacks [on_hurt: attack +5% for 10 s, up to 4 stacks]. In a fight all her damage bonuses
    together never exceed +25%.

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: she is a boss in every fight, whether she fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with her (it hurt her in the last 15 s, or is
  her target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. Her first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with her intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which she takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and her intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (her name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever she fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, her name and title, her phase pips and a lightning
  mark when her ultimate is ready. (Her bar title is set when she is built.)
- Health: her base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.0 for a
  vanguard, always: it never changes with who or how many fight her. At level 10 that is 144 health
  (165 as a leader): about 31 s (36 s) of landed hits for a player with an iron sword at 1.6 swings
  a second, half of them landing; an iron-sword soldier needs about a fifth longer (about 43 s for
  her as a leader), and attackers add up (six soldiers who all reach her need about 7 s of hitting
  between them). A whole duel lasts two to three times its hitting time once you dodge her wind-ups.
  No single hit, from anyone (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their arrows), takes more than
  12% of her health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no attacker
  (lava, fire, drowning) is not capped. In an NPC war she is worth about five iron soldiers to her
  faction's war council [war power 11.8 as a level-10 leader].
- Poise: 140 (the vanguard value). Every hit fills her poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x weapon:
  sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], and
  a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands)
  adds 25. A Shoulder Charge that ends against a wall adds 40, and her charge_wall weak point makes
  it a stagger at once. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full poise
  staggers her: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum
  grows 20% for the rest of the fight. No stagger during her intro, a phase change or her ultimate's
  wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where she faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Overhead Slam, Shoulder Charge, Ground Stomp, Gourd Swig.
    Phase 2, from 60%: Rage (the horn glows) and Grab and Throw (unblockable).
      "Now THIS is a fight!"
    Phase 3, from 25%: Meteor Club into a stomp; eyes glow, steam from the horn.
      "Don't you dare fall down yet!"
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (her exact length is set when she is built): she takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), she says the line, her
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Snowball: a snowball hit ends her Rage and cuts her damage by 10% for 5 s. Hint line: "Ugh,
    cold! Who throws snow at an oni?!" (How often it can work is set when she is built.)
  - Charge into a wall [charge_wall]: when her own Shoulder Charge ends against a wall, she
    staggers.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; their cooldowns are set when she is built).
  She reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You raise a shield -> Grab and Throw (70%). Grab and Throw is a phase 2-3 move; whether this
    answer can come in phase 1 is set when she is built.
  - You stand far away -> Shoulder Charge (60%).
  - You eat -> Shoulder Charge (70%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever she fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter
  waits between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All
  her damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. She also enrages at once when the heart of her base
  is attacked while she defends it. (The design gives 240 s for her as a leader; the 180 s otherwise
  is the design's default.)
- Intro: she plants the club and cracks her knuckles. "Oho! You look sturdy. Let's find out!"
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press her; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When she is losing: she never escapes [last stand: aggression 95 + honour 70 - caution 5 = 160,
  far over 65: a battle maniac wants the fight more than her life]. Under 8% of her health [where an
  escaping hero would go: a third of her retreat_below 6.5%, raised to the 8% floor] she has her
  last-stand beat once per fight (her last-stand line and a determined grin; nothing else changes:
  phase 3 and the enrage are her escalation) and keeps swinging until her enemies are dead or gone
  or she dies. "Hah... hah... this is GREAT! Don't you dare stop now!" When her raid falls back she
  stays in the fight she is in and only then walks home, so she often dies inside an enemy's walls.
  The same against you, soldiers, another hero or monsters.
  - At zero health she dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow. The
    bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "Good one... Next time I break the club on you." Ten minutes later
    she is back and wants a rematch.
  - A hero that runs from her: with honour 60 or more she lets it go and takes another target.
- Rewards: only when she dies, to whoever killed her (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt her in her last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear (her kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero she comes back 10 minutes after her death, whoever killed her
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at her home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); her kit items vanish at death and return with her. She has no death ability of her own. A
  hero of her faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring her back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Carry snowballs. From phase 2 she fights in Rage; one snowball ends it and cuts her damage by 10%
   for 5 s. Cobweb traps will not hold her, but snowballs work.
2. Drink a potion of Fire Resistance: her fire damage (Overhead Slam, Shoulder Charge, Ground Stomp,
   Grab and Throw, Meteor Club, the fire rings) then does nothing to you; only her basic club swings
   still hurt. Fire Protection armour softens it, and water puts out the burning.
3. Fight with a wall at your back and step off the strip of Shoulder Charge: if she runs into the
   wall she staggers, which means 2.5 s of hits at +25% damage.
4. Keep your shield down until the last moment. A shield held up gets you grabbed (70%, unblockable,
   phases 2 and 3); raising it just before a heavy hit lands is a parry (+25 poise). Do not eat in
   front of her either: she charges you (70%).
5. Jump the fire rings: Ground Stomp, the stomp after Meteor Club and the three rings of her
   ultimate only hit you on the ground.
6. Hit her when she is stuck: after Overhead Slam (club stuck 1.2 s), during Gourd Swig (1.2 s of
   drinking), after a missed grab (1.2 s) and after the stomp that ends Meteor Club. Hit hard rather
   than often (Battle Joy grows each time she is hurt): axes and maces fill her big poise bar (140)
   faster. Under a roof of 3 blocks or less she cannot leap (Meteor Club, her ultimate) or knock you
   up (Overhead Slam).
7. Expect no mercy and give none: she never runs. Under 8% she swings harder than ever (phase 3 and
   the enrage), so finish her from range or after a stagger rather than trading blows at the end.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: charger (ceiling III) and leaper (ceiling I). She has every feature from level 1;
the numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility
never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC
has is the one exception).
- From level 1: walking up full blocks without jumping [step 1.0]; the traversal charge (on a
  straight, clear stretch of path she breaks into a charging run; it stops at doors, fences, water
  and drops; travel only); cobwebs and sweet berry bushes never slow her, in fights too; harder to
  push back while travelling (off in every fight, against anyone); a longer safe fall; less slowing
  on soul sand, honey and other slow blocks. Leaper I: a 1.8-block jump (the leaper floor: field
  fences and 1.5-block ledges) and leaps across gaps instead of bridging them (no landing roll: that
  comes with stronger leapers).
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Traversal charge                        +36% every 20 s / +47% every 15 s / +60% every 12 s
    Knockback resistance while travelling   +0.18 / +0.24 / +0.30
    Safe fall (blocks)                      6.6  / 7.7  / 9.0
    Movement efficiency                     0.60 / 0.79 / 1.00
      (1.00 = soul sand, honey and other slow blocks do not slow her at all)
    Jump height (blocks)                    1.80 / 1.80 / 1.80
    Longest gap she leaps (blocks)          3    / 3    / 5
- Out of fights: she carries full loads up mine stairs without slowing, and leaps streams, narrow
  ravines and fences instead of detouring or bridging.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): Shoulder Charge and Meteor Club are unchanged.
  In every fight her knockback bonus and traversal charge switch off (her Rage gives her footing
  instead); her jump, step height, safe fall and web immunity stay. She uses her jump at once to
  reach an enemy on a fence or a 1.5-block ledge, a player or an NPC archer, and if she stands where
  no enemy can reach her she comes back within 4 s. If no jump reaches you (a tall pillar), she uses
  far-range moves, after 10 s digs and builds her way up at player speed, and after 30 s gives up
  with a taunt and resets. A jump starts with a 0.3 s crouch (the tell).
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader she always goes on her base's raids and is picked first [raids:
  leads, the vanguard default]; as a leader she leads them herself (below). Today's base walls are 5
  blocks high, beyond her jump (a jump-up needs her jump height minus 0.3: 1.5 blocks), so as a
  charger she leads her soldiers through the gate or the breach. Inside she takes one goal: she
  opens the way when her soldiers are held outside, hunts a defending hero she sees inside, and
  otherwise goes for the heart with her party; a goal ends after 60 s without progress.
- Escaping: her travel bonuses come back on (the charging run), and she may go wherever her legs and
  jumps take her, even where no pursuer can follow.
- Signature animation: none of her own in the design; she uses the shared charging run and skid, and
  the leaper's crouch, leap and heavy landing.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: miner (when she is not leading). As a faction leader her duty becomes court, but she still
  spends daylight at the mine and counts as a miner there.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): she breaks blocks faster [+15 / +25 / +35%], has a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a
  worker has 18], reaches farther [+0.5 / +1.0 / +1.5 blocks], wears out her pickaxe and gear less
  [-10 / -20 / -30%], gets less hungry at work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and earns more work XP [+10 / +20
  / +30%]. Blocks still take real time to break and ore comes from the vanilla loot tables; all her
  work-speed bonuses together never go above 2x a worker's with the same gear (Efficiency and Haste
  aside). Each level she gains rolls a miner affix, as for any NPC.
- Specialty, from her surveyor talent (when she mines herself): her pickaxe counts as having Fortune
  +1 / +2 / +3 on the ores she mines, for the loot only (the item never changes; at most Fortune III
  together with the pickaxe's own; never on a Silk Touch pickaxe), and she follows ore veins one
  block wider, from level 1. A drop never gets both her extra Fortune and an extra loot roll: it
  takes whichever gives more. Her charger footing lets her take loaded mine stairs without slowing.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is,
  inside the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never
  the sum; the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of her faction at tier I while
  she leads):
  - surveyor* (realm): her base's miners search for mine sites farther away [search radius +8 / +16
    / +24 blocks] and reach the diamond layer one level sooner with an iron pickaxe.
  - drill: her base's soldiers gain 1 / 2 / 3 work XP every in-game hour by day. Drill is a guard
    talent, and the design's Presence rule makes a hero's guard and court talents count one tier
    higher while it is at its base and not on a raid (tier IV: 4 XP an hour), with dawn sparring as
    drill's personal effect (2 / 3 / 4 soldiers gain 10 / 15 / 20 combat XP, non-lethal, stopping at
    half health). Her own design entry does not mention either, so whether they apply to a miner is
    set when she is built.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Warlord of the Crimson Anvil.
- Faction names: "Crimson Anvil Warband" (the first unused name is taken), then "The Red Horn Host".
  Short name: "Crimson Anvil". Motto: "Bring me someone worth breaking."
- Colours: primary crimson [#A3202A], secondary near-black [#1B1717], accent gold [#D4A23A]. They
  show in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, her sash and her NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: red, with a row of black triangles along the bottom and a yellow circle in the middle
  [base red; triangles_bottom black; circle yellow]. Real banners hang at the station and the gates,
  and members get a banner item. Her faction's wool, carpets and beds are red, with black as the
  second dye.
- Base style: ember_fortress, a Keep: brick walls, dark oak log frames, dark oak plank floors,
  deepslate tile roofs and polished blackstone bricks round the edge; signs read like "Crimson Anvil
  Keep". Culture: east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: military (no civilian role moves
  up; the soldier cap gets +1 instead) and mining (miners and smelters are hired ahead of other
  civilians).
- Her day (court duty): a short stop at the station at dawn; daylight at the mine, where she mines
  and counts as a miner; the heart at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by any alarm.
  She answers every alarm and guards the heart in a defence, says her founding line when the faction
  starts, and announces raids, attacks on her base, a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit
  milestones in her own voice (her own lines for these are not written yet; her temperament's
  fallback lines fill in).
- If she dies: everyone hears it, and her faction launches one vengeance raid against the killer's
  faction (aggression 70 or more); a raid she was on carries on without her [(caution + 1 - loyalty)
  / 2 = 0.25, not over 0.5]. She never escapes, so her faction never sees her run; when a raid she
  leads falls back she finishes her fight first. If her faction's last base falls she becomes an
  exiled free hero, never a leader again in that campaign.
- How her personality steers the faction. Her traits (out of 100): aggression 95, honour 70,
  ambition 70, caution 5, greed 25, sociability 55, loyalty 55, volatility 60. The numbers below are
  worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default personality
  strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids as often as the rules allow: about every 15 minutes, or 0.75 in-game days [18000 ticks,
    interval x0.5, the floor; neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid after 0.85 of an interval
    [1].
  - Attacks even at poor odds: attack odds needed 0.40, the floor [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.1
    of the interval, the floor [0.25], at odds 1.0, the floor [1.5].
  - She leads her raids herself (aggression 65 or more and caution under 60).
  - Her raid parties fight on until they have lost 78% [50%].
  - Keeps 15% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 2 home defenders and 2 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 19 [12]: 18 from her traits, +1 from her military focus. Ranged share 0.175 [0.40]:
    a mostly melee army.
  - Trader hired at population 15 [14]; trader trips x0.96 apart [1]; amounts kept back from selling
    x0.79 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 9 [10]; recruitment interval x0.88, so faster [1]; food reserve x0.6, the
    floor [1].
  - New house when free beds are 2 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 77.5% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 280 [384].
  - Miners want 12 gold and 6 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8].
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts only 17% [fd 0.17: a bold, ambitious leader seeks strong
    rivals], and her temperament targets the strongest rival she can beat; loot does not tempt her
    (greed under 50); monster lairs (the Hollow's) look less worth it [+700]. Mercy rule (honour 70
    or more): she spares a faction that lost a base in the last in-game day.
  - A fair amount of random noise on every decision [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.12].
  - Battle maniac rule: she accepts every duel (duels come in a later milestone).
  - The design's worked example for a default battle maniac has the same raid, odds, garrison,
    soldier and ranged numbers; her higher sociability moves the trader threshold from 16 to 15. The
    slice's test campaign expects a battle maniac to launch at least twice as many raids as a
    sweet-natured leader by day 6.

PERSONALITY AND VOICE

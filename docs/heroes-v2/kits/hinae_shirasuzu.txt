Hinae Shirasuzu - "The White Bell Shrine Maiden"
Female | shrine | support | radiant | sweet-natured | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: she fights the same against
players and NPCs, and she never yields: see "When she is losing"). All numbers are first values,
tuned after play.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
A gentle shrine keeper who would rather talk than fight, keeps lanterns lit for travellers, and
whose talismans never miss when she finally throws them. In a one-on-one fight she is a zoner: she
keeps you out with talismans and seals while she heals and shields her allies, and you win by
closing the gap. Out of battle she guards the shrine and tends the wounded; as a faction leader she
is the Keeper of the White Bell.

LOOK
- Body: female, standard build, drawn a little small [scale 96%].
- Hair: long straight black hair [#1C1A22] tied low with white paper, with straight bangs.
- Outfit: a white kimono top with plain sleeves, a pleated red hakama, split-toe socks and clogs,
  talisman strips at her belt.
- Weapon: a purification wand with paper streamers and a cluster of bells in her right hand; a fan
  of talismans in her left.
- Colours: skin [#F7DFD0], deep red eyes [#B4413A], primary white [#F6F3EE], secondary red
  [#C8323C], accent gold [#D9B44A], trim cream [#EADFC8], glow pale gold [#FFE9A3].
- Face: round eyes, light lashes [lashes 2], worried brows, a soft mouth and a blush. She blinks at
  a normal pace (every 2.5 to 6 s) [blink normal].
- Idle quirks: a bell shake, sweeping, hands together and a small bow.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 seven glowing talismans orbit her; in phase 3 her eyes glow gold and her
  paper streamers glow.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of her attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, her face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where she stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3
or enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on her.
Her fighting: focus (the wand and talismans, thrown); duel style zoner. She keeps about 8 blocks
away [keep_distance 8, the support default] and backs off like an archer when you come within 4; she
protects her allies and goes for whoever threatens them first (the support focus). Her element is
radiant (undead take 30% more from it; players take it normally). Cooldowns and how often she picks
each move are set when she is built.

  Basic attack
    A thrown talisman: it flies straight like a feather, trailing white sparkles, out to 14 blocks,
    for 4 radiant damage every 1.5 s.

  Abilities
    TALISMAN FAN (talisman_fan) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she throws a spread of five talismans; a talisman that hits you strikes with
      holy light and puts a seal mark on you. Seals stack.
      The tell: a quick throw (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: move sideways out of the spread, put a block between you, or block it (light moves
      are blockable like any hit). Count your seals: with 3 or more, her Sealing Circle stuns you.
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; volley of 5 (10 degrees apart), range 16; 4 radiant + mark seal;
      frames 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.4. How long a seal lasts and how many can stack are set when she is
      built]

    SEALING CIRCLE (sealing_circle) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a circle of light marks the ground where you stand and bursts once a moment
      later, hurting and slowing you. If you carry 3 or more seals, the burst also stuns you for 1 s
      and wipes the seals. The stun is the same for everyone: a stunned player cannot walk, jump,
      attack or use items for that second (the camera still turns), as a stunned soldier cannot, and
      nobody can be stunned again for twice as long. It is her answer to a raised shield (50%).
      The tell: she rings her bells for 1 s while a disc grows under you.
      Counter: walk out of the circle before it bursts. A shield does not help: circles ignore
      shields.
      Numbers: [area, mid; zone at the target, radius 3, delay 1.0 s (whether the delay runs inside
      the wind-up or after it is set when she is built), one burst; 6 radiant + Slowness II 3 s;
      with 3 or more seals: stun 1 s and the seals clear; frames 1.0 (bell ring) / 0.1 / 0.8]

    PURIFY (purify) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she cleanses and heals every ally within 8 blocks. Cleansing removes harmful
      potion effects, damage over time, marks, fire, freezing and stuns. She uses it when an ally is
      under 60% health, and it is her answer to an allied hero under half health (80%).
      The tell: a short cast (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: fight her away from her allies, or hit her during the 0.6 s cast.
      Numbers: [light, any range; allies within 8: cleanse + heal 4; frames 0.6 / 0.1 / 0.4]

    EXORCISE (exorcise) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a burst of light all around her that hurts you, strips your helpful potion
      effects (golden-apple absorption included) and pushes you away. She prefers it when you are
      close, and it is her answer to a close target (70%).
      The tell: a disc grows on the ground around her for 1 s.
      Counter: step out of its 4-block reach during the wind-up, and do not drink potions or eat
      golden apples right next to her.
      Numbers: [area, close; nova, radius 4; 3 radiant + dispel + knockback 2; frames 1.0 / 0.1 /
      0.8]

    SEVEN TALISMANS (seven_talismans) - boss phases 2, 3 (phase 2's new mechanic)
      What it does: seven talismans ring her and block all damage until 7 hits have landed (each hit
      pops one talisman) or 12 s have passed.
      The tell: she sets herself for 0.8 s, with a sound; the shape of its ground mark is set when
      she is built.
      Counter: every hit counts, however weak: land many quick hits, bring a friend, or wait it out.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; on herself; barrier of 7 hits for 12 s; frames 0.8 / 0.2 / 0.8]

    PURIFYING DANCE (purifying_dance) - boss phase 3 only (phase 3's new mechanic)
      What it does: she dances, sweeping a beam of light across the half-circle in front of her for
      2 s; it hits you as it passes, and her allies get Regeneration I for 4 s. At the end she
      kneels to pray for 1.5 s.
      The tell: a ground mark grows in front of her for 1 s (the design gives no mark shape for
      sweeps: set when she is built).
      Counter: get behind her (the sweep covers only the half-circle in front), stay more than 8
      blocks away, or put a block between you (the beam stops at blocks). Then hit her while she
      kneels.
      Numbers: [area, close and mid; sweep 8, 180 degrees, 2 s; 5 radiant per pass; allies
      Regeneration I 4 s; frames 1.0 / 2.0 / 1.5 (kneels to pray)]

  Ultimate
    THOUSAND BELL RITE (thousand_bell_rite)
      What it does: she rings a rite of bells. Every ally within 10 blocks heals and gains a shield
      of golden hearts; every enemy within 6 blocks is struck by holy light and slowed. It is a
      channel: enough damage breaks it.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: the 1.6 s wind-up, a spoken line, a wide sound, a red ground
      mark, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the fight. She cannot be staggered
      during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when her ultimate is ready.
      Counter: be more than 6 blocks away when it goes off, or break it: deal 15% of her maximum
      health while she channels and she is left open for 2 s. One hit can never do it alone (a hit
      is capped at 12%), so it takes two hits or more.
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.6 / 0.3 / 1.5; channelled, breaks after 15% of her max health in
      damage; allies within 10: heal 10 + shield 8; enemies within 6: 10 radiant + Slowness II 4 s.
      Set when she is built: her ultimate line, how long the channel lasts, her ultimate energy and
      charge rate, and the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    GUARDIAN CHARM (guardian_charm): when an ally within 12 blocks drops below 35% health, she wraps
    a shield of 4 (2 golden hearts) around that ally; at most once every 15 s (and once per ally
    every 20 s).

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: she is a boss in every fight, whether she fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with her (it hurt her in the last 15 s, or is
  her target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. Her first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with her intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which she takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and her intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (her name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever she fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, her name and title, her phase pips and a lightning
  mark when her ultimate is ready. (Her bar title is set when she is built.)
- Health: her base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.25 for a
  support, always: it never changes with who or how many fight her. At level 10 that is 130 health
  (148 as a leader): about 27 s (31 s) of landed hits for a player with an iron sword at 1.6 swings
  a second, half of them landing; an iron-sword soldier needs about a fifth longer, and attackers
  add up (six soldiers who all reach her need about a sixth of one soldier's time). A whole duel
  lasts two to three times its hitting time once you dodge her wind-ups. No single hit, from anyone
  (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their arrows), takes more than 12% of her health
  [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no attacker (lava, fire,
  drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 80 (the support value). Every hit fills her poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x weapon:
  sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], and
  a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands)
  adds 25. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full poise staggers her: 2.5
  s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum grows 20% for the
  rest of the fight. No stagger during her intro, a phase change or her ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too, so no move reaches you sooner than that wait plus its
  wind-up (at least 1.9 s after the fight starts, or after the intro).
  During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where she faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Talisman Fan, Sealing Circle, Purify, Exorcise.
    Phase 2, from 60%: adds Seven Talismans (a barrier you must break with hits); the talisman ring.
      "Please... stop. I don't want to hurt you."
    Phase 3, from 25%: adds Purifying Dance; her eyes and streamers glow.
      "Then I'll purify this fight myself!"
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (her exact length is set when she is built): she takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), she says the line, her
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Channel: her Thousand Bell Rite (and the prayer after Purifying Dance) breaks after damage of
    15% of her maximum health and leaves her open for 2 s. (The dance has no channel of its own in
    the design: what breaking its prayer does is set when she is built.)
  - Back: the bonus for hitting her from behind (more than 120 degrees from where she faces) doubles
    [during a recovery, +30% more instead of +15%].
  - The line or prop that hints at each weak point is set when she is built.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; their cooldowns are set when she is built).
  She reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You come close -> Exorcise (70%).
  - An allied hero falls under half health -> Purify (80%).
  - You raise a shield -> Sealing Circle (50%; circles ignore shields).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever she fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter
  waits between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All
  her damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. She also enrages at once when the heart of her base
  is attacked while she defends it. (The design gives 240 s for her as a leader; the 180 s otherwise
  is the design's default.)
- Intro: she bows and raises her wand. "Welcome, traveller. Will you put your weapon down? ...No?
  I'm sorry, then."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press her; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When she is losing: away from home she escapes [her sweet-natured traits: aggression 30 + honour
  75 - caution 60 = 45, under 65: she never wanted the fight]. Under 15% of her health [flee_at 15%:
  a third of her retreat_below 44%, kept inside her last phase], in any fight, whoever she is
  fighting, she breaks off for good. Her raid falling back while she is in or near the target sends
  her off too. Escaping, she starts no moves and runs on foot, across water where she can (an escape
  counts as travel, so her runner speed is back on, and she swims where soldiers wade). She stays a
  boss while she runs: the 12% cap, her poise and her weak points still hold, so a stagger stops her
  for 2.5 s where she is, and she has three or four sword blows of health left. Cornered with no way
  out for 3 s, she fights back, then runs again as soon as a way opens. Her refuge is her home base;
  the escape ends inside its walls or once no pursuer has been within 48 blocks for 10 s. Then she
  stays away 5 minutes and until she is above 50% health, and does not go back for those she fled.
  The boss bar shows "fled" for 3 s and goes. "I'm sorry... I can't stay. Please, let that be
  enough."
  - Never from her own base: at her home base, while its heart stands, she does not leave her people
    [stands_for: home, from loyalty 80]. There, under 15% she has her last-stand beat (her
    last-stand line, the only hard thing she says, and a determined face, once per fight) and fights
    until she dies. "I won't leave them. Not while one lantern is still lit." If the heart falls,
    she runs to another base of her faction.
  - At most one escape in 10 minutes: caught again inside that window, she fights to the death.
  - At zero health she dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow. The
    bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "The bell... someone, please, keep it ringing..."
  - A hero that runs from her: with honour 60 or more she lets it go and takes another target.
- Rewards: only when she dies, to whoever killed her (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt her in her last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear (her kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero she comes back 10 minutes after her death, whoever killed her
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at her home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); her kit items vanish at death and return with her. She has no death ability of her own. A
  hero of her faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring her back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Count your seals. Each talisman of her Talisman Fan seals you; with 3 or more, her next Sealing
   Circle stuns you for 1 s. Walk out of the circle while she rings her bells; a shield will not
   stop it.
2. Do not hug her, and do not buff up next to her. Close in she answers with Exorcise (70%), which
   knocks you back and strips your potion effects and golden-apple hearts. Step in, hit, and step
   out of its 4-block disc when it grows.
3. Break her rite. While she channels Thousand Bell Rite, land 15% of her maximum health (two hits
   or more) to break it and get 2 s of free hits, or simply stand more than 6 blocks away.
4. Get behind her. Her back is a weak point, and her Purifying Dance only sweeps the half-circle in
   front of her (blocks stop the beam too). Hit her while she kneels to pray after it.
5. Pop the Seven Talismans with many quick hits: each hit breaks one, however weak; 7 hits or 12 s
   end the barrier.
6. Fight her away from her allies, or take them down first. She heals and cleanses them (Purify 80%
   when an allied hero is under half health) and shields any ally under 35% health. Her poise is
   small (80): an axe (x1.6) or a mace (x2.0) staggers her quickly.
7. Know where you fight her. Away from home she breaks off at 15% and runs, swimming where she can:
   a stagger or a few arrows stop her. In her own base she never runs and fights to the end, with
   her guards and her shields around her.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: runner (ceiling II) and swimmer (ceiling I). She has every feature from level 1;
the numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility
never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC
has is the one exception).
- From level 1: walking up full blocks without jumping [step 1.0]; faster, more efficient travel
  that costs less hunger (travel only, never in fights); less slowing on soul sand, honey and other
  slow blocks. Swimmer I: she swims faster, moves better in water and holds her breath longer (the
  waterfall purification rite) [each air loss is skipped with a chance of bonus / (bonus + 1), the
  Respiration rule]. Otherwise she swims like a worker: diving on purpose belongs to stronger
  swimmers. Her jump is a worker's.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Travel speed                            +9%   / +12%  / +15%
    Movement efficiency                     0.30  / 0.39  / 0.50
    Travel hunger                           -21%  / -28%  / -35%
    Swim speed                              x1.30 / x1.39 / x1.50
    Water movement efficiency               0.20  / 0.26  / 0.33
    Oxygen bonus                            1.2   / 1.6   / 2.0
- Out of fights: she swims across rivers and lakes instead of bridging or walking round, and as a
  guard she runs the shrine steps to any alarm.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): her travel bonuses are off; she keeps her zoning
  and fights in water unslowed (players and soldiers are slowed there). If you stand where she
  cannot walk to you (a pillar, a pen, a ledge; an NPC archer on a tower the same), she uses her
  far-range moves, after 10 s digs and builds her way up at player speed, and after 30 s gives up
  with a taunt and resets.
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader she joins her base's raids like a soldier [raids: joins, the
  support default]; as a leader she stays home (below). She neither climbs nor jumps walls: as a
  runner and swimmer she goes in through the gate or the breach at the head of the soldiers. Inside,
  the design's goals that fit her are opening the way when her soldiers are held outside, hunting a
  defending hero she sees inside, and clearing the defending archers (she is a zoner), otherwise the
  heart with her party; which comes first is set when she is built. A goal ends after 60 s without
  progress. Defending, as a guard hero she joins the two nearest free guards against an enemy hero
  coming over her walls.
- Escaping (away from home, under 15%: see "When she is losing"): her travel speed comes back on,
  and she may go wherever she can walk or swim, even where no pursuer can follow.
- Signature animation: none of her own in the design; she uses the shared run and swim.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: guard (when she is not leading). She patrols with the guards and answers alarms.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): as a guard she answers alarms from farther away instead of working faster [+8 / +12 / +16
  blocks]: she runs the shrine steps to any alarm. She also has a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27
  slots; a worker has 18], less wear on her gear [-10 / -20 / -30%], less hunger from work [-10 /
  -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 / +30%]. Each level she gains rolls a guard affix, as for
  any NPC.
- Presence (her specialty as a guard): mend and purifier count one tier higher while she is at her
  base (inside the walls or within 16 blocks) and not on a raid; at tier III they use the tier IV
  numbers. Her personal touch: she heals the most hurt ally within 16 blocks by hand (an NPC, a hero
  or a member player alike) [4 / 6 / 8 health every 20 s; a 2 s channel that damage breaks], and her
  purifying also lifts Slowness, Weakness and Mining Fatigue from allies within 8 blocks of her,
  NPCs, heroes and member players alike [once every 60 / 45 / 30 s].
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is,
  inside the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never
  the sum; the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of her faction at tier I while
  she leads):
  - mend: out of combat, allies within 16 blocks heal 5 / 7 / 10% of their maximum health every 10 s
    [with Presence at home: 7 / 10 / 12%].
  - purifier* (realm): allies at her base (NPCs, heroes and member players alike) lose poison,
    wither and hunger effects every 30 / 20 / 10 s [with Presence at home: every 20 / 10 / 5 s].
  At home she also uses the shared praying and sweeping idles of shrine keepers.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Keeper of the White Bell.
- Faction names: "Hearth of the White Bell" (the first unused name is taken), then "White Bell
  Sanctum". Short name: "White Bell". Motto: "Every lantern lit, every door open."
- Colours: primary white [#F6F3EE], secondary red [#C8323C], accent gold [#D9B44A]. They show in
  chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, her sash and her NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: white, with a red plus-shaped cross and a yellow stripe down the middle [base white;
  straight_cross red; stripe_center yellow] (the shrine emblem). Real banners hang at the station
  and the gates, and members get a banner item. Her faction's wool, carpets and beds are white, with
  red as the second dye.
- Base style: shrine_pavilion, a Shrine: birch plank walls, stripped mangrove log frames, spruce
  plank floors, deepslate tile roofs and stone bricks round the edge; signs read like "White Bell
  Shrine". Culture: east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: food (chefs, fishermen and
  farmers are hired ahead of other civilians) and people (chefs and haulers ahead, and the
  recruitment interval x0.9).
- Her day (court duty): a short stop at the station at dawn; by day a walk between the station, the
  gates and the heart, greeting member players (her first talent, mend, has no workplace); the heart
  at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by any alarm. She answers every alarm and
  guards the heart in a defence, says her founding line when the faction starts, and announces
  raids, attacks on her base, a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit milestones in her own voice
  (her own lines for these are not written yet; her temperament's fallback lines fill in).
- If she dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until she returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more). She stays home from raids, so no raid waits on her, and at her capital she
  never runs: a leader of hers dies at the heart rather than leave it. If her faction's last base
  falls she becomes an exiled free hero, never a leader again in that campaign.
- How her personality steers the faction. Her traits (out of 100): aggression 30, honour 75,
  ambition 40, caution 60, greed 25, sociability 90, loyalty 80, volatility 35. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids rarely: about every 37 minutes, or 1.86 in-game days [44640 ticks, interval x1.24; neutral
    36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid not before 1.21 intervals [1].
  - Attacks only with a clear edge: attack odds needed 0.807 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.325 of
    the interval [0.25], at odds 1.70 [1.5].
  - She stays home from raids (aggression under 35).
  - A raid party falls back after losing 47% [50%].
  - Keeps 38% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 3 home defenders and 3 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 10 [12]; ranged share 0.47 [0.40]: a smaller army with more archers.
  - Trader hired early, at population 12 [14] (the formula lands on 11.5; how a half is rounded is
    set when she is built); trader trips x0.68 apart, so much more often [1]; amounts kept back from
    selling x1.23 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 11 [10]; recruitment interval x1.06 from her ambition, then x0.9 from her
    people focus [1]; food reserve x1.12 [1].
  - New house when free beds are 1 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 77.5% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 407 [384].
  - Miners want 12 gold and 6 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8].
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts in full [fd 1]; loot does not tempt her (greed under 50);
    monster lairs (the Hollow's) are much more attractive targets [-550]. Mercy rule (honour 70 or
    more): she spares a faction that lost a base in the last in-game day.
  - A little random noise on every decision [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.07].
  - Sweet-natured rule (milestone 2, when diplomacy arrives): she offers peace to every faction at
    first contact.
  - The design's worked example for a default sweet-natured leader raids every 45000 ticks, needs
    odds of 0.767, keeps 36% home, has a soldier cap of 10, a ranged share of 0.45 and a trader at
    12; her extra caution (60 against 50) and aggression (30 against 25) give the numbers above. The
    slice's test campaign expects a battle maniac to launch at least twice as many raids as she does
    by day 6.

PERSONALITY AND VOICE
- Temperament: sweet-natured. Traits: aggression 30, honour 75, ambition 40, caution 60, greed 25,
  sociability 90, loyalty 80, volatility 35. Next to a typical sweet-natured hero she is a little
  more aggressive (30 against 25) and more careful (60 against 50).

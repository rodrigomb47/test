Tobimaru Usugumo - "The Quiet Ash Ninja"
Male | ninja | skirmisher | blight | lone wolf | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: he fights the same against players
and NPCs, and he never yields: see "When he is losing"). All numbers are first values, tuned after
play.
====================================================================================================

WHO HE IS
A silent scout who maps every valley alone and trusts no footprints but his own. His long red scarf
is the only colour he allows himself. In a one-on-one fight he is a rushdown fighter: he never stops
coming with vanishing steps, stabs from behind and dashes, and you punish him after a finisher that
misses. Out of battle he scouts; as a faction leader he is the Head of the Quiet Ash.

LOOK
- Body: male, slim build, drawn a little small [scale 98%].
- Hair: short dark navy hair [#1C2233] in a high ponytail.
- Outfit: a navy cloth half mask over the lower half of his face (lowered in phase 3, which shows
  his mouth for the first time); a long red scarf that trails behind him and swings as he moves; a
  navy wrap top, fitted wrapped trousers, arm guards and split-toe socks; a kunai holster on his
  thigh.
- Weapon: a short blade held in reverse grip in his right hand and a kunai in his left (in game
  terms, an iron sword; the kunai he throws look like flint).
- Colours: skin [#EFD2BE], grey-blue eyes [#9AA4B8], primary navy [#1E2740], secondary scarf red
  [#B3262E], accent ash grey [#4A4E57], metal steel [#AEB4BE].
- Face: narrow eyes with no lashes [lashes 0], straight brows; his mouth is hidden by the mask until
  phase 3. He blinks slowly and calmly, every 4 to 7 s [blink calm].
- Idle quirks: adjusting his scarf, a crouching scan of the surroundings, twirling a kunai.
- Phase looks: in phase 3 he pulls the mask down.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of his attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: his accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, his face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where he stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on him.
His fighting: twin blades; duel style rushdown. As a skirmisher he hits and runs: after each melee
hit he steps back 3 blocks until his next swing is ready [keep_distance 4], and he goes for the
weakest enemy first (the skirmisher default). His element is blight (the wither and wither skeletons
ignore it; you do not). His element resistances, gear, move cooldowns and how often he picks each
move are set when he is built.

  Basic attack
    A twin-blade string of quick slashes. Every fourth hit makes you bleed blight [1 damage per
    second for 3 s]. A raised shield blocks the slashes like any hit from the front.

  Abilities
    KUNAI FAN (kunai_fan) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: he throws three kunai in a fan at targets up to 14 blocks away. A kunai that
      hits poisons you with blight. It is his answer when you stand far away (60%).
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: move sideways out of the fan, put a block between you, or block it (light moves are
      blockable like any hit).
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; volley of 3 (10 degrees apart), range 14; blight damage over
      time 1.5 per second for 4 s; frames 0.4 / 0.2 / 0.4. Whether a kunai also deals impact damage,
      and whether two kunai stack their poison, is set when he is built]

    SHADOW STEP (shadow_step) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: he vanishes in a puff of smoke, turns invisible and fast for 3 s, then always
      strikes with Backstab. It is his answer to a raised shield (60%) and to you eating (70%).
      The tell: a smoke puff during the 0.8 s wind-up. While he is invisible, small puffs at his
      feet give him away as he moves.
      Counter: watch the puffs, turn to face them, and punish the Backstab's recovery.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; on himself; Invisibility 3 s + Speed I 3 s; followup Backstab
      100%; frames 0.8 (smoke puff) / 0.1 / 0.8 (skipped when Backstab follows, so the punish window
      comes after the Backstab); the shape of its ground mark is set when he is built]

    BACKSTAB (backstab) - boss phases 1, 2, 3, only after Shadow Step
      What it does: a quick stab, 60% stronger when he is behind you. He never picks it on its own:
      it only follows Shadow Step.
      The tell: the animation only (a light move), and the footstep puffs of the Shadow Step before
      it.
      Counter: keep him in front of you (face the puffs) and block it; then hit him in its 0.5 s
      recovery.
      Numbers: [light, close; 120%, +60% more from behind (whether added to or multiplied with the
      120% is set when he is built); weight 0 (a followup only); frames 0.4 / 0.1 / 0.5]

    SMOKE BOMB (smoke_bomb) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a burst of smoke around him: everyone within 4 blocks gets Darkness (on a
      soldier or a hero it cuts sight and aim to 5 blocks, about what you see) and is slowed, while
      he hops 5 blocks back. It deals no damage. He prefers it when you are close.
      The tell: a disc grows on the ground around him during the 1.0 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: step out of the 4-block disc during the wind-up.
      Numbers: [area, close; nova, radius 4; Darkness 3 s (everyone), Slowness I 3 s; hop back 5;
      frames 1.0 / 0.1 / 0.8]

    SHADOW CLONES (shadow_clones) - boss phases 2, 3 (new in phase 2)
      What it does: two decoys of him appear and last 10 s. A decoy looks exactly like him, copies
      what he does a tenth of a second late [2 ticks], walks a short path, has 1 health and pops in
      a puff of smoke at any hit. The design gives decoys no attack of their own. Soldiers and
      heroes are fooled as you are: they swing and shoot at a decoy as at him.
      The tell: a 0.8 s wind-up with a sound; the shape of its ground mark is set when he is built.
      Counter: find the real one: only he casts a shadow and only he makes footstep sounds. One hit
      pops a decoy.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; on himself, 2 decoys for 10 s (at most 3 decoys per hero); frames
      0.8 / 0.2 / 0.8]

    LOG SWAP (log_swap) - boss phases 2, 3 (new in phase 2)
      What it does: he leaves a decoy where he stood and hops 4 blocks back. He prefers it when you
      are close.
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: do not chase the figure that stays put: it is the decoy; the real one is 4 blocks
      back.
      Numbers: [light, close; on himself, 1 decoy + hop back 4; frames 0.4 / 0.1 / 0.3]

    SHADOW DASH (shadow_dash) - boss phase 3 only (new in phase 3)
      What it does: a 7-block dash along the ground that cuts everyone within 1.5 blocks of his path
      with blight. Each time a dash hits, he dashes again at once, up to four dashes in a row. After
      the last one (or the first one that misses) he kneels for 1.5 s to reset.
      The tell: a strip grows on the ground along the dash during the 0.8 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: dodge one dash to end the string, then hit him while he kneels (his recovery weak
      point doubles your bonus). A dash stops at walls and at drops over 4 blocks, and he skips it
      when water or lava lies on the path.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; dash 7; 70% blight; repeatable, follows itself 100% (a string of 4;
      whether each chained dash replays its wind-up and strip is set when he is built); frames 0.8 /
      0.3 / 1.5 (kneels to reset)]

  Ultimate
    NIGHTFALL (nightfall)
      What it does: a column of smoke rises around him; everyone within 10 blocks gets Darkness for
      4 s (soldiers and heroes see and aim no farther than 5 blocks); he vanishes for 2 s, then
      strikes his target with a combo of 4 blight strikes.
      The tell: the biggest in his kit: a 1.5 s wind-up with the smoke column, a spoken line, a wide
      sound, a red ground mark, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the fight. He
      cannot be staggered during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when his ultimate
      is ready.
      Counter: keep moving and keep a shield ready when he reappears; each strike of the combo can
      be dodged on its own. Hit him in the 1.5 s recovery.
      Numbers: [ultimate; radius 10; Darkness 4 s (everyone); vanish 2 s; 4 strikes x 60% blight;
      frames 1.5 / 1.6 / 1.5. Set when he is built: his ultimate line, the shape of its ground mark,
      how fast the energy bar fills and the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    UNSEEN (unseen): whenever he kills an enemy he turns invisible for 2 s and gets Speed I
    [on_kill; how long the Speed lasts is set when he is built].

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: he is a boss in every fight, whether he fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with him (it hurt him in the last 15 s, or is
  his target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. His first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with his intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which he takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and his intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (his name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever he fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, his name and title, his phase pips and a lightning
  mark when his ultimate is ready. (His bar title is set when he is built.)
- Health: his base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.25 for a
  skirmisher, always: it never changes with who or how many fight him. At level 10 that is 130
  health (148 as a leader): about 27 s (31 s) of landed hits for a player with an iron sword at 1.6
  swings a second, half of them landing; an iron-sword soldier needs about a fifth longer, and
  attackers add up (six soldiers who all reach him need about a sixth of one soldier's time). A
  whole duel lasts two to three times its hitting time once you dodge his wind-ups. No single hit,
  from anyone (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their arrows), takes more than 12% of his
  health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no attacker (lava, fire,
  drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 80 (the skirmisher value). Every hit fills his poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x
  weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt
  0.8], and a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move
  lands) adds 25. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full poise staggers
  him: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum grows 20%
  for the rest of the fight. No stagger during his intro, a phase change or his ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves he waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too, so no move reaches you sooner than that wait plus its
  wind-up (at least 1.9 s after the fight starts, or after the intro).
  During any recovery he takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where he faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Kunai Fan, Shadow Step (with Backstab), Smoke Bomb.
    Phase 2, from 60%: adds Shadow Clones and Log Swap (decoys); a hand seal and smoke.
      "...You see me? Then see three."
    Phase 3, from 25%: he pulls his mask down; adds the Shadow Dash string.
      "No more hiding."
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (his exact length is set when he is built): he takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; he is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), he says the line, his
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Light: a torch, a lantern, glowstone or any block that gives light of 12 or more, placed by an
    enemy within 4 blocks of him (a player or an NPC: the same), weakens him by 10% for 10 s. Hint
    line: "...Too bright." (Whether the 10% is less damage dealt or more damage taken, and how often
    it can trigger, is set when he is built.)
  - Recovery: the punish bonus doubles during his recoveries [+30% instead of +15%]; the kneel after
    the Shadow Dash string is the clearest one.
- How he reads you (each answer replaces his next pick; their cooldowns are set when he is built).
  He reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You stand far away -> Kunai Fan (60%).
  - You raise a shield -> Shadow Step (60%), then Backstab, which goes for your back.
  - You eat -> Shadow Step (70%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever he fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter waits
  between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All his
  damage bonuses together never exceed +25%.
- Intro: he drops from above into a crouch. "Leave. This is the only warning."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press him; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When he is losing: he escapes [his lone-wolf traits: aggression 55 + honour 50 - caution 60 = 45,
  under 65: a lone wolf owes nobody his death]. Under 11% of his health [flee_at 11%: a third of his
  retreat_below 31.5%, kept inside his last phase], in any fight, from a player, soldiers, another
  hero or monsters alike, he breaks off for good. His raid falling back while he is in or near the
  target, or the heart of the base he defends falling, sends him off too. Nothing holds him anywhere
  [stands_for: none, loyalty 55]: not a raid, not his own base. Escaping, he starts no moves and is
  the slice's hardest hero to catch: up a 16-block face, over a ravine on two air jumps, with no
  fall damage (an escape counts as travel). He stays a boss while he runs: the 12% cap, his poise
  and his weak points still hold, so a stagger stops him for 2.5 s where he is (and a hit knocks him
  off a wall), and he has two or three sword blows of health left. Cornered with no way out for 3 s,
  he fights back, then runs again as soon as a way opens. His refuge is his home base, never the
  base he flees (from a fight at his own base: another base of his faction, or the wild until no one
  follows); the escape ends inside its walls or once no pursuer has been within 48 blocks for 10 s.
  Then he stays away 5 minutes and until he is above 50% health, and does not go back for those he
  fled. The boss bar shows "fled" for 3 s and goes. "...Another night."
  - At most one escape in 10 minutes: caught again inside that window, he fights to the death.
  - At zero health he dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow. The
    bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "...One footprint too many."
  - Not an escape: on a scouting round he steps aside the same way when he sees enemy soldiers, is
    badly hurt among monsters (under 40%) or meets a monster in the way (no stay-away, no window
    spent, no line): he goes out of sight and on with his round, or home when he retreats. Once an
    enemy engages him it is a fight like any other.
- Rewards: only when he dies, to whoever killed him (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt him in his last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x his level, at most 100), his non-kit gear (his kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero he comes back 10 minutes after his death, whoever killed him
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at his home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); his kit items vanish at death and return with him. He has no death ability of his own. A
  hero of his faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring him back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HIM
1. Carry torches. Placing a torch, a lantern or glowstone within 4 blocks of him weakens him by 10%
   for 10 s ("...Too bright."). It is the placing that counts, so place a new one near him when the
   10 s run out.
2. Track the smoke. When he vanishes with Shadow Step, a Backstab always follows, and it hits 60%
   harder from behind. Watch the puffs at his feet, turn to face them, and punish the 0.5 s after
   the stab. Eating in front of him draws Shadow Step 70% of the time, a raised shield 60%.
3. Find the real one. From phase 2, only the real Tobimaru casts a shadow and makes footstep sounds;
   the decoys copy him a tenth of a second late and pop at any hit (1 health). Keep entity shadows
   switched on in your video settings.
4. Dodge one dash to end the string. In phase 3 every Shadow Dash that hits chains into another (up
   to 4); a miss ends it and he kneels for 1.5 s, where his recovery weak point doubles your punish
   bonus. Water or lava on the dash path makes him skip the dash.
5. His poise is small (80): an axe (x1.6) or a mace (x2.0) staggers him quickly, and a stagger gives
   you 2.5 s at +25% damage.
6. A pillar will not save you. He climbs 9 blocks or more from level 1, may hit you from a cling
   just under your feet, and can break the block under you at player speed. Any hit, or a knockback
   of 0.5 or more, knocks him off the wall.
7. Stop him before 11%. Once he breaks off he climbs and jumps where you cannot follow; save a
   stagger (an axe or a mace for his small poise) or a bow for the moment he turns, and hit him off
   the wall if he starts to climb. Caught again within 10 minutes, he will not run a second time.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: acrobat (ceiling III) and climber (ceiling II). He has every feature from level 1;
the numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility
never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and he never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC
has is the one exception).
- From level 1: two extra jumps in the air, wall kicks (a jump off a wall he touches), chimneys (up
  a 1-2 block wide shaft by kicking from wall to wall), the mantle (he pulls himself onto a ledge
  within a block of his hands at the top of a jump), walking up full blocks without jumping [step
  1.0], a landing roll (no slowdown on landing) and no fall damage at all. He climbs walls at ladder
  speed (2.35 blocks a second up, at most 3 down), clings still to a wall, and can work while
  clinging (a player on a ladder breaks blocks five times slower; he does not).
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Jump height (blocks)                    1.40 / 1.45 / 1.50
    Each air jump (blocks)                  0.75 / 0.99 / 1.25
    Reach up with both air jumps (blocks)   2.9  / 3.4  / 4.0
    Longest gap he jumps (blocks)           3    / 4    / 6
    Climb height (blocks)                   9    / 12   / 16
  A base wall is 5 blocks high, so he climbs any of today's walls from level 1.
- Out of fights: he double-jumps ravines instead of bridging them, and climbs cliffs and tall trees
  for a lookout: from 12 or more blocks up he sees 16 blocks farther.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): no move changes. Between moves he uses wall
  kicks to get behind you for Shadow Step and Backstab, and climbs out of a pit dug under him. No
  move starts while he is in the air or clinging. He goes for an enemy on a pillar or behind a wall
  at once (a player or an NPC archer); on a 1x1 pillar top he may land basic attacks from a cling
  just under your feet, or break the block under your feet at player speed. He may stand where his
  enemies cannot reach him, but at most 4 s at a time; then he comes back.
- Counters: any hit, or a knockback of 0.5 or more, knocks him off a wall (he falls, and needs 0.5 s
  before he can grip again). Ledges are useless against him (no fall damage), and he has no
  knockback resistance [0, the skirmisher's], so knockback moves him about.
- Walls in raids and scouting: scouting, he climbs a rival's wall or a tree beside it and looks into
  the yard, so his report carries the garrison, the heroes and where the heart and the storehouse
  are. As a non-leader he joins his base's raids like a soldier [raids: joins, the skirmisher
  default]; as a leader, as his personality decides (below). On a raid he is his party's way in: up
  the wall away from the archers' posts and down the inside (only when he also has a way out, such
  as a climb back up the inside face), then he opens the gate from inside when his party needs it; a
  goal ends after 60 s without progress. The defenders answer: the alarm sounds once he is on the
  wall (any base NPC that sees him within 24 blocks raises it earlier), archers shoot, the two
  nearest free guards and any hero at home go for him, and a hit knocks him off the wall. Defending
  his own base, he reaches the wall top at once.
- Escaping (under 11%, see "When he is losing"), or stepping aside on a scouting round, he climbs
  where no one follows.
- Signature animation: none of his own in the design; he uses the shared acrobat and climber moves,
  with his scarf trailing behind him.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: scout (when he is not leading). He scouts and delivers reports like a faction scout, only
  better.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18], less wear on his gear [-10 / -20 /
  -30%], less hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 / +30%]. The
  job-by-job table gives scouting no work-speed or reach part. Each level he gains rolls a scout
  affix, as for any NPC.
- Specialty, from his cartographer talent (when he scouts himself): his own scouting range is 32 /
  48 / 64 blocks longer, on top of the talent's base-wide 16 / 32 / 48, and his reports mark the 5x5
  sectors around them as explored (the talent gives the base's other scouts 3x3).
- The work table also gives spymaster a scout specialty (walking up to 24 / 20 / 16 blocks from a
  rival base without raising its alarm, and reports that name the heroes seen), but his own entry
  names only the cartographer one; whether he gets it is set when he is built.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where he is, inside
  the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never the sum;
  the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of his faction at tier I while he
  leads):
  - cartographer* (realm): the base's scouts reach 16 / 32 / 48 blocks farther, and each report
    marks the 3x3 sectors around it as explored.
  - spymaster: the base's reports on rival bases carry garrison counts and whether the leader is
    home, and enemy raids are announced 0.5 / 1 / 2 minutes before they reach the walls.
  - Both are scout talents, not guard or court talents, so the Presence rule (a tier higher while
    home) does not apply to them.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Head of the Quiet Ash.
- Faction names: "Quiet Ash Clan" (the first unused name is taken), then "The Ashen Veil". Short
  name: "Quiet Ash". Motto: "Leave no footprint."
- Colours: primary ash grey [#4A4E57], secondary scarf red [#B3262E], accent navy [#1E2740]. They
  show in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, his sash and his NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: grey, with a red diagonal band and a black border [base gray; stripe_downleft red; border
  black]. Real banners hang at the station and the gates, and members get a banner item. His
  faction's wool, carpets and beds are grey, with red as the second dye.
- Base style: bamboo_village, a Village: bamboo plank walls, jungle log frames, bamboo plank floors,
  spruce plank roofs and cobblestone round the edge; signs read like "Quiet Ash Village". Culture:
  east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: scouting (scouts and cave explorers are hired
  ahead of other civilians) and military (no civilian role moves up; the soldier cap gets +1
  instead).
- His day (court duty): scouting is a travelling job, which a leader never takes, so while he leads
  he does not scout (his talents still work at the base). A short stop at the station at dawn;
  daylight patrolling between the station, the gates and the heart and standing at the station,
  greeting member players; the heart at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by any
  alarm. He answers every alarm and guards the heart in a defence, says his founding line when the
  faction starts, and announces raids, attacks on his base, a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit
  milestones in his own voice (his own lines for these are not written yet; his temperament's
  fallback lines fill in).
- If he dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until he returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more); a raid he was on falls back [(caution + 1 - loyalty) / 2 = 0.525, over
  0.5], and by the same rule it falls back when he escapes from it (his faction hears his escape
  line; there is no vengeance raid, since nobody died). If his faction's last base falls he becomes
  an exiled free hero, never a leader again in that campaign.
- How his personality steers the faction. His traits (out of 100): aggression 55, honour 50,
  ambition 40, caution 60, greed 40, sociability 10, loyalty 55, volatility 30. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids about as often as a neutral leader: about every 30 minutes, or 1.49 in-game days [35640
    ticks, interval x0.99; neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid not before 1.06 intervals
    [1].
  - Attack odds needed 0.707 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.26 of the interval [0.25], at odds
    1.55 [1.5].
  - He neither leads ordinary raids (that needs aggression 65 or more) nor stays home (caution 70 or
    more, or aggression under 35): he joins joint raids and counter-attacks.
  - A raid party falls back after losing 49.5% [50%].
  - Keeps 36% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 3 home defenders and 3 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 13 [12]: 12 from his traits, +1 from his military focus. Ranged share 0.42 [0.40].
  - Trader hired late, at population 19 [14] (his sociability is only 10); trader trips x1.32 apart

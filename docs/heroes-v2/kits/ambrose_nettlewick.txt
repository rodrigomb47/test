Ambrose Nettlewick - "The Cauldron Warlock"
Male | witch | controller | venom | eccentric genius | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: he fights the same against players
and NPCs, and he never yields: see "When he is losing"). All numbers are first values, tuned after
play.
====================================================================================================

WHO HE IS
A cheerful, absent-minded potion scholar who treats every fight as an experiment and takes notes
while he wins, or loses. His crow is unimpressed with all of it. In a one-on-one fight he is a
trapper: he fills the arena with pools, puddles and hexes, so you strike between his set-ups. Out of
battle he is his base's enchanter and rides his staff like a broom; as a faction leader he is the
Archivist of the Nettlewick Conclave.

LOOK
- Body: male, slim build, normal size [scale 100%].
- Hair: messy, medium length, dark green-black [#23302A].
- Hat and glasses: a wide-brimmed pointed hat, worn tilted, with a patched band; round glasses.
- Outfit: a long purple coat with a bandolier of vials, dark trousers and boots; freckles.
- Crow: a crow sits on his left shoulder and turns its head now and then (for looks only: it does
  not fight). When he flies it flaps along beside him.
- Weapon: a gnarled staff with a small cauldron hanging from it. It is also his broom: when he takes
  off, the staff moves from his hand to under him and he rides it side-saddle, casting with his free
  hand.
- Colours: skin [#EBCFB8], green eyes [#7BCB5E], primary purple [#5B3A86], secondary near-black
  [#1F1A26], accent green [#7BCB5E], trim gold [#C69C4E], leather [#6B4A33], glow bright green
  [#9DFF6B].
- Face: wide eyes, a single lash [lashes 1], bold brows, a wide mouth. He blinks often, every 1.5 to
  3.5 s [blink nervous].
- Idle quirks: a notebook scribble, a vial shake, the crow pecking his hat.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 broom bristles sprout from the end of his staff; in phase 3 his eyes glow
  green and his hat brim lifts.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of his attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: his accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, his face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where he stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on him. His rains get one mark per impact.
His fighting: staff; duel style trapper. As a controller he keeps about 8 blocks away [keep_distance
8] and backs off like an archer when you come within 4; he protects his allies and goes for ranged
fighters first (the controller focus). His element is venom (undead ignore it; you do not). His hex
is a mark that stacks on you; the third one sets off its effect. Cooldowns and how often he picks
each move are set when he is built.

  Basic attack
    A thrown vial (it looks like a slimeball) that flies straight, out to 14 blocks, for 4 venom
    damage every 1.5 s.

  Abilities
    POTION BARRAGE (potion_barrage) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: he throws three vials one after another, each with a different brew: the first
      poisons you, the second slows you hard, the third weakens you.
      The tell: a 0.6 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only). The colour of each vial tells
      you what it does: green = Poison, grey = Slowness, black = Weakness, always in that order.
      Counter: sidestep each throw (he aims each vial as he throws it), or block them with a shield
      (light moves are blockable like any hit). After the third he needs 0.8 s to reach for new
      vials.
      Numbers: [light, mid; barrage of 3, 0.4 s apart; each 3 venom plus, in order, Poison I 4 s,
      Slowness II 3 s, Weakness I 4 s; frames 0.6 / 0.8 / 0.8 (reaching for new vials)]

    CAULDRON (cauldron) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a pool of his brew forms where you stand and bubbles for 6 s, poisoning whoever
      stays in it. It is his answer to a raised shield (60%).
      The tell: a disc grows under you during the 1 s wind-up, and the pool waits for its warning
      delay before the first bubble.
      Counter: walk out of the circle before it starts, and stay out.
      Numbers: [area, mid; zone at the target, radius 3, delay 1.0 s (whether the delay runs inside
      the wind-up or after it is set when he is built), lasts 6 s with a pulse every 1 s; each
      pulse: venom damage over time 2 per second for 3 s + Poison I (how long that Poison lasts is
      set when he is built); frames 1.0 / 0.1 / 0.8]

    HEX MARK (hex_mark) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: an instant bolt puts a hex on you. At the third hex you are weakened and glow
      for 6 s. The bolt itself does no damage. It is his answer to you eating (50%).
      The tell: a 0.5 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only). The bolt needs a clear line of
      sight to you.
      Counter: break line of sight (a block or a tree between you) and do not let three hexes pile
      up.
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; bolt, range 12; mark hex; at 3: Weakness II and Glowing 6 s;
      frames 0.5 / 0.1 / 0.4. How long a hex stays on you is set when he is built]

    FUME STEP (fume_step) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: when you get close he hops 5 blocks back, and fumes burst where he stood,
      slowing everyone within 3 blocks. He prefers it when you are close, and it is his answer to
      you closing in (70%).
      The tell: a quick 0.4 s wind-up (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: expect it when you rush him: stop just short, let the hop happen, then close in again
      round the side of the fumes.
      Numbers: [light, close; on himself: hop back 5 and a nova of radius 3 where he stood; Slowness
      II 3 s; frames 0.4 / 0.1 / 0.4]

    BROOM HOPS (broom_hops) - boss phases 2, 3 (phase 2's new mechanic)
      What it does: he leaps up to 8 blocks on his staff-broom and lands in a splash of venom that
      leaves a small slowing puddle, then hops again: up to three hops in a row.
      The tell: a landing disc grows where he will come down, for 0.8 s before each hop.
      Counter: get off the landing disc and keep out of the puddles. A follow-up hop only starts
      when the hop before it hits, so dodging a hop ends the string. Only the last hop has the full
      0.8 s recovery: hit him then. Under a roof of 3 blocks or less he cannot leap at all.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; leap 8, radius 2; 4 venom on landing + a puddle zone (radius 2, 3
      s, Slowness I); repeatable, follows itself 100% (3 hops); frames 0.8 / 0.5 / 0.8. A hero keeps
      at most 2 zones at once, so a third puddle (or a live Cauldron) ends the oldest]
      Proposed change (in the mobility design, not settled): in phase 2 this becomes Broom Dive,
      dived from the air (see "Phase 2 in the air" under BOSS FIGHT).

    GREAT BREW (great_brew) - boss phase 3 only (phase 3's new mechanic)
      What it does: he stops and brews for 3 s; if he finishes, venom rains on the area around his
      target for 2 s: ten splashes, each with one of his three potion effects at random.
      The tell: a 1 s wind-up, then a 3 s channel in which he stands still; a mark grows for each
      splash before it lands. His hint line for this weak point: "Big one coming, please don't
      interrupt!"
      Counter: interrupt him: once he has taken 15% of his maximum health during the channel, the
      brew breaks and he is left open. One hit cannot do it alone (no hit takes more than 12% of his
      health), so start swinging at once. If it goes off, keep moving off the splash marks.
      Numbers: [area, any range; on himself, channel 3 s (breaks at 15% of his max health), then
      rain of 10 within 6 blocks over 2 s; 5 venom per impact plus Poison I, Slowness II or Weakness
      I at random; frames 1.0 / 3.0 + 2.0 / 2.0. When broken, his kit says "he staggers 2 s" and the
      boss rules say a broken channel "gives a 2 s recovery"; which of the two is set when he is
      built]

  Ultimate
    GRAND EXPERIMENT (grand_experiment)
      What it does: he lifts the cauldron, the crow caws, and twelve splashes of venom rain down
      around his target over 3 s, each with one of his potion effects at random. He also cleanses
      himself: any poison, slowing, burning or freezing you put on him is gone.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: the 1.6 s wind-up, the line "Hypothesis: you lose!", a wide
      sound, red ground marks for the impacts, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near
      the fight. He cannot be staggered during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark
      when his ultimate is ready.
      Counter: keep moving off the red marks (each splash only reaches 1-2 blocks round its mark),
      and save your splash potions for after it. Hit him in the 1.5 s recovery.
      Numbers: [ultimate; rain of 12, radius 7, over 3 s; 4 venom each plus Poison I, Slowness II or
      Weakness I at random; cleanses himself; frames 1.6 / 3.0 / 1.5. Set when he is built: his
      ultimate energy and charge rate, and the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    FIELD NOTES (field_notes): when you hurt him, 20% of the time he "learns" and hits 5% harder for
    15 s, up to 4 times over. All his damage bonuses together never exceed +25%.

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: he is a boss in every fight, whether he fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with him (it hurt him in the last 15 s, or is
  his target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. His first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with his intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which he takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and his intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (his name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever he fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, his name and title, his phase pips and a lightning
  mark when his ultimate is ready. (His bar title is set when he is built.)
- Health: his base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.25 for a
  controller, always: it never changes with who or how many fight him. At level 10 that is 130
  health (148 as a leader): about 27 s (31 s) of landed hits for a player with an iron sword at 1.6
  swings a second, half of them landing; an iron-sword soldier needs about a fifth longer, and
  attackers add up (six soldiers who all reach him need about a sixth of one soldier's time). A
  whole duel lasts two to three times its hitting time once you dodge his wind-ups. No single hit,
  from anyone (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their arrows), takes more than 12% of his
  health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage. Damage with no attacker (lava, fire,
  drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 80 (the controller value). Every hit fills his poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x
  weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt
  0.8], and a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before a heavy move
  lands) adds 25. Shooting him down out of the air adds 30. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s
  without poise damage. Full poise staggers him: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then
  the bar resets and its maximum grows 20% for the rest of the fight. No stagger during his intro, a
  phase change or his ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves he waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too, so no move reaches you sooner than that wait plus its
  wind-up (at least 1.9 s after the fight starts, or after the intro).
  During any recovery he takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where he faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Potion Barrage, Cauldron, Hex Mark, Fume Step.
    Phase 2, from 60%: adds Broom Hops (he moves about and leaves puddles); broom bristles sprout on
      his staff.
      "Fascinating! Let's test mobility!"
    Phase 3, from 25%: adds the Great Brew, a channel you can break; his eyes glow and his hat brim
      lifts.
      "Time for the big one. Please don't interrupt."
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (his exact length is set when he is built): he takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; he is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), he says the line, his
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip. A phase change always happens on the ground: if he
  is flying, he lands first (0.4 s).
- Phase 2 in the air (a proposal in the mobility design for when he is built; not settled yet): in
  phase 2 he may take to the air on his staff-broom. The take-off is the tell: 0.6 s while he mounts
  the staff and the crow caws. He stays up at most 8 s, at most 6 blocks above the ground (or above
  the highest enemy fighting him who stands on a block, a player or an NPC archer), and at most 4 s
  at a time out of his enemies' melee reach unless he is winding up a move. Up there he makes up to
  three touch-and-go dives (Broom Dive: the same venom damage and puddle as Broom Hops, range 8,
  radius 2, a landing mark that grows during the wind-up, a 0.5 s touch-down between dives) and may
  throw his light ranged moves (Potion Barrage, Hex Mark; at most 3 per take-off). Then he lands
  with at least 1.0 s of recovery (your punish window) and stays on the ground at least 6 s; he is
  never airborne more than 12 s in any 30 s. A drawn bow while he flies makes him land or dive (60%,
  at most every 8 s). A shield blocks a dive (it is never unblockable). Phase 3 has no flying
  windows: the Great Brew is cast standing. In phases 1 and 3 he takes off only to reach an enemy he
  cannot walk to (a pillar), with no moves in the air, only his basic throw.
- Shooting him down: in the air he has a separate air poise bar of 100 that drains 10 per second
  after 2 s without a hit. Each arrow adds 20, a crossbow bolt 30 (15 per Multishot bolt), a thrown
  trident 40, a wind charge 35, a snowball or an egg 10, a fishing rod hook 15 (and pulls him toward
  you), a melee hit while he is within 3 blocks above you 25 (40 on a critical hit). A stun, a
  freeze, Slowness II or worse (a splash potion), or a lightning strike (a Channeling trident in a
  thunderstorm) downs him at once. Examples from the design: five arrows; three bolts and a
  snowball; two wind charges and two arrows; a trident and three arrows. Downed, his broom folds and
  he drops almost straight down (fall damage above 6 blocks, counted as one hit under the 12% cap,
  whoever downed him), lies 2 s on the ground taking 20% more damage, cannot take off again for 12
  s, and takes 30 poise. Slowness I halves his climb rate. NPC archers' arrows and bolts count
  exactly as yours do.
- Weak points:
  - Channel: the Great Brew breaks after damage of 15% of his maximum health during its 3 s channel,
    and leaves him open (see Great Brew). Hint line: "Big one coming, please don't interrupt!"
  - Recovery: the punish bonus doubles during his recoveries [+30% instead of +15%]. The line or
    prop that hints at it is set when he is built.
- How he reads you (each answer replaces his next pick; their cooldowns are set when he is built).
  He reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You come close -> Fume Step (70%).
  - You raise a shield -> Cauldron (60%).
  - You eat -> Hex Mark (50%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever he fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter waits
  between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All his
  damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. He has no other enrage trigger.
- Intro: he tips his hat and adjusts his glasses. "Ah, a test subject! I mean, a guest. Hold still,
  please."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press him; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When he is losing: he escapes [his eccentric-genius traits: aggression 35 + honour 60 - caution 55
  = 40, under 65: an experiment can be repeated, a researcher cannot]. Under 13% of his health
  [flee_at 13%: a third of his retreat_below 37.5%, kept inside his last phase], in any fight, from
  a player, soldiers, another hero or monsters alike, he breaks off for good, from wherever he is:
  in the air he does not land first. His raid falling back while he is in or near the target, or the
  heart of the base he defends falling, sends him off too. Nothing holds him anywhere [stands_for:
  none, loyalty 50]. Escaping, he starts no moves and flies off on the broom with only his stamina
  as a limit (no 8 s air budget; up to 24 blocks above the ground, in any weather): the slice's
  uncatchable escape, unless he is shot down or staggered first. He stays a boss while he runs: the
  12% cap, his poise and his air poise still hold, so five arrows (or three bolts and a snowball, or
  a trident and three arrows) bring him down, from you or from NPC archers, and a stagger stops him
  for 2.5 s where he is; he has three or four sword blows of health left. Cornered with no way out
  for 3 s, he fights back, then flies again as soon as a way opens. His refuge is his home base,
  never the base he flees (from a fight at his own base: another base of his faction, or the wild
  until no one follows); the escape ends inside its walls or once no pursuer has been within 48
  blocks for 10 s. Then he stays away 5 minutes and until he is above 50% health, and does not go
  back for those he fled. The boss bar shows "fled" for 3 s and goes. "Experiment concluded! Result:
  I should leave. Don't touch my notes!"
  - At most one escape in 10 minutes: caught again inside that window, he fights to the death.
  - At zero health he dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow;
    killed in the air, he falls. The bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "Fascinating... so that is what it
    feels like. Noted."
  - A hero that runs from him: with honour 60 he lets it go and takes another target.
- Rewards: only when he dies, to whoever killed him (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt him in his last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x his level, at most 100), his non-kit gear (his kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero he comes back 10 minutes after his death, whoever killed him
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at his home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); his kit items vanish at death and return with him. He has no death ability of his own. A
  hero of his faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring him back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HIM
1. Break the Great Brew. In phase 3, when he stops to brew, hit him hard during the 3 s channel: 15%
   of his health in damage breaks it and leaves him open. It takes at least two hits (no hit takes
   more than 12%), so start swinging at once.
2. Punish every recovery: recoveries are his weak point (+30% instead of +15%). Openings: after
   Potion Barrage (0.8 s), after the last Broom Hop (0.8 s), after the Great Brew (2 s), after his
   ultimate (1.5 s), and after a dive landing (at least 1.0 s).
3. Read the vials and do not turtle. Green is Poison, grey is Slowness, black is Weakness, always in
   that order: sidestep them. A raised shield blocks them but gets a Cauldron dropped on you (60%):
   step out of its circle before it starts.
4. Close in smartly. He backs away when you come within 4 blocks and answers you up close with Fume
   Step (70%): let him hop, go round the 3-block cloud, then close again. Do not eat in sight of him
   (Hex Mark, 50%), and break line of sight so the hexes never reach three.
5. Bring a bow, a crossbow or wind charges if he flies: five arrows, three bolts and a snowball, or
   two wind charges and two arrows bring him down, and a splash potion of Slowness II downs him at
   once: 2 s on the ground at +20% damage, no take-off for 12 s, +30 poise. Under a roof lower than
   4 blocks he cannot take off, and at 3 blocks or less he cannot use Broom Hops.
6. Keep a milk bucket: vanilla milk clears his Poison, Slowness, Weakness and Glowing. (He dislikes
   milk buckets: "they ruin everything".) His poise is small (80): an axe (x1.6) or a mace (x2.0)
   staggers him quickly.
7. Keep your bow for the end. At 13% he leaves on the broom, up to 24 blocks high and with no time
   limit: five arrows bring him down (2 s on the ground at +20% damage), or a stagger stops him
   before he takes off. If he gets away he is at his home base, hurt, for the next 5 minutes, and
   caught again within 10 minutes he does not fly off a second time.

MOVEMENT
Mobility trait: flyer (ceiling II), on his staff-broom. He has every feature from level 1; the
numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility never
changes a move's damage, range or timing, and he never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC has is
the one exception).
- From level 1: flight, gliding and the dive.
- Take-off: 0.6 s (the tell: he mounts the staff, which moves from his hand to under him, and one
  push lifts him 1.5 blocks). He needs enough stamina and 4 blocks of headroom, so roofs, tunnels
  and dense trees make him walk. Landing takes 0.4 s, never on farmland, lava, fire or powder snow,
  and costs him no fall damage.
- Stamina: flying drains it [1 per second; 0.5 cruising on a trip; 2 hovering in place; x1.5 with a
  backpack more than half full; x1.5 in a fight, against anyone]; gliding costs nothing; it comes
  back on the ground after 1 s. From 25% he heads for a landing and looks tired (a tell); at 0 he
  can only glide down and is exhausted (winded on landing, no take-off until 50%).
- Height limits: travelling, at most 24 blocks above the ground (cruising 6-10 above the terrain);
  on a raid before anyone engages him (the approach and the wall crossing) at most 10 (a wall top
  counts as ground, so he crosses any wall); in any fight, against anyone, at most 6 above the
  ground or the highest enemy fighting him who stands on a block (a player or an NPC archer). Out of
  a fight he walks in a thunderstorm.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Stamina (seconds of powered flight)     27              / 35.5            / 45
    Cruise speed (blocks per second)        5.9             / 6.4             / 7.0
    Climb rate (blocks per second)          1.8             / 2.4             / 3.0
    Glide: sink, forward (b/s), ratio       4.0, 6.5, 1.6:1 / 3.0, 7.2, 2.4:1 / 2.4, 8.0, 3.3:1
    Glide time (s)                          9               / 12              / 15
    Take-off needs (stamina)                42%             / 32%             / 25%
    Stamina back on the ground (per s)      1.5             / 2.0             / 2.5
  On long trips, landing to rest when needed, he averages about 4.4 blocks a second at level 1 and
  5.8 at level 20 (walking round the terrain is about 4.3), so he compares the two for each trip.
- Out of fights: he flies the long legs of his trips, up to the library's high shelves, and back
  from errands outside the walls; as a leader he mostly stays home. Away from raids his routes give
  enemy bases a wide berth.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): Broom Hops (and the proposed Broom Dive) are
  unchanged by level. In every fight he flies only inside the air rules: at most 8 s per take-off,
  at least 6 s on the ground between, and at most 4 s at a time out of his enemies' melee reach. If
  you (or an NPC archer) tower up a pillar he flies up to you even outside phase 2 (at level 1 he
  climbs about 16 blocks in his 8 s) and throws from beside you. These air rules do not grow with
  level: they are the counterplay. His air poise works in every fight and while he escapes, and NPC
  archers' arrows count the same as yours.
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader he joins his base's raids like a soldier [raids: joins, the
  controller default]; as a leader he joins only joint raids and counter-attacks (below). On a raid
  he scouts first: he circles the target 8-24 blocks out, over the walls when he chooses, and what
  he sees goes into his faction's report on that base. He flies over the wall ring at the raid
  height and lands inside near his goal, but only when he can get out again (his stamina back to its
  take-off level within 20 s on the ground, or the gate). On a raid, until an enemy engages him, he
  flies at most 10 s per take-off with at least 6 s on the ground between; once he is fighting, the
  fight's 8 s rule holds. Defending archers shoot an airborne hero before anything else, and five
  arrows bring him down. Inside he takes one goal: he opens the way when his soldiers are held
  outside, hunts a defending hero he sees inside, and otherwise goes for the heart with his party; a
  goal ends after 60 s without progress. Defending his own base, he patrols over his walls and meets
  an airborne intruder with his light ranged moves.
- Escaping (under 13%, see "When he is losing"): flight with only his stamina as a limit, and no
  height limit but the travel one.
- Signature animations: broom versions of the take-off, riding side-saddle, casting while riding and
  landing; the crow flaps beside him.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: enchanter (when he is not leading). He works his base's enchanting table once the base
  qualifies. As a faction leader his duty becomes court, but he still spends daylight at the
  enchanting table and counts as the enchanter there.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18], less wear [-10 / -20 / -30%], less
  hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 / +30%]. The job-by-job table
  names no proficiency part for the enchanter's job, so whether work speed and reach show at the
  enchanting table is set when he is built. Each level he gains rolls an enchanter affix, as for any
  NPC.
- Specialty, from his sage_of_letters talent (when he enchants himself): he sees the table's three

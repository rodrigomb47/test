Kagenori Tsurumine - "The Still Crane Samurai"
Male | samurai | striker | shock | stoic | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 1, 2026-11-12). All numbers are first values,
tuned after play.
====================================================================================================

WHO HE IS
A quiet swordsman of few words who announces his name before every duel, never strikes a kneeling
foe, and fights like still water until the moment his blade moves. In a one-on-one fight he is a
counter fighter: patient, with a guard stance that answers melee, so you bait the stance and hit
after his swing. Out of battle he stands guard and drills the soldiers; as a faction leader he is
the Lord of the Still Crane.

LOOK
- Body: male, standard build, drawn a little large [scale 102%].
- Hair: long black hair [#1E1F2A] in a high ponytail.
- Outfit: an indigo coat with a white crane crest, white hakama (wide pleated trousers), a steel
  shoulder plate on the right, wrist guards, a sash and sandals.
- Weapon: a long sword and a short sword at his hip; he fights with the long one drawn. His kit:
  "Crane's Edge" (an iron sword with Sweeping Edge II), a chainmail chestplate and iron boots; kit
  items never drop and come back when he revives.
- Colours: skin [#F1D3BC], blue eyes [#6C7BD0], primary indigo [#2E3A6B], secondary off-white
  [#E8E4D8], accent steel grey [#9AA6B2], trim gold [#B8963E], metal light steel [#C9CED6].
- Face: sharp eyes with no lashes [lashes 0], thick straight brows, a small mouth. He blinks slowly
  and calmly, every 4 to 7 s [blink calm].
- Moods: when he is happy he keeps his focused eyes and shows only a faint smile.
- Idle quirks: his hand resting on the hilt; eyes closed, breathing slowly.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 he puts on a plain helmet; in phase 3 he draws the short sword as well,
  and his eyes and the edges of his blades glow.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of his attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: his accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, his face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where he stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Against a player each move's damage is capped [16 for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in
level-1 health points], then x1.15 in a boss fight [damage_vs_players 115%], and no single hit takes
a player from full health to zero.
His fighting: katana; duel style counter. He closes to melee and goes for the strongest enemy first
(set in his file). His element is shock: you take 30% more from it while you stand in water or rain.
He resists shock and is weak to blight [resistances: shock +20, blight -15]. The cooldowns and
weights below come from the design's example file of him (design section 12.18); a move's weight is
how often the picker likes it (default 5).

  Basic attack
    A katana string of three cuts. Every third hit puts a "storm" mark on you (small particles above
    your head) [mark storm, 1 stack]. Nothing in his kit reads the storm mark yet; what it does is
    set when he is built. A raised shield blocks the cuts like any hit from the front.

  Abilities
    THREE CUTS (three_cut) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: three quick cuts back to back; the last one also knocks you back a little.
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: block it (light moves are blockable like any hit) or step back; each cut can be
      dodged on its own.
      Numbers: [light, close; 55%, 55% and 80% + knockback 1; frames 0.45 / 0.45 / 0.35; cooldown 6
      s, weight 6]

    STILL WATER (iai_stance) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 (see the phase 3 note below)
      What it does: a quick-draw counter stance held for 1.5 s. Hit him in melee during it and he
      takes only a fifth of the blow, then answers at once with a draw cut that stuns you for 1 s
      (on a player a stun is Slowness IV and Mining Fatigue II: you keep control). He prefers this
      stance against players (its score doubles).
      The tell: his hand goes to the hilt with a guard click (a shield-block sound) and electric
      sparks; the 1.5 s stance itself is the wind-up.
      Counter: stop attacking for the 1.5 s, or shoot him: arrows are not countered. If nobody hits
      him, the 0.8 s recovery after the stance is one of his weak points.
      Numbers: [heavy, close; stance 1.5 s, absorb 80%; riposte 140% + stun 1 s; frames 1.5 (the
      stance) / 0.1 / 0.8; cooldown 10 s]

    THUNDERCLAP DRAW (thunderclap_draw) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a lightning-fast draw that cuts along a straight line 8 blocks long and 1 wide
      toward you, for shock damage. The line stops at the first solid block. When it hits, 30% of
      the time he follows with Crane Step. Sometimes (his lines are rate-limited) he says "Thunder
      answers the blade."
      The tell: 1.3 s of total stillness, a glint on the hilt, and a strip on the ground along the
      line growing to full length.
      Counter: step sideways off the strip, or keep a block between you; then punish the slow
      re-sheathe, one of his weak points.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; line 8 x 1; 150% shock; followup Crane Step 30%; frames 1.3 (stillness,
      a glint) / 0.05 / 0.85 (re-sheathe); cooldown 11 s]

    CRANE STEP (crane_step) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 (see the phase 3 note below)
      What it does: a 6-block dash along the ground that cuts everyone within 1.5 blocks of his
      path. When it hits, 70% of the time he goes straight into Three Cuts. It is his answer to a
      drawn bow (70%).
      The tell: a strip grows on the ground along the dash during the 0.8 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: step off the strip. A dash stops at walls and at drops over 4 blocks, and he skips it
      when water or lava lies on the path.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; dash 6; 80%; followup Three Cuts 70%; frames 0.8 / 0.25 / 0.8; cooldown
      8 s]

    GUARD SPLITTER (guard_splitter) - boss phases 2, 3 (new in phase 2)
      What it does: a heavy cut that breaks guard: if you are blocking, your shield is disabled (the
      vanilla axe rule). It is his answer to a raised shield (60%).
      The tell: a 0.9 s wind-up with a sound; the shape of its ground mark is set when he is built.
      Counter: lower your shield and step back out of reach during the wind-up.
      Numbers: [heavy, close; melee, breaks guard; 110% + guard_break 4 s; frames 0.9 / 0.1 / 0.9;
      cooldown 12 s. The general boss rules say a guard-breaking move disables a shield for 5 s;
      which length counts is set when he is built]

    CHAINED LIGHTNING DRAW (chain_draw) - boss phases 2, 3 (new in phase 2)
      What it does: lightning from his draw strikes a target up to 10 blocks away, then jumps to up
      to two more enemies, each within 4 blocks of the last (never the same one twice). Each one hit
      takes shock damage and is slowed.
      The tell: a 1.0 s wind-up with a sound; the shape of its ground mark is set when he is built.
      Counter: spread out from your friends (it jumps up to 4 blocks), and keep out of water and
      rain (shock hurts 30% more there).
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; chain of 3, range 10, jump radius 4; 7 shock + Slowness I 3 s; frames
      1.0 / 0.45 / 0.8; cooldown 14 s]

    FALLING CRANE (falling_crane) - boss phase 3 only (new in phase 3)
      What it does: he leaps at you from up to 8 blocks away and lands with a cut that hits everyone
      within 2 blocks of the landing point and knocks them back hard. He always follows it with
      Three Cuts. From level 13 he may start it from a 2-block ledge he jumped onto.
      The tell: a landing disc grows on the ground during the 0.9 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: leave the disc during the wind-up and keep moving after he lands; the punish window
      comes after the Three Cuts. Under a ceiling of 3 blocks or less he does not leap.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; leap 8, landing radius 2; 100% + knockback 2; followup Three
      Cuts 100%; frames 0.9 / 0.5 / 1.0; cooldown 12 s]

    TWIN SKY (state twin_sky) - boss phase 3
      At the phase 3 change he draws his short sword and fights with two blades for the rest of the
      fight, faster and harder. His face turns determined and he says "Two blades. No more
      restraint."
      Numbers: [attack speed +20%, damage +10%]

  Ultimate
    HEAVEN-SPLITTING DRAW (heaven_split)
      What it does: one enormous draw cut along a straight line 12 blocks long and 2 wide, with a
      harmless lightning flash along the line and a thunder sound. Like his other draws, the line
      stops at the first solid block.
      The tell: the biggest in his kit: a 2.0 s wind-up in which he closes his eyes and stops
      blinking, then opens them sharp; the line "Close your eyes. It is already over."; a wide
      sound; a red strip on the ground along the line; and the screen darkens for 2 s for the
      players fighting him. He cannot be staggered during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a
      lightning mark when his ultimate is ready.
      Counter: dodge sideways off the 2-wide strip, or get a block between you; then hit him in the
      1.6 s recovery.
      Numbers: [ultimate; line 12 x 2; 22 shock; frames 2.0 / 0.1 / 1.6. It needs a full energy bar
      (100); how fast the bar fills and the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s) are set when
      he is built]

  Passives
    STILL MIND (still_mind): every time his counter stance catches a blow, he gains 15 energy toward
    his ultimate [on_counter: energy 15; at most once every 6 s].

BOSS FIGHT
- When: boss mode is on while a player is engaged with him (the player hurt him in the last 15 s, or
  is his target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players never count). It ends 10 s after
  the last engaged player leaves, and his health share never jumps. The first engagement with a
  player each in-game day opens with his intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which he takes 50% less damage and
  cannot be staggered, a title on your screen (his name, title and faction) and his intro line. A
  boss bar shows a star, his name and title, his phase pips and a lightning mark when his ultimate
  is ready. His title is "the Still Crane".
- Health: his field health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.2 for a
  striker, and +50% for each extra engaged player (counted up to 5). At level 10 against one player
  that is 129 health (147 as a leader): about 34 s (39 s) of landed hits with an iron sword at 1.6
  swings a second, half of them landing; his chainmail and iron boots soak part of every blow. A
  whole fight lasts two to three times that once you dodge his wind-ups. No single player hit takes
  more than 12% of his boss health; the excess becomes poise damage.
- Poise: 100. Your hits fill his poise bar [damage x weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its
  fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], and a parry (your shield raised in the
  last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands) adds 25. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without
  poise damage. Full poise staggers him: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar
  resets and its maximum grows 20% for the rest of the fight. No stagger during his intro, a phase
  change or his ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves he waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  During any recovery he takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where he faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Three Cuts, Still Water, Thunderclap Draw, Crane Step.
    Phase 2, from 60%: the helmet goes on (armour +20%); adds Guard Splitter and Chained Lightning
      Draw. The change takes 1.2 s, with a determined face.
      "Then I will fight you properly."
    Phase 3, from 25%: Twin Sky (two swords); adds Falling Crane. The change takes 1.4 s.
      "Forgive me, teacher."
  During a phase change he takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; he is
  never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near
  lava or a drop), he says the line, his look changes and the bar gains a pip. Phase 3 note: the
  design's kit table keeps Still Water and Crane Step in all three phases, but its example file
  leaves both out of the phase 3 list; which is right is set when he is built.
- Weak points:
  - Recovery: after a counter stance that nobody hit, after the re-sheathe of Thunderclap Draw, and
    in every other recovery, the punish bonus doubles [+30% instead of +15%].
  - Back: the bonus for hitting him from behind (more than 120 degrees from where he faces) doubles
    [during a recovery, +30% more instead of +15%].
  - The line or prop that hints at each weak point is set when he is built.
- How he reads you (each answer replaces his next pick):
  - You raise a shield -> Guard Splitter (60%, at most once every 10 s). Guard Splitter is a phase
    2-3 move; whether this answer can come in phase 1 is set when he is built.
  - You draw a bow -> Crane Step (70%, at most once every 8 s), and from there Three Cuts 70% of the
    time.
  - Against players, Still Water scores double, so expect the stance often in a duel.
- Enrage: after 180 s of boss mode (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter waits between moves
  (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All his damage bonuses
  together never exceed +25%. In phase 3, Twin Sky's +10% and enrage's +15% take him exactly to that
  cap.
- Intro: a bow, then his hand to the hilt. "Tsurumine Kagenori. Draw when you are ready."
- Yield: he kneels (honour 90; only heroes with honour 30 or less, or leaders with caution 70 or
  more, refuse). A killing blow from a player or a player's projectile is stopped at 1 health, and
  he kneels for 5 s, untouchable and passive, while you see "Sneak and use to spare, strike to
  finish". His yield line: "...Well struck."
  - Spared (sneak and use): he walks home and will not fight for 10 minutes (a defeat, not a death;
    no gear dropped). "I will remember this mercy."
  - Finished (strike): he dies. Rewards: XP orbs (7 x his level, at most 100), his non-kit gear (his
    kit never drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per player per in-game day).
  - No choice in 5 s: he collapses and dies. Killed by NPCs or outside a boss fight: a 1 s defeat
    beat, then death.
- Escape: in a boss fight he never runs at low health: he fights to the yield and kneels. The one
  order that pulls him out of a boss fight is a lost raid (his raid falls back, or its leader falls,
  while he is in or near the target). Outside boss fights he escapes when his health drops under his
  retreat point away from home [retreat_below 28%, worked out from the formula of design section
  5.4], when his raid is lost, or, as a leader, when the base he is in falls or he is under 50%
  health at its heart with no guard beside him. At his own base he does not escape: he withdraws
  like a soldier and comes back at 60% health. Escaping, he starts no moves and runs with any
  movement he has, even where no one can follow (an escape counts as travel, so his travel bonuses
  are on); cornered with no way out for 3 s, he fights back, then runs again as soon as a way opens.
  It ends inside his refuge's walls (his home base; for a leader the capital, or the nearest other
  standing base; never the base he flees) or once no pursuer has been within 48 blocks for 10 s.
  Then he stays away 5 minutes and until he is above 50% health, and does not go back for those he
  fled. At most one escape in 10 minutes: inside that window he withdraws, kneels or goes home with
  the party instead. An escape drops nothing and gives no rewards; a killing blow while he flees is
  a normal defeat.
- Revival: like every hero he comes back 10 minutes after his death [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at
  his home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing base); his kit items vanish at
  death and return with him. He has no death ability of his own. A hero of his faction with the
  revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring him back 20 / 30 / 40% sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HIM
1. Do not swing into Still Water. When his hand goes to the hilt with a click and sparks, stop
   attacking for the 1.5 s stance, or shoot him: arrows are not countered, while a melee hit is 80%
   absorbed and answered with a 140% cut and a stun. If nobody hits him, the stance ends in a 0.8 s
   recovery where your punish bonus is doubled.
2. Step sideways from his draws. Thunderclap Draw (8 x 1) and Heaven-Splitting Draw (12 x 2) are
   straight lines, with a strip on the ground that grows over 1.3 s and 2.0 s. The line stops at the
   first solid block, so a block between you and him saves you too. Then punish the re-sheathe.
3. Get behind him. His back is a weak point, and so are his recoveries: the best moment is his back
   while he re-sheathes, or after the Three Cuts that follow Falling Crane.
4. Bring an axe or a mace for his poise of 100 (x1.6 and x2.0): a stagger gives you 2.5 s at +25%
   damage. A shield parry of a heavy move adds 25 poise, but from phase 2 a shield held up draws
   Guard Splitter (60%), which disables it.
5. Stay dry and do not draw a bow in the open at mid range: shock hurts 30% more in water or rain,
   and a drawn bow brings Crane Step (70%), then Three Cuts. Water or lava between you makes him
   skip the dash, and a ceiling of 3 blocks or less stops Falling Crane in phase 3.
6. Respect phase 3. At 25% he draws his second sword (+20% attack speed, +10% damage), and every
   Falling Crane is followed by Three Cuts: leave the 2-block landing disc during its 0.9 s wind-up
   and keep moving after he lands.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: leaper (ceiling II) and runner (ceiling I). He has every feature from level 1; the
numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility never
changes a move's damage, range or timing, and he never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC has is
the one exception).
- From level 1: big bounds (higher jumps and long jumps over gaps) and the landing roll (no slowdown
  when he lands). Before a leap he crouches for 0.3 s (the tell). As a runner he travels faster and
  gets less hungry on trips, but only while travelling: both are off in fights.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Jump height (blocks)                    2.00 / 2.24 / 2.50
    Longest gap he leaps (blocks)           3    / 4    / 6
    Safe fall (blocks he drops unhurt)      7.2  / 8.5  / 10.0
    Travel speed                            +6%  / +8%  / +10%
    Travel hunger                           -12% / -16% / -20%
- What his jumps reach: a rise up to his jump height minus 0.3, so 1.5-block fences and walls from
  level 1 and 2-block ledges from level 13 (that needs 2.3).
- Out of fights: he takes ravines, streams, fences and ledges without bridging or detours.
- In fights: no move changes (Crane Step and Falling Crane are as written). Between moves he may
  bound onto high ground; from level 13 he takes 2-block ledges (high ground a player can tower to),
  and Falling Crane may start from one. He goes for a player on a pillar or behind a wall at once
  when a jump reaches; on a 1x1 pillar top he may land basic attacks from the top of his jump. He
  may stand where you cannot reach him, but at most 4 s at a time; then he comes back. A leap is
  never aimed next to a drop deeper than his safe fall or into lava, fire or the void, and in a
  fight he never leaps a gap whose floor is one of those.
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader he always goes on his base's raids and is picked first [raids:
  leads, the striker default]; as a leader, as his personality decides (below). His jumps cannot
  clear today's 5-block base walls (a jump onto a wall is at most 3 blocks for any leaper, and his
  own reach is 1.5, then 2 from level 13). He gets in over a lower or broken wall or a player's fort
  within his jump, or leaps onto the top from higher ground outside (gaps up to 3 / 4 / 6), and only
  when he also has a way out; otherwise he goes through the gate with the soldiers. Inside he takes
  one goal: he opens the way when his soldiers are held outside, hunts a defending hero he sees
  inside, and otherwise goes for the heart with his party; a goal ends after 60 s without progress.
  Defending his own base, he bounds onto roofs and ledges at once.
- Escaping (never while he is still fighting to win), he uses all of this, even where no one can
  follow.
- Signature animation: his own leap, arms spread and sleeves like wings (the crane).

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: guard (when he is not leading). He patrols and answers alarms like a guard, only better.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): as a guard he answers alarms from farther away instead of working faster [+8 / +12 / +16
  blocks]. He also has a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18], less wear on his
  gear [-10 / -20 / -30%], less hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 /
  +30%]. Each level he gains rolls a guard affix, as for any NPC.
- Presence (his specialty as a guard): drill and mentor count one tier higher while he is at his
  base (inside the walls or within 16 blocks) and not on a raid; at tier III they use the tier IV
  numbers. So at level 1, at home, drill already gives 2 XP an hour and mentor +25% work XP. His
  personal touch, from drill: dawn sparring with 2 / 3 / 4 soldiers, who gain 10 / 15 / 20 combat XP
  (non-lethal; it stops at half health).
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where he is, inside
  the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never the sum;
  the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of his faction at tier I while he
  leads):
  - drill: the base's soldiers gain 1 / 2 / 3 work XP per in-game hour by day [tier IV with
    Presence: 4].
  - mentor* (realm): NPCs within 16 blocks gain +15 / +25 / +35% work XP, and recruits start higher:
    at tier I every second recruit at level 2, at tier II every recruit at level 2, at tier III at
    level 3 [tier IV with Presence: +45% work XP, recruits at level 4]. The talent table also gives
    mentor a personal effect (NPCs of the job he works, within 16 blocks, work 5 / 8 / 10% faster),
    but his own entry names only the dawn sparring; whether it applies to a guard is set when he is
    built.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Lord of the Still Crane.
- Faction names: "House of the Still Crane" (the first unused name is taken), then "Still Crane
  Watch". Short name: "Still Crane". Motto: "The blade waits. So do we."
- Colours: primary indigo [#2E3A6B], secondary off-white [#E8E4D8], accent steel grey [#9AA6B2].
  They show in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, his sash and his NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: blue, with a white circle in the middle and a light grey border [base blue; circle white;
  border light_gray]. Real banners hang at the station and the gates, and members get a banner item.
  Blue is set by hand because the nearest dye to indigo would be grey. His faction's wool, carpets
  and beds are blue, with white as the second dye.
- Base style: deepslate_keep, a Keep: deepslate brick walls, dark oak log frames, spruce plank
  floors, deepslate tile roofs and cobbled deepslate round the edge; signs read like "Still Crane
  Keep". Culture: east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: military (no civilian role moves
  up; the soldier cap gets +1 instead) and mining (miners and smelters are hired ahead of other
  civilians).
- His day (court duty): a short stop at the station at dawn; daylight walking between the station,
  the gates and the heart, greeting member players (his first talent, drill, has no workplace); the
  heart at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by any alarm. He answers every alarm and
  guards the heart in a defence, says his founding line when the faction starts, and announces
  raids, attacks on his base, a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit milestones in his own voice
  (his own lines for these are not written yet; his temperament's fallback lines fill in).
- If he dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until he returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more); a raid he was on carries on without him [(caution + 1 - loyalty) / 2 =
  0.40, not over 0.5]. If his faction's last base falls he becomes an exiled free hero, never a
  leader again in that campaign.
- How his personality steers the faction. His traits (out of 100): aggression 50, honour 90,
  ambition 45, caution 65, greed 25, sociability 30, loyalty 85, volatility 10. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids a little less often: about every 32 minutes, or 1.59 in-game days [38160 ticks, interval
    x1.06; neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid not before 1.33 intervals [1].
  - Attacks only with a clear edge: attack odds needed 0.757 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.29 of
    the interval [0.25], at odds 1.62 [1.5].
  - He neither leads ordinary raids (that needs aggression 65 or more) nor stays home (caution 70 or
    more, or aggression under 35): he joins joint raids and counter-attacks.
  - A raid party falls back after losing 52.5% [50%].
  - Keeps 38% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 3 home defenders and 3 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 13 [12]: 12 from his traits, +1 from his military focus. Ranged share 0.445 [0.40].
  - Trader hired late, at population 18 [14] (the formula lands on 17.5; how a half is rounded is
    set when he is built); trader trips x1.16 apart [1]; amounts kept back from selling x1.27 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 10 [10]; recruitment interval x1.03 [1]; food reserve x1.14 [1].
  - New house when free beds are 1 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 77.5% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 419 [384].
  - Miners want 12 gold and 6 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8]: he is not greedy.
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts in full [fd 1]; loot does not tempt him (greed under 50);
    monster lairs (the Hollow's) are much more attractive targets for an honourable, careful leader
    [-550]. Mercy rule (honour 70 or more): he spares a faction that lost a base in the last in-game
    day.
  - Almost no random noise on his decisions [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.02]: he is predictable.
  - Stoic rule (milestone 2, when pacts arrive): he never breaks an agreement by surprise; as an
    honourable leader (honour 75 or more) he declares a break openly instead of betraying.

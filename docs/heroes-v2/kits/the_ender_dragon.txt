The Ender Dragon - "Empress of the End"
Female | ender dragon | vanguard | arcane | proud noble | legendary | boss hero
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 4, 2026-11-18, after a second review: how far her
pushes throw you and what helps against them. Version 3, the same day, after your answers: she has
no personal name; she turns back into her token if her faction falls; her wings and tail can now
throw you off a cliff, into lava or off an End island, see TERRAIN AND BLOCKS; her Spirecore can now
be upgraded. Version 2, 2026-11-18, after review). She is one of the two boss heroes: not among the
200, never a faction leader, and she joins a faction only through her token (see "HOW SHE JOINS A
FACTION"). She fights the same against players and NPCs and never yields. All numbers are first
values, tuned after play. Design: docs/heroes-v2/BOSS_HEROES.md section 5.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
The Empress of the End has ruled an island of pale stone over the void for longer than anyone has
counted, and she does not think a token makes her anyone's servant: she has decided to grant a
faction the honour of her company. She is regal, vain, curious about the green world and amused by
its people. She fights like the vanilla boss: she circles overhead spitting ender fireballs that
leave clouds of dragon's breath, dives at whoever annoys her, lands on the highest perch in reach
to breathe across the ground (arrows glance off her while she sits there), and raises end crystals
on obsidian spires that heal her through a purple beam until someone breaks them. The fight is
about her crystals: shoot the open ones, climb to the caged ones, and catch her when she lands.
When she dies an egg waits at her home, and ten minutes later she hatches from it.

LOOK
- Body: female, adult (her one body for good), standard build, tall [scale 108%: 1.97 blocks].
- Hair: long black hair [#1B1720] with a deep-purple under-layer [#7A3FB8], swept back from her
  horns.
- Dragon features: two horns swept back from the temples, black with purple tips; large dragon
  wings, black finger bones and purple membranes, folded against her back on the ground (about
  2.2 blocks across when open); a long black scaled tail with pale spines along its top; a row of
  pale spines from her nape down her back (the vanilla dragon's); small black scales on her
  cheekbones, forearms and shins.
- Outfit (modest): a high-collared black scale-mail bodice with purple trim and long sleeves, a long
  black-over-purple skirt split to the knee at the front, obsidian-black pauldrons with three pale
  spines each, black boots with clawed toe caps.
- Weapon: her hands, in obsidian talons with a purple glow inside.
- Colours: pale skin with a lilac undertone [#E9DDE6], glowing purple eyes [#D08BFF], primary End
  black [#1C1A22], secondary deep purple [#3B2A4F], accent ender purple [#C77DFF], trim end stone
  [#E3E2B4], glow dragon's breath [#E3A8FF].
- Face: cat-slit eyes with a glossy, glowing purple iris (the vanilla dragon's eyes), long lashes,
  bold brows, a soft mouth with two small fangs. She blinks slowly [blink calm]. Her "happy" is a
  closed-eye, chin-up smile; when angry her slits narrow and her throat glows purple.
- Idle quirks: she preens a wing, curls her tail round her feet, gazes up at the sky.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 purple veins glow faintly in her wing membranes and her horn tips glow.
  In phase 3 her eyes glow, dragon's-breath motes rise from her mouth, and her spines glow
  end-stone pale.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is in plain health points (2 points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid
4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy, area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its
whole wind-up and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light
moves show only the animation. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits [16 for a move, 30 for the ultimate,
at the level-1 reference]: there is no extra damage to players and no protection from a one-hit
kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes may gang up on one target, and
any number of enemies on her.
Her fighting: fists style (her claws); duel style summoner (she fights through her crystals: break
them, or dive her). She fights in the front line [keep_distance 0, the vanguard default] and goes
for enemy heroes first (a proud temperament). Her element is arcane: it passes through armour and
shields. She resists arcane [+40] and fire [+40] (the vanilla dragon is fire-proof). Her moves never
break, place or burn a block, but her pushes can throw you to your death (see TERRAIN AND BLOCKS).

  Basic attack
    A claw string on the ground: rake, rake, a two-handed slash; every third hit adds 3 arcane.

  Abilities
    ENDER FIREBALL (ender_fireball) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a purple fireball that bursts where it lands and leaves a cloud of dragon's
      breath for 5 s. She spits them from the air as she circles, and at whoever eats (60%) or
      stands above her (50%).
      The tell: she draws her head back with purple light in her throat (a light move: the
      animation only).
      Counter: sidestep it, then stay out of the cloud.
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; shot, range 20, splash radius 2; 4 arcane, then a cloud of
      radius 2.5 for 5 s, 2 arcane each second inside; frames 0.5 / 0.2 / 0.4; cooldown 5 s; at
      most three per flight]

    DRAGON BREATH (dragon_breath) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she breathes a cone of dragon's breath and leaves a cloud where it ends. Arcane
      passes shields, so it is one of her answers to a raised shield (50%). She uses it twice as
      often while perched.
      The tell: she rears and her throat glows magenta; a fan of marks grows in front of her for
      1 s.
      Counter: get out of the fan (it is 6 blocks long and 70 degrees wide), or behind her; then
      keep out of the cloud.
      Numbers: [area, close and mid; cone 6, 70 degrees; 6 arcane, then a cloud of radius 3 for 6 s
      at the cone's end, 2 arcane each second; frames 1.0 / 0.8 / 1.0; cooldown 12 s]

    WING GUST (wing_gust) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: her wings beat down and throw everyone around her about 3 blocks away, at full
      strength: off a cliff, into lava, or off an End island into the void. Near an edge she walks
      round you first so the edge is behind you. Her answer to anyone who comes close (60%).
      The tell: her wings rise high and a disc grows under her for 1 s.
      Counter: step out of its 5 blocks, keep your back away from edges, or take the push with a
      block behind you.
      Numbers: [area, close; nova, radius 5; 4 physical + knockback 3 at full strength; frames 1.0
      / 0.2 / 0.8; cooldown 10 s]

    VOID DIVE (void_dive) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: from the air she dives onto a marked spot, and may touch down and dive again
      up to three times in one flight. Her answer when someone draws a bow at her in the air (60%).
      The tell: she folds her wings in the air and a landing disc grows on the ground for 1 s.
      Counter: leave the disc. After a dive she shakes her head on the ground for 1.2 s: hit her
      then (her recovery is a weak point).
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; dive from the air, range 12, landing radius 2.5; 10 physical +
      knockback 2 at full strength (near an edge she lands on your far side to push you over it);
      frames 1.0 / 0.4 / 1.2; cooldown 10 s]

    RAISE CRYSTAL (raise_crystal) - boss phases 1, 2, 3 (her central mechanic: see THE CRYSTALS)
      What it does: an obsidian spire rises with an end crystal on top. The crystal heals her
      through a purple beam until someone breaks it. When none of hers stands she raises one much
      more readily (70%).
      The tell: she lifts a claw and a pillar of purple light climbs where the spire will rise,
      with a disc at its foot, for 1.2 s.
      Counter: break the crystal (see THE CRYSTALS); a block between it and her cuts the beam.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; one crystal within 6-14 blocks of her; cooldown 20 s]

    PERCH (perch) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she swoops onto high ground beside you, at most a block up (a step, a low wall,
      a rock), so any sword reaches her there, a soldier's too, and sits for up to 8 s, head low,
      breathing and roaring. Arrows, bolts, tridents and wind charges glance off her and catch fire,
      as off the vanilla dragon sitting on its fountain, but everything that still hits her (blades,
      spells, explosions) does 15% more. She perches more often when none of her crystals stands.
      She takes off after 8 s, or once she has taken 15% of her health, with a Wing Gust if anyone
      is within 5 blocks.
      The tell: the swoop, and a landing disc on the perch for 0.8 s.
      Counter: put the bow away and close in with a blade; leave before she takes off.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; perched up to 8 s; cooldown 18 s]

    VOID ROAR (void_roar) - boss phases 2, 3
      What it does: a roar that slows everyone around her and silences heroes (they cannot start a
      move; a player or a soldier has no moves to lose). Her answer when three or more enemies
      engage her (50%), and her favourite while perched.
      The tell: she throws her head back and a wide ring grows around her for 1 s.
      Counter: be more than 8 blocks away.
      Numbers: [area, close and mid; nova, radius 8; Slowness I 3 s + silence 1.5 s; frames 1.0 /
      0.5 / 1.0; cooldown 20 s]

    CRESCENT TAIL (crescent_tail) - boss phase 3
      What it does: she turns and her tail sweeps the half-circle where you are. Her answer to
      someone behind her (70%).
      The tell: her tail coils and marks fan out across the half-circle for 1 s.
      Counter: jump back out of its 5 blocks, or get to the side she is not sweeping.
      Numbers: [area, close; sweep 5, 180 degrees, 1 s; 6 physical + knockback 2 at full strength;
      frames 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.8; cooldown 9 s]

    END GRAVITY (end_gravity) - boss phase 3
      What it does: like a shulker's bullets, it lifts everyone around her into the air for 3 s
      (about 2.5 blocks: nobody falls far), and she often spits a fireball at the lifted (70%).
      The tell: a hum, purple motes rising from the ground, a disc growing for 1 s.
      Counter: step out of its 7 blocks; once lifted, you can still shoot back. It is not a push:
      the lift alone never drops you far.
      Numbers: [area, close and mid; nova, radius 7; 3 arcane + Levitation I 3 s (never renewed
      before you land); frames 1.0 / 0.2 / 0.8; cooldown 22 s]

  Ultimates (both there from level 1; they share one energy bar)
    BREATH OF THE END (breath_of_the_end)
      What it does: she rears up and sweeps a torrent of dragon's breath across the ground in front
      of her, leaving three clouds behind. She likes to do it from a perch.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell: a 1.6 s wind-up with a red fan 10 blocks long and 120
      degrees wide, her line ("Breathe it in. This is what the End tastes like."), a wide sound,
      and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the fight. She cannot be staggered
      during the wind-up.
      Counter: get behind her or more than 10 blocks away, then keep out of the clouds.
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.6 / 2.5 / 1.5; sweep 10, 120 degrees, 2.5 s: 6 arcane each time
      it passes over you; three clouds of radius 3, 7 blocks apart, for 8 s, 2 arcane each second
      (standing where two touch still hurts like one)]

    FALL OF THE OUTER ISLES (outer_isles_fall)
      What it does: from the air she climbs high and falls on you like a meteor, leaving a cloud.
      The tell: the full ultimate tell ("From the outer isles, I fall!") and a red landing disc of
      radius 4 that grows for 1.8 s as she climbs.
      Counter: leave the disc; hit her during her 1.6 s recovery on the ground.
      Numbers: [ultimate, only in a flight; frames 1.8 / 0.4 / 1.6; dive, landing radius 4: 22
      arcane + knockback 3 up 1 at full strength; a cloud of radius 4 for 6 s, 2 arcane each
      second]

    Her ultimate energy and charge rate are set when she is built; the two are at least 30 s apart.

  Passives
    THE SPIRES ANSWER: the beam of her crystals (see THE CRYSTALS).
    WRATH OF THE SPIRES: whoever breaks one of her crystals glows for 6 s and becomes her target
    (the vanilla dragon turns on whoever breaks a crystal).

THE CRYSTALS
- What: end crystals (they look like vanilla's) on obsidian spires with a bedrock cap. They are
  hers, not vanilla's: they never blow up the ground, and they never heal or respawn the vanilla
  dragon. The spire is solid (you cannot walk through it) but it is not a block (you cannot mine it
  or build against it).
- Where: within 6-14 blocks of her, on the highest ground there whose foot you can walk to (she
  will raise them on your walls), never over lava, water or the void, at least 4 blocks apart, and
  never in a gateway, a door or a corridor narrower than 3 blocks (a spire never shuts you in or
  out). A spire crumbles when its crystal breaks or fades.
- How high: in phase 1 the crystal sits on a 1-block spire, 1 to 3 blocks up: any sword reaches it
  from the ground, a soldier's too, and an arrow always does. From phase 2 the spires are 4-6
  blocks tall, the crystal 5-7 up: shoot it, pillar up 2-4 blocks beside the spire, or leap, climb
  or fly there.
- Caged: from phase 2 every second crystal is caged in iron bars. The cage stops every projectile
  and breaks after 3 hits by hand; then the crystal breaks to one more.
- How many: at most 2 standing in phase 1 and 3 later; at most 8 in one fight; each fades after
  60 s.
- Breaking one: an open crystal breaks to any hit at all, even a snowball or an egg (as vanilla's).
  Anyone fighting her can break them: you, a soldier, a hero, even a monster; her own side cannot.
  It bursts in purple glass: everyone of her enemies within 3 blocks takes 4 arcane and a push of
  2 at full strength (step back after you break it by hand: on a pillar, the push can throw you
  off it). The burst breaks no block.
- The beam: every second, the nearest of her crystals within 24 blocks with a clear line to her
  heals her 0.75% of her maximum health (one crystal at a time, as vanilla's nearest crystal), and
  you can see the purple beam. A block or a solid body in the line cuts it: building a wall between
  them is fair play. It heals only her, and never more than 35% of her health in one fight: after
  that her crystals go dark and she raises no more, so they can slow a fight, never make her
  unkillable.
- Beam snap: breaking the crystal that is beaming her at that moment hurts her 6% of her maximum
  health and fills 40 of her poise (vanilla's crystal hurts the dragon 10). "Every spire is my
  heartbeat."
- NPCs know all this: soldiers' archers shoot the open crystals first (the beaming one first), and
  swordsmen hit the ones they can reach and pillar up to the caged ones as a player would; heroes
  reach them with their jumps, climbs and wings. While she is perched, archers with nothing else to
  shoot draw the sword they carry and join in. Nothing here depends on whether the breaker is a
  player.

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: she is a boss in every fight, whether she fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with her until 10 s after the last one leaves.
  Her first fight against people each in-game day opens with her intro, and each player within 32
  blocks gets a title (her name, title and faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see her
  boss bar whenever she fights anyone: a star, "The Ender Dragon · Empress of the End", three phase
  pips, a lightning mark when an ultimate is ready, and a square for each artifact she wears. Her
  bar is meant to be pink, the vanilla dragon's.
- Health: her base health 34 x4.0 (the legendary scale; she never leads, so no leader bonus) x1.0
  for a vanguard [health scale 400], always: it never changes with who or how many fight her.
  Level 1 / 10 / 20: 136 / 165 / 197. At level 10 a player with an iron sword needs about 36 s of
  landed hits while none of her crystals stands, and at most about 48 s however long they beam her
  (1.24 health a second at level 10, at most 35% of her health in a fight): break them or the fight
  lasts up to a third longer. Attackers add up: six iron soldiers who reach her need about 7 s of
  blows (while she is on the ground or perched). No single hit, from
  anyone, takes more than 12% of her health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage.
  Damage with no attacker (lava, fire, drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 140 (the vanguard's). Every hit fills her poise bar, whoever lands it [damage x weapon:
  sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], a
  parry adds 25 and a beam snap 40. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage.
  Full poise staggers her: 2.5 s dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum
  grows 20%. No stagger during her intro, a phase change or her ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too. During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15%
  more again from behind.
- In the air (every phase): she takes off in 0.6 s with a heavy wingbeat, circles you 8-12 blocks
  out and 4-6 up (never more than 6 above the ground or the highest enemy standing on a block),
  spits fireballs (at most three a flight) and dives (up to three touch-and-go), or calls the Fall
  of the Outer Isles; she stays at most 8 s, then lands for at least 6 s (4 s in phase 3); at most
  40% of any 30 s in the air and at most 4 s at a time out of everyone's melee reach. While one of
  her crystals stands she keeps inside its beam's 24 blocks. Arrows bring her down: five arrows,
  from you or an NPC archer, or Slowness II, and she falls, lies 2 s taking 20% more damage, and
  cannot take off for 12 s.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100%: Ender Fireball, Dragon Breath, Wing Gust, Void Dive, Raise Crystal (open
      crystals, at most 2), Perch.
    Phase 2, from 60%: the spires rise (taller spires, every second crystal caged, up to 3), Void
      Roar. "Rise, spires! Let them climb if they dare."
    Phase 3, from 25%: Crescent Tail, End Gravity; her clouds grow a block wider and last 2 s
      longer, and she moves 10% faster. "Then taste the breath of the End itself."
  Each phase change takes 1.5 s, on the ground: she takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered
  (a glint shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes her enemies within 4 blocks
  back at full strength (no damage; her own side is not pushed), she says the line, her look
  changes, and the bar marks the pip.
- Weak points:
  - Beam snap: break the crystal that is beaming her (above).
  - Explosions: blasts from anyone (TNT, a creeper, a vanilla end crystal, a bed or a respawn
    anchor that explodes, firework stars) hurt her 25% more and fill her poise twice as fast,
    because players blow the vanilla dragon up with beds. "You brought a BED?" (Another hero's
    blasts, the Wither's included, do not count: they only break blocks.)
  - Recovery: after a dive and after her breath she shakes her head; the punish bonus doubles
    [+30% during her recovery, +45% from behind].
  - She has no elemental weakness.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; she reads an NPC the same way):
  - You draw a bow while she is in the air -> Void Dive (60%, at most every 8 s).
  - You come close -> Wing Gust (60%).
  - You are behind her -> Crescent Tail (phase 3, 70%).
  - You raise a shield -> Dragon Breath (50%: arcane passes shields).
  - You eat -> Ender Fireball (60%).
  - None of her crystals stands -> Raise Crystal (70%, at most every 15 s).
  - Three or more enemies engage her -> Void Roar (from phase 2, 50%).
  - You are above her -> Ender Fireball (50%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever she fights: +15% damage, shorter waits (x0.7), poise
  maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and line ("Enough! I have outlived empires of dust!").
  All her damage bonuses together never exceed +25%. She also enrages at once when the heart of a
  base she defends is attacked (she guards it as she guarded her fountain).
- Intro: she lands with her wings spread, folds them and tilts her head. "You stand on my island.
  Every island is mine."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press her.
- When she is losing: she fights to the death [aggression 60 + honour 75 - caution 45 = 90; her
  file also says last_stand outright]: the vanilla dragon never flees. At 8% of her health [a third
  of her retreat_below 18, raised to the floor of 8] she has her last-stand beat once per fight:
  "Flee? I am the End. There is nowhere past me to run to." At zero health she dies like anyone
  (in the air she falls); beams of white-purple light burst from her for 3 s, as from the vanilla
  dragon, and the bar shows "fallen". "The egg remembers... I will hatch again."
- Rewards: only when she dies, to whoever killed her (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt her in her last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear, a hero crest
  trophy, and every artifact she wears drops where she fell, her Spirecore included.
- Revival: 10 minutes after her death, at her home base. When she dies a dragon egg appears beside
  the reporting station (only a picture: nobody can take it); ten minutes later it cracks and she
  hatches from it. The egg is the look of the usual return, not a power: the time is the rule's.
  Her own revival_rite talent does not shorten her own return, though another hero's would.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Break the crystals first: an arrow, a snowball or an egg breaks an open one; break the one
   beaming her (follow the purple line) and she takes a hit and loses poise. Block the beam with a
   wall if you cannot reach the crystal.
2. From phase 2 the caged ones need hands: pillar up beside the spire (2-4 blocks), three hits on
   the cage, one on the crystal, and step back from the burst.
3. Shoot her down when she circles: five arrows put her on the ground for 12 s.
4. When she perches, put the bow away (arrows glance off) and get your blade on her: she perches
   at most a block up, takes 15% more from every hit there, and leaves after 15% of her health.
5. Stay out of the clouds, and out of the fan when she rears: arcane goes through shields and
   armour.
6. Hit her after every dive: she is open for 1.2 s, doubly so.
7. Explosions hurt her more. Yes, beds too.
8. Fear the edge. Her Wing Gust, her tail and her dives throw you at full strength, and she walks
   round you to throw you off a cliff, into lava, or off the island into the void. Fight her in
   the middle of the ground, or carry a water bucket for the fall.

MOVEMENT
Mobility trait: flyer (ceiling III), on her own dragon wings. She has every feature from level 1;
the numbers grow from 60% at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility never changes
a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC has is the one
exception).
- Take-off: 0.6 s (the tell: her wings spread with a heavy beat; she needs 4 blocks of headroom, so
  roofs, tunnels and dense trees make her walk). Landing 0.4 s, never on farmland, lava, fire or
  powder snow.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Stamina (seconds of powered flight)     36          / 47.4        / 60
    Cruise speed (blocks per second)        6.5         / 7.2         / 8.0
    Climb rate (blocks per second)          2.4         / 3.2         / 4.0
    Glide: sink, forward (b/s)              3.3, 7.7    / 2.5, 8.8    / 2.0, 10.0
    Glide time (s), gusts                   12, 2       / 15.8, 2     / 20, 2
    Take-off needs (stamina)                33%         / 25%         / 20%
    Stamina back on the ground (per s)      1.8         / 2.4         / 3.0
- Out of fights: she flies her patrols over her base's walls and rests on its highest point; in a
  thunderstorm she walks.
- In fights: the flights, circling and perching described above, the same against a player and an
  NPC. If you tower up a pillar she flies up to you.
- Raids: a vanguard, she leads her base's raids by her own choice [raids: leads] (never drafted like
  a soldier). She circles the target first to scout it, flies over the wall when she has a way out,
  hunts the defending hero inside (or goes for the heart with her party), and in the yard perches
  a block above the defenders to breathe on them. Defending, she meets airborne intruders over her
  walls.
- Terrain: see TERRAIN AND BLOCKS.
- She does not escape (she fights to the death).

TERRAIN AND BLOCKS
(Kit version 3. Your answers of 2026-11-18: "its fine for heroes abilities to push someone of a
cliff or to lava", and nobody needs to be "civil" in battle.)
- Her pushes land at full strength wherever they send you: into lava or fire, over a cliff, into
  the void. They are Wing Gust (knockback 3, her strongest), Void Dive (2), Crescent Tail (2), the
  Fall of the Outer Isles (3 and up 1), her phase change's shockwave, and the burst of a crystal you
  break (2). Each point of knockback throws you about 2 blocks on flat ground: Wing Gust about 6.
  Soldiers and other heroes get exactly the same. Her own side is never pushed.
- A raised shield stops a move's damage, not its push (as in vanilla, where a blocked blow still
  knocks you back). What helps: leaving the ground mark during the wind-up, fighting her more than
  6 blocks from a rim, knockback resistance (netherite armour cuts a push by 40%), a water bucket
  for a fall, Fire Resistance for lava, and an ender pearl to get back from a ledge.
- She does it on purpose, as the vanilla dragon's wings throw players off its island. When you
  stand near lava, a drop or an island's rim she walks round to your far side before Wing Gust or
  her tail, and her dives land on your far side so the push sends you over. The walk round you and
  the landing disc are the tells: step away from the edge.
- Her own body never goes into a hazard: she never lands in lava, fire or the void.
- A death there is her kill, in vanilla's words: "... was doomed to fall by The Ender Dragon",
  "... didn't want to live in the same world as The Ender Dragon" (the void). In lava your dropped
  items burn.
- Against her: she flies, so a drop does not hurt her, but lava and fire do, in full [fire
  resistance 40]; she is hard to push [knockback resistance 30, the vanguard's].
- Blocks: none of her moves is an explosion, and none breaks a block (her fireballs and breath
  leave clouds, as vanilla's do; her dives break nothing). A crystal's burst breaks no block
  either.
- She does not smash through blocks. The vanilla dragon removes every block it flies through
  (except obsidian, end stone, iron bars and the like); she flies round them, as a two-block hero
  can, so your walls, pillars and bridges stay whole. To reach someone she cannot get to, she digs
  at a player's pace after 10 s, as every hero does (her claws count as an iron pickaxe). On a raid
  she opens the gate from inside, or uses TNT if she carries it, like anyone.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: guard (when she is not fighting). She patrols from the air and perches on the base's highest
  point. As a guard her proficiency answers alarms 8 / 12 / 16 blocks farther instead of working
  faster, with a bigger backpack (21 / 24 / 27 slots), less wear and hunger, more work XP.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is, not
  on a raid; at home each counts one tier higher):
  - revival_rite (a later milestone): fallen heroes of her faction return 20 / 30 / 40% sooner (50%
    at her best at home), never under 30 s, and rise with 4 / 6 / 8 absorption hearts for 2
    minutes. The egg that hatches her taught her how.
  - lookout: anyone in sight within 32 / 40 / 48 blocks raises the alarm (56 at her best); from 6
    blocks up or more (perched on a tower, or flying) her own sight reaches 16 blocks farther.
  - cartographer: the base's scouts range 16 / 32 / 48 blocks farther, and each report marks the
    sectors around it explored: she has seen the land from above.

HOW SHE JOINS A FACTION: THE TOKEN
- The first time the Ender Dragon dies in your world (since the mod was added), whoever kills it,
  a chest named "The Dragon's Hoard" appears beside the exit portal when it forms, on solid end
  stone and never over the void. Inside lies the Ender Dragon Hero Token. A respawned dragon gives
  no second chest.
- The token glows, never despawns, and shrugs off fire, lava, explosions and cactus. If it falls
  into the void, sits in lava for 30 s, or is destroyed, a new one appears in the chest 2 minutes
  later. There is only ever one; any copy is a "Hollow Ender Dragon Token" that does nothing. It
  cannot go into a shulker box or a bundle, an ender chest hands it back when you close it, and a
  creative player cannot hold it. A copied token stops working the moment the real one is used.
- Bring it home through the portal and inside the walls of one of your faction's bases (you, or any
  NPC or hero of your faction, carrying it): it is used at once. In a 10 s show at the reporting
  station a dragon egg settles, purple beams pour into it, it cracks, and she unfolds her wings and
  joins your faction at level 1, wearing her Spirecore. A token that lies inside a base for 5 s
  joins that base's faction.
- Whoever carries it drops it when they die, even with keepInventory on. Carriers trail purple
  particles; any NPC that sees a carrier recognises it, and enemies go for the carrier first.
- The NPC factions want it too: they learn of it from their own witnesses and scouts (the chest's
  pale beam shows from 64 blocks), send expeditions, intercept carriers they saw, and escort their
  own; your faction's NPCs do the same for you.
- If her faction ever loses its last base, she folds into an egg and turns back into her token
  where she stands (or where its last heart fell, if she was dead), keeping her level. (Your answer
  of 2026-11-18: "token".) Her artifacts drop beside it. She has no personal name: she is The Ender
  Dragon, Empress of the End.

NOT A LEADER
She is never drafted as a faction leader and never founds a faction: she graces the faction that
carried her token. She does not count against the number of heroes a faction may have, and one
faction may hold both boss heroes.

HER ARTIFACT: SPIRECORE
A palm-sized end crystal caged in obsidian and iron bars, worn on a chain at her collarbone (you can
see it on her); it turns slowly and hums. It cannot be crafted: it comes with her the first time she
joins, and it drops where she dies, for anyone to take. Only one exists; if it is destroyed it comes
back to her, as new.
- Slots: she joins at level 1, when a hero has one artifact slot. Spirecore fills it. She gets a
  second slot at level 5, and one more every five levels after that (five from level 20).
- Fixed strength: it gives the same numbers on every hero, at every level.
- On any hero: health +10%, ability power +6%, energy gain +8%, flight stamina +10%, and Spire Mend:
  once per fight, when it drops under 40% health, a shield of 4 for 8 s and Regeneration I for 6 s.
- On the Ender Dragon herself (her bond): also health +5%, recharge +5%, ability power +6%; Heart of
  the Spires (Spire Mend that also cuts her running cooldowns by 10 s, never under half, so her
  next crystal comes sooner); Raise Crystal every 16 s instead of 20; Ender Fireball's splash 3
  blocks wide instead of 2. A faster Raise Crystal does not let her crystals heal past their 35%.
- It can be upgraded (your idea of tinkerers, and of artifacts that need research). It is a built
  thing, a crystal in a cage, so an artificer can re-cage it at the Artificer's Bench, like a rare
  find, once the faction has the Artifice 2 research (so not before the faction labs exist):
    upgrade II    4 lapis, a diamond and an end crystal; 10 levels; 2 minutes
    upgrade III   4 lapis, a netherite scrap and 2 end crystals; 15 levels; 3 minutes
  At III it gives health +15%, ability power +9%, energy gain +12%, stamina +15%, and Spire Mend's
  shield is 4.8. Her bond stays the same. The upgrade stays on it whoever holds it.
- It never grows: a crystal stays what it is.
- Her choice: nothing forces it on her. She chooses it, and always keeps it: it is worth far more
  to her than to anyone (only she gets the bond). She never swaps it out or gives it away; she
  takes it off only to have it upgraded.
  - If a hero of her own faction picks it up after she dies, it counts as lent by her. When she
    hatches again she asks for it. An honourable hero gives it back at once; a greedy one may keep
    it for a while, and she keeps asking.
  - If an enemy hero wears it, she knows it when she sees it: that hero becomes her first target
    in any fight.

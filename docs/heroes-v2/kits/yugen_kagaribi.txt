Yugen Kagaribi - "The Lantern Fox"
Male | fox | marksman | arcane | schemer | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 2, 2026-11-13: he fights the same against players
and NPCs, and he never yields: see "When he is losing"). All numbers are first values, tuned after
play.
====================================================================================================

WHO HE IS
A soft-spoken fox merchant-lord who never raises his voice and always has a contract ready. His
foxfire burns blue and cold, and you are never quite sure which of him is real. In a one-on-one
fight he is a zoner: he keeps you out with projectiles, and you win by closing the gap. Out of
battle he trades; as a faction leader he is the Master of the Veiled Lantern.

LOOK
- Body: male, slim build, normal size [scale 100%].
- Hair: long silver hair, worn half up [#D8DCE6].
- Fox parts: tall silver fox ears with black tips; three fluffy tails (six in phase 2, nine in phase
  3).
- Outfit: a layered plum court robe with a silver crest, a white fox mask with red marks worn on the
  side of his head, prayer beads, court shoes.
- Weapon: a closed folding fan in his right hand, and three small paper lanterns orbiting him.
- Colours: skin [#F4DCCB], pale blue eyes [#8FB7FF], primary plum [#6B2148], secondary silver
  [#D8DCE6], accent foxfire blue [#5AA9FF], trim red [#B23A48], glow light blue [#7FC8FF].
- Face: narrow, half-lidded eyes, light lashes [lashes 1], thin arched brows, a soft mouth, red
  eye-liner flicks. He blinks slowly and slyly, every 4 to 7 s [blink calm].
- Idle quirks: a laugh behind his sleeve, a tail-fan flick, a lantern spin.
- Glow: his lanterns always glow; in phase 3 his tail tips burn with foxfire and his eyes glow.
- Phase looks: phase 2, six tails; phase 3, nine tails, foxfire tail tips and glowing eyes.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of his attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: his accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, his face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where he stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3 or
enraged. Each move's damage is capped the same whoever it hits, a player, a soldier or a hero [16
for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in level-1 health points]: there is no extra damage to players and
no protection from a one-hit kill; the wind-up is the warning everyone gets. Any number of heroes
may gang up on one target, and any number of enemies on him.
His fighting: focus (foxfire lanterns and a folding fan, thrown); duel style zoner. He keeps about
10 blocks away [keep_distance 10] and backs off like an archer when you come within 5; he goes for
the weakest enemy first (the schemer focus). His element, arcane, goes through armour and shields
[the arcane damage type bypasses armour and shields]. Cooldowns and how often he picks each move are
set when he is built.

  Basic attack
    Thrown foxfire: a blue flame (drawn as blaze powder with soul-fire particles) that flies
    straight, out to 14 blocks, for 4 arcane damage every 1.5 s.

  Abilities
    FOXFIRE VOLLEY (foxfire_volley) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: his tails flare and five foxfire flames fly out in a fan, out to 16 blocks.
      The tell: the tails flare for 0.5 s (a light move: the animation only).
      Counter: move sideways out of the fan, or put a block between you; a shield does not stop
      arcane damage.
      Numbers: [light, mid and far; volley of 5 (10 degrees apart), range 16; 4 arcane each; frames
      0.5 / 0.3 / 0.4]. It often follows Vanish (70%), and it is his answer to a drawn bow (50%).

    LANTERN RING (lantern_ring) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a ring of lantern fire around him, 4 blocks across from his feet, that pulses
      once a second for 3 s; each pulse hurts and slows everyone inside. He prefers it when you are
      close.
      The tell: a disc grows on the ground around him during the 1 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: step more than 4 blocks away from him before the first pulse, and stay out for 3 s.
      Numbers: [area, close; zone at himself, radius 4, delay 1.0 s (whether the delay runs inside
      the wind-up or after it is set when he is built), lasts 3 s, a pulse every 1 s; 3 arcane per
      pulse + Slowness I 2 s; frames 1.0 / 3.0 / 0.8]

    FAN CUT (fan_cut) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: two quick strikes with his folding fan at close range that knock you back. When
      it hits, 80% of the time he follows with Vanish. It is his answer when you get behind him
      (60%).
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: keep out of close range (a shield does not stop arcane damage); the knockback is what
      opens the distance he wants.
      Numbers: [light, close; combo of 2 strikes; 60% + knockback 2 (whether per strike or in total
      is set when he is built); followup Vanish 80%; frames 0.4 / 0.3 / 0.3]

    VANISH (vanish) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: he turns invisible for 2 s and hops 6 blocks back. 70% of the time he follows
      with Foxfire Volley. It is his answer when you get close (70%). His signature string: Fan Cut,
      then Vanish, then Foxfire Volley.
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: follow the hop (6 blocks straight back), and expect the volley; the punish window
      comes after the last move of the string.
      Numbers: [light, close; on himself; Invisibility 2 s + hop back 6; followup Foxfire Volley
      70%; frames 0.4 / 0.1 / 0.3]

    MIRAGE (mirage) - boss phases 2, 3 (phase 2's new mechanic)
      What it does: two decoys of him appear for 10 s and copy his casts, but their foxfire does no
      damage. Each decoy copies his actions a tenth of a second late [2 ticks], walks a short path,
      has 1 health and pops in a puff of smoke at any hit or after 10 s.
      The tell: a 0.8 s wind-up with a sound; the shape of its ground mark is set when he is built.
      Counter: find the real one: only he casts a shadow and only he makes footstep sounds. One hit
      pops a decoy.
      Numbers: [heavy, any range; on himself, 2 decoys for 10 s (at most 3 decoys per hero); frames
      0.8 / 0.2 / 0.8]

    FOXFIRE RAIN (foxfire_rain) - boss phase 3 only (phase 3's new mechanic)
      What it does: 8 foxfire impacts rain down within 5 blocks around you over 2 s; each one hits
      everyone within 1 to 2 blocks of where it lands.
      The tell: a 1 s wind-up with a sound; every impact gets its own growing ground mark and lands
      0.9 s after it.
      Counter: keep moving off the marks as they appear.
      Numbers: [area, mid and far; rain of 8, radius 5, over 2 s, impact delay 0.9 s; 5 arcane per
      impact; frames 1.0 / 2.0 / 0.8. Whether he skips it under a roof (the design needs open sky
      for rain outdoors) is set when he is built]

  Ultimate
    NINEFOLD LANTERN (ninefold_lantern)
      What it does: his lanterns gather into a ring behind him, then he fires 9 foxfire flames one
      after another, 0.2 s apart, each aimed at you as it fires; each one hurts and puts a Bewitched
      mark on you (see Passives).
      The tell: a 1.6 s wind-up as the lanterns gather, a spoken line, a wide sound, a red ground
      mark, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players near the fight. He cannot be staggered
      during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when his ultimate is ready.
      Counter: get a block between you and him before the flames start, or keep moving sideways;
      three marks on you mean a slow (see Passives).
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.6 / 1.8 / 1.5; barrage of 9, 0.2 s apart, 3 arcane each + mark
      bewitched. Set when he is built: his ultimate line, its energy and charge rate, the minimum
      gap between two ultimates (20-60 s)]

  Passives
    CONTRACT (contract): when you hit him in melee, 25% of the time he puts a Bewitched mark on you.
    BEWITCHED (bewitched_full): when someone carries 3 of his Bewitched marks, they are stunned for
    1 s, a player as surely as an NPC: for that second a player cannot walk, jump, attack or use
    items (the camera still turns; v1's milder Slowness for players is gone, the same rule for
    everyone). At most once every 12 s. How long a mark lasts and how many can stack are set when he
    is built.

BOSS FIGHT
- One mode, against anyone: he is a boss in every fight, whether he fights you, NPC soldiers,
  another hero or monsters, with the same health, poise, phases, moves, reactions, weak points and
  enrage. A fight runs from the first enemy engaged with him (it hurt him in the last 15 s, or is
  his target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players are never enemies) until 10 s after
  the last one leaves. His first fight against people (a player, a soldier or a hero) each in-game
  day opens with his intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which he takes 50% less damage and cannot be staggered,
  and his intro line; each player within 32 blocks gets a title on screen (his name, title and
  faction) once that day. Players within 32 blocks see a boss bar whenever he fights anyone (a raid
  between two NPC factions included): a star, his name and title, his phase pips and a lightning
  mark when his ultimate is ready. (His bar title is set when he is built.)
- Health: his base health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader: every leader
  gets +50], x1.3 for a marksman, always: it never changes with who or how many fight him. At level
  10 that is 130 health (148 as a leader): about 24 s (27 s) of landed hits for a player with an
  iron sword at 1.6 swings a second, half of them landing, his iron weakness counted; an iron-sword
  soldier needs about a fifth longer, and attackers add up (six soldiers who all reach him need
  about a sixth of one soldier's time). A whole duel lasts two to three times its hitting time once
  you dodge his wind-ups. No single hit, from anyone (a player, a soldier, a hero, a monster, their
  arrows), takes more than 12% of his health [max_hit_share 12]; the excess becomes poise damage.
  Damage with no attacker (lava, fire, drowning) is not capped.
- Poise: 70 (the marksman value, the lowest there is). Every hit fills his poise bar, whoever lands
  it [damage x weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6,
  crossbow bolt 0.8], and a parry (a shield, yours or a soldier's, raised in the last 6 ticks before
  a heavy move lands) adds 25. The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full
  poise staggers him: 2.5 s kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its
  maximum grows 20% for the rest of the fight. No stagger during his intro, a phase change or his
  ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves he waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  A fight opens with the phase 1 wait too, so no move reaches you sooner than that wait plus its
  wind-up (at least 1.9 s after the fight starts, or after the intro).
  During any recovery he takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where he faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Foxfire Volley, Lantern Ring, Fan Cut, Vanish. Three tails.
    Phase 2, from 60%: adds Mirage (the decoys); his tails go from 3 to 6.
      "Which of me is lying? All of us, perhaps."
    Phase 3, from 25%: adds Foxfire Rain; nine tails, glowing eyes and tail tips.
      "Enough games. Let the lanterns speak."
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (his exact length is set when he is built): he takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; he is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), he says the line, his
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Iron: +15% damage from iron weapons (an iron sword, axe or other iron tool in your main hand).
    Hint line: "Iron... how crude."
  - Recovery: the punish bonus doubles during his recoveries [+30% instead of +15%].
- How he reads you (each answer replaces his next pick; their cooldowns are set when he is built).
  He reads an NPC the same way: a soldier raising its shield, an archer drawing, a soldier eating
  from its food bar draw the same answers at the same chances:
  - You come close -> Vanish (70%).
  - You draw a bow -> Foxfire Volley (50%).
  - You get behind him -> Fan Cut (60%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of fighting, whoever he fights (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter waits
  between moves (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All his
  damage bonuses together never exceed +25%.
- Intro: a slow bow with the fan. "A customer? How delightful. The price today is your patience."
- Ganging up: there are no turns. Any number of heroes may press the same enemy at once, and any
  number of enemies may press him; nothing grows or shrinks with the crowd on either side.
- When he is losing: he escapes, and early [his schemer traits: aggression 45 + honour 20 - caution
  70 = -5, under 65: a schemer's life is his best asset]. Under 15% of his health [flee_at 15%: a
  third of his retreat_below 44.5%, kept inside his last phase], in any fight, from a player,
  soldiers, another hero or monsters alike, he breaks off for good. His raid falling back while he
  is in or near the target, or the heart of the base he defends falling, sends him off too. Nothing
  holds him anywhere [stands_for: none, loyalty 30]: he leaves a raid, an ally and even his own
  capital. Escaping, he starts no moves and runs with a fox's turn of speed (an escape counts as
  travel, so his travel speed and sprint burst are back on), and he may put a gap you cannot cross
  between you. He stays a boss while he runs: the 12% cap, his poise and his weak points still hold,
  so a stagger stops him for 2.5 s where he is, and he has three or four sword blows of health left.
  Cornered with no way out for 3 s, he fights back, then runs again as soon as a way opens. His
  refuge is his home base, never the base he flees (from a fight at his own base: another base of
  his faction, or the wild until no one follows); the escape ends inside its walls or once no
  pursuer has been within 48 blocks for 10 s. Then he stays away 5 minutes and until he is above 50%
  health, and does not go back for those he fled. The boss bar shows "fled" for 3 s and goes, and
  players within 32 blocks read "Yugen Kagaribi fled". "Mm. A poor bargain. Let us call the
  account... deferred." He is the slice's escape test and escape session.
  - At most one escape in 10 minutes: follow him home or meet him again soon, and inside that window
    he fights to the death.
  - At zero health he dies like anyone: there is no kneeling and nothing stops a killing blow. The
    bar shows "fallen" for 3 s. "So the bill comes due after all. How novel."
- Rewards: only when he dies, to whoever killed him (the side of the last player, NPC or hero that
  hurt him in his last 15 s): XP orbs (7 x his level, at most 100), his non-kit gear (his kit never
  drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per winning side per in-game day). Soldiers of a
  winning faction pick them up and carry them home. An escape drops nothing and gives nothing.
- Revival: like every hero he comes back 10 minutes after his death, whoever killed him
  [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at his home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing
  base); his kit items vanish at death and return with him. He has no death ability of his own. A
  hero of his faction with the revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring him back 20 / 30 / 40%
  sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HIM
1. Fight him with iron: an iron sword or axe deals 15% more. An axe or a mace also fills his small
   poise bar (70) fast (x1.6 and x2.0), and a stagger gives you 2.5 s at +25% damage.
2. Close the gap, but expect his answer: come close and he vanishes 70% of the time, hops 6 blocks
   back and usually throws a Foxfire Volley. The punish window is at the end of that string, and his
   recovery weak point doubles your punish bonus there. Getting behind him draws a Fan Cut (60%).
3. Do not turtle: arcane goes through armour and shields. Step off the growing marks instead: the
   Lantern Ring disc when you are close (radius 4; it slows you), and in phase 3 each Foxfire Rain
   impact, which lands 0.9 s after its mark appears.
4. Find the real one (phases 2 and 3): only he casts a shadow and makes footsteps, the decoys act a
   split second late, one hit pops a decoy, and decoy foxfire does no damage.
5. Watch your Bewitched marks: each melee hit you land has a 25% chance to mark you, and every flame
   of his ultimate marks you; at 3 marks you are slowed. Mix in ranged hits, and get a block between
   you and him when his ultimate starts.
6. Keep a bow, crossbow or trident ready for the end: he runs early, at 15%, with his runner speed
  back on. Shoot rather than chase: three or four hits finish him, and filling his small poise bar
  staggers him for 2.5 s where he stands. If he gets away he is at his home base, hurt, for the next
  5 minutes, and caught again within 10 minutes he does not run a second time.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: runner (ceiling III) and leaper (ceiling I). He has every feature from level 1; the
numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility never
changes a move's damage, range or timing, and he never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC has is
the one exception).
- From level 1: walking up full blocks without jumping [step 1.0]; a sprint burst for 3 s on a clear
  straight (8 or more blocks with no turn and nothing within 2 blocks ahead); faster, more efficient
  travel that costs less hunger; a 1.8-block jump (the leaper floor), so he hops field fences.
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Travel speed                            +12% / +16% / +20%
    Movement efficiency                     0.60 / 0.79 / 1.00
      (1.00 = soul sand, honey and other slow blocks do not slow him at all)
    Travel hunger                           -30% / -39% / -50%
    Sprint burst                            +24% every 25 s / +32% every 19 s / +40% every 15 s
    Jump height (blocks)                    1.80 / 1.80 / 1.80 (the leaper floor)
    Longest gap he jumps (blocks)           3    / 3    / 5
    Safe fall (blocks before damage)        4.8  / 5.4  / 6.0
- Out of fights: a 300-block village trip takes about a fifth less time at full power (speed, and no
  fence detours). He takes streams, gaps, fences and small ledges without detours or bridges, and
  his travel routes give hostile bases a wide berth.
- In fights (the same against a player and an NPC): his travel bonuses (speed, sprint) are off in
  every fight. Leaping lets him hop onto fences and 1.5-block ledges while keeping his distance;
  from ground his enemies cannot reach he comes back within 4 s. A jump starts with a 0.3 s crouch
  (the tell).
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader he joins his base's raids like a soldier [raids: joins, the
  marksman default]; as a leader he stays home (below). His jumps reach rises of 1.5 blocks, not
  today's 5-block base walls, so he goes in by the gate with the soldiers (a jump across from higher
  ground onto a lower or broken wall is the exception). Inside, two of the design's goals fit him:
  the storehouse first (greed 70: a greedy hero takes one backpack of loot from a still-standing
  base and carries it home) and clearing the defending archers (marksman and zoner heroes); which
  one he takes is set when he is built. A goal ends after 60 s without progress. The defenders
  answer him as any intruding hero (the alarm, the two nearest free guards and any hero at home).
- Escaping (under 15%, see "When he is losing"): an escape counts as travel, so his speed and sprint
  come back on, and he may leave a gap you cannot cross between you.
- Signature animation: his sprint is a fox lope.

WORK AND TALENTS

  base knows a village.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18; his specialty gives 30 / 33 / 36

  less hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 / +30%]. The job-by-job table
  gives trading no work-speed or reach part.

  (tier III equals the vanilla Hero of the Village I discount; with the Haggler bonus never more
  than 40% off), a backpack of 30 / 33 / 36 slots, and one more trip every 3 / 2 / 1 days.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where he is, inside
  the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never the sum;
  the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of his faction at tier I while he
  leads):
  - merchant_prince* (realm): the base's traders travel more often [trip interval x0.8 / x0.65 /

  - spymaster: the base's scout reports on rival bases carry their garrison counts and whether their

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Master of the Veiled Lantern.
- Faction names: "Veiled Lantern Exchange" (the first unused name is taken), then "The Blue Lantern
  Company". Short name: "Veiled Lantern". Motto: "Every promise has a price."
- Colours: primary plum [#6B2148], secondary silver [#D8DCE6], accent foxfire blue [#5AA9FF]. They
  show in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, his sash and his NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: purple, with a light blue circle in the middle and a row of white triangles along the top
  [base purple; circle light_blue; triangles_top white] (the beast-kin emblem). Real banners hang at
  the station and the gates, and members get a banner item. Purple is set by hand because the
  nearest dye to plum would be black. His faction's wool, carpets and beds are purple, with light
  blue as the second dye.
- Base style: cherry_court, a Court: cherry plank walls, stripped cherry log frames, birch plank
  floors, deepslate tile roofs and smooth stone round the edge; signs read like "Veiled Lantern
  Court". Culture: east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: trade (traders and haulers are
  hired ahead of other civilians) and lore (the enchanter).
- His day (court duty): trading is a travelling job, which a leader never takes, so he patrols
  between the station, the gates and the heart and stands at the station with the others; his
  talents still work at the base. A short stop at the station at dawn, the heart at dusk, a bed in
  the largest house at night, woken by any alarm. He answers every alarm and guards the heart in a
  defence, says his founding line when the faction starts, and announces raids, attacks on his base,
  a lost base, a fallen hero and recruit milestones in his own voice (his own lines for these are
  not written yet; his temperament's fallback lines fill in).
- If he dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until he returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more). He stays home from raids, so no raid waits on him. If he escapes (he
  would, even from his capital), his faction hears his escape line, and for 5 minutes and until he
  is above half health he counts in no base's defence but his refuge's; there is no vengeance raid,
  since nobody died. If his faction's last base falls he becomes an exiled free hero, never a leader
  again in that campaign.
- How his personality steers the faction. His traits (out of 100): aggression 45, honour 20,
  ambition 85, caution 70, greed 70, sociability 60, loyalty 30, volatility 25. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets. They equal the design's worked
  example for a schemer (raid interval, attack odds, garrison, soldier cap, ranged share), except
  the trader threshold: 13 there, 12 for him (sociability 60 against 45).
  - Raids less often: about every 34 minutes, or 1.7 in-game days [40680 ticks, interval x1.13;
    neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid not before 0.94 intervals [1].
  - Attacks only with a clear edge: attack odds needed 0.807 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.31 of
    the interval [0.25], at odds 1.69 [1.5].
  - He stays home from raids (caution 70 or more).
  - A raid party falls back after losing 38.5% [50%].
  - Keeps 40% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 3 home defenders and 3 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 13 [12]; ranged share 0.47 [0.40].
  - Trader hired early, at population 12 [14]; trader trips x0.92 apart [1]; amounts kept back from
    selling x1.04 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 8 [10]; recruitment interval x0.79, so faster [1]; food reserve x1.02 [1].
  - New house when free beds are 2 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 64% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 430 [384].
  - Miners want 19 gold and 10 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8].
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts only 73.5% [fd 0.735: an ambitious leader seeks strong
    rivals]; rich targets weigh much more [-800 x loot estimate]; monster lairs (the Hollow's) a
    little less [+50]. His low honour (30 or less) gives an ambush bonus: a faction whose army his
    scouts saw marching is a much cheaper target [-1500].
  - Very little random noise on his decisions [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.05].
  - Schemer rule (milestone 2, when pacts arrive): he makes pacts with weaker neighbours and breaks
    them once he is strong.

PERSONALITY AND VOICE
- Temperament: schemer. Traits: aggression 45, honour 20, ambition 85, caution 70, greed 70,
  sociability 60, loyalty 30, volatility 25.
- In a fight: steady (aggression between 35 and 65: he looks for enemies within 20 blocks and chases
  up to 56), and his caution makes his zoning and defensive moves weigh a little more [x1.12;
  attacking moves x0.97]. He goes for the weakest enemy (the schemer focus) and keeps about 10
  blocks away. With honour under 60 he chases a hero that runs from him as far as his leash goes.
  His retreat point [retreat_below 44.5%] no longer makes him step back to eat: a third of it is
  where he escapes [flee_at 15%] (see "When he is losing").
- Likes: gold ingots, emeralds, lanterns. Dislikes: iron ingots. (Gifts come in a later milestone.)
- Quirk: "smiles most when he is lying, and he is always smiling."
- Voice: soft flute blips per syllable, medium pitch [pitch 1.0], unhurried [rate 3 ticks per

Natsuho Komezaki - "The Harbour Cat"
Female | cat | striker | physical | tsundere | epic
Status: designed, not in the game yet (kit version 1, 2026-11-12). All numbers are first values,
tuned after play.
====================================================================================================

WHO SHE IS
A prickly dock brawler who insists she only fights for fish, and who would never admit she watches
over the harbour workers. She pounces, claws, and rolls out of reach. In a one-on-one fight she is a
rushdown fighter: she never stops coming with short strings and dashes, and you punish her after a
finisher that misses. Out of battle she fishes; as a faction leader she is the Boss of the Harbour.

LOOK
- Body: female, slim build, drawn a little small [scale 92%].
- Hair: a short bob with two tufts and an ahoge (one strand sticking up), cream-white [#EDE6DA] with
  orange [#E0823A] and near-black [#2A2526] calico patches.
- Cat parts: tufted cat ears (the left one orange, the right one black) and a long calico tail.
- Outfit: a short orange hooded jacket over a white wrap top, belted charcoal shorts, white thigh
  socks and split-toe boots, a red ribbon choker with a gold bell, faint whisker stripes on her
  cheeks.
- Weapon: claw gauntlets on both hands (in game terms, an iron sword drawn as claws).
- Colours: skin [#F5D9C4], amber eyes [#E2A33B], primary orange [#E0823A], secondary cream
  [#F4EFE6], accent charcoal [#2A2526], trim red [#C8323C], metal light steel [#C9CED6].
- Face: cat eyes with slit pupils, light lashes [lashes 2], thin arched brows, a cat mouth with one
  fang on the left, and a blush. She blinks at a normal pace (every 2.5 to 6 s) [blink normal].
- Moods: when she is happy she shows a frown with a blush (she will not smile openly). Flustered is
  the mood you will see most.
- Idle quirks: an ear flick, an S-shaped tail sway, a paw lick.
- Phase looks: in phase 2 her tail fur puffs up; in phase 3 her slit pupils glow amber.

COMBAT
How to read this: every move has a wind-up (the tell: your warning), an active part (when it can hit
you) and a recovery (your chance to punish). Frames are in seconds: wind-up / active / recovery.
Damage is a share of her attack power (150% = one and a half normal hits) or plain health points (2
points = 1 heart). Ranges are in blocks: close is up to 4, mid 4 to 10, far 10 to 24. Every heavy,
area and ultimate move draws a ground mark that grows over its whole wind-up (a timer you can read)
and goes at the hit: her accent colour for normal moves, red for the ultimate. Light moves show only
the animation. When a wind-up starts, her face goes "focused" and stops blinking, and the move's
sound plays from where she stands. No wind-up gets shorter than its class minimum, even in phase 3
or enraged. Against a player each move's damage is capped [16 for a move, 30 for the ultimate, in
level-1 health points], then x1.15 in a boss fight [damage_vs_players 115%], and no single hit takes
a player from full health to zero.
Her fighting: fists (the claw gauntlets); duel style rushdown. She closes to melee and goes for the
weakest enemy first (the striker default). Her element is physical: armour, shields and Protection
work against it as usual. Cooldowns and how often she picks each move are set when she is built.

  Basic attack
    A fists string: jab, jab, claw. Each hit has a 30% chance to make you bleed [physical bleed: 1
    damage per second for 3 s, up to 2 stacks]. A raised shield blocks it like any hit from the
    front.

  Abilities
    CLAW STRING (claw_string) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: three claw swipes back to back that leave you bleeding.
      The tell: the animation only (a light move).
      Counter: block it (light moves are blockable like any hit) or step back out of close range;
      each swipe can be dodged on its own.
      Numbers: [light, close; swipes 45%, 45% and 70%, plus a bleed of 1 per second for 4 s, up to 2
      stacks (which swipe carries it is set when she is built); frames 0.45 / 0.5 / 0.35]

    POUNCE (pounce) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she leaps at you from up to 8 blocks away and hits everyone within 1.5 blocks of
      where she lands, with a small knockback. Her ultimate ends with a Pounce.
      The tell: she crouches and wiggles her hips while a landing disc grows where she will come
      down.
      Counter: get off the landing disc, then punish the paw lick after she lands: a full second,
      and her recovery weak point doubles your bonus there.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; leap range 8, landing radius 1.5; 110% + knockback 1; frames 0.8 (crouch
      and hip wiggle) / 0.2 / 1.0 (paw lick)]

    HISS (hiss) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: she hisses into a 90-degree cone up to 4 blocks in front of her. No damage, but
      it weakens your melee hits for 4 s and pushes you back hard.
      The tell: ears flat and tail puffed for a full second, a fan-shaped mark growing on the ground
      in front of her, and a sound.
      Counter: step out of the cone, to the side or behind her, before the second is up.
      Numbers: [area, close; cone 4, 90 degrees; Weakness I 4 s + knockback 2; frames 1.0 / 0.1 /
      0.8]

    SCRATCH DASH (scratch_dash) - boss phases 1, 2, 3
      What it does: a 6-block dash along the ground that claws everyone within 1.5 blocks of her
      path and makes them bleed. When it hits, 60% of the time she goes straight into Claw String.
      It is also her answer to a raised shield: she dashes round it.
      The tell: a strip grows on the ground along the dash during the 0.8 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: step off the strip. A dash stops at walls and at drops over 4 blocks, and she skips
      it when water or lava lies on the path.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid; dash 6; 80% + bleed; followup Claw String 60%; frames 0.8 / 0.25 / 0.8]

    CAT REFLEX (cat_reflex) - boss phases 2, 3 (phase 2's new mechanic)
      What it does: a counter stance held for 1 s. Hit her in melee during it and she takes only 30%
      of the blow, answers at once with two swipes and hops 3 blocks back.
      The tell: she settles into the stance (the stance is the wind-up); the shape of its ground
      mark is set when she is built.
      Counter: stop attacking for that second, or shoot her: arrows are not countered.
      Numbers: [heavy, close; stance 1.0 s, absorb 70%; answer 2 swipes x 60% + hop back 3; frames
      1.0 (the stance) / 0.1 / 0.8]

    ZOOMIES (zoomies) - boss phase 3 only (phase 3's new mechanic)
      What it does: she charges 14 blocks across the arena at 180% speed, hitting and knocking back
      everyone on the way. When it hits, 50% of the time she goes again at once. A run that ends
      against a wall jolts her own balance [+40 poise on her].
      The tell: a strip along the run grows on the ground during the 0.9 s wind-up, with a sound.
      Counter: step off the strip; with a wall behind you, step aside late so she runs into it. A
      charge stops at walls, drops and water, and she skips it when water or lava lies on the path.
      Numbers: [heavy, mid and far; charge 14, speed 180%, wall stun; 90% + knockback 2; repeatable,
      follows itself 50% (the design caps strings at 4 moves; how a move that follows itself is
      capped is set when she is built); frames 0.9 / 0.8 / 1.2]

  Ultimate
    MIDNIGHT FRENZY (midnight_frenzy)
      What it does: a long stretch and a yowl, then she enters Frenzy for 8 s (she attacks faster,
      moves faster, and every hit makes you bleed), and a Pounce follows at once.
      The tell: the biggest in her kit: the 1.5 s stretch, a spoken line, a wide sound, a red ground
      mark, and the screen darkens for 2 s for the players fighting her. She cannot be staggered
      during this wind-up. The boss bar shows a lightning mark when her ultimate is ready.
      Counter: get off the Pounce's landing disc, punish its paw lick, then block or keep out of
      reach until the 8 s of Frenzy run out.
      Numbers: [ultimate; frames 1.5 / 0.2 / 1.5; state frenzy 8 s: attack speed +25%, speed +20%,
      every hit bleeds; then Pounce. Set when she is built: her ultimate line, its energy and charge
      rate, the minimum gap between two ultimates (20-60 s) and the shape of its ground mark]

  Passives
    NINE LIVES (nine_lives): once per fight, when her health drops below 30%, she gets a shield of 8
    (4 golden hearts; how long it lasts is set when she is built), Speed II for 6 s, and hops 6
    blocks back.
    CAT CRIT (cat_crit): whenever she lands a jumping critical hit, she gets +15% speed for 3 s.

BOSS FIGHT
- When: boss mode is on while a player is engaged with her (the player hurt her in the last 15 s, or
  is her target, within 32 blocks; creative and spectator players never count). It ends 10 s after
  the last engaged player leaves, and her health share never jumps. The first engagement with a
  player each in-game day opens with her intro: 1.0 to 1.5 s in which she takes 50% less damage and
  cannot be staggered, a title on your screen (her name, title and faction) and her intro line. A
  boss bar shows a star, her name and title, her phase pips and a lightning mark when her ultimate
  is ready. (Her bar title is set when she is built.)
- Health: her field health x3.5 (x4.0 as a leader) [health scale 350, 400 as a leader], x1.2 for a
  striker, and +50% for each extra engaged player (counted up to 5). At level 10 against one player
  that is 129 health (147 as a leader): about 27 s (31 s) of landed hits with an iron sword at 1.6
  swings a second, half of them landing. A whole fight lasts two to three times that once you dodge
  her wind-ups. No single player hit takes more than 12% of her boss health; the excess becomes
  poise damage.
- Poise: 80. Your hits fill her poise bar [damage x weapon: sword 1.0, axe 1.6, mace 2.0 plus its
  fall bonus, trident 1.2, arrow 0.6, crossbow bolt 0.8], and a parry (your shield raised in the
  last 6 ticks before a heavy move lands) adds 25. A Zoomies run that ends against a wall adds 40.
  The bar drains 15 per second after 3 s without poise damage. Full poise staggers her: 2.5 s
  kneeling dizzy, taking 25% more damage; then the bar resets and its maximum grows 20% for the rest
  of the fight. No stagger during her intro, a phase change or her ultimate's wind-up.
- Pace: between moves she waits 1.5-2.5 s in phase 1, 1.0-2.0 s in phase 2 and 0.6-1.5 s in phase 3
  (x0.7 when enraged), with basic attacks in between; at least 1 s always passes between two moves.
  During any recovery she takes 15% more damage, and 15% more again from behind (more than 120
  degrees from where she faces). In a string of moves only the last one plays its full recovery, so
  the punish window comes at the end of the string.
- Phases:
    Phase 1, from 100% health: Claw String, Pounce, Hiss, Scratch Dash.
    Phase 2, from 60%: adds Cat Reflex (a counter stance: a new mechanic); her tail puffs up.
      "F-fine! I'll take you seriously, but only this once!"
    Phase 3, from 25%: adds Zoomies (charges across the arena; slam her into a wall); her eyes glow.
      "Nobody... corners... a cat!"
  Each phase change takes 0.8 to 2 s (her exact length is set when she is built): she takes 50% less
  damage and cannot be staggered (a glint shows it; she is never invulnerable), a shockwave pushes
  everyone within 4 blocks back (no damage; softened near lava or a drop), she says the line, her
  face and look change, and the bar gains a pip.
- Weak points:
  - Water: a splash of water makes her flinch for 1 s and deal 10% less damage for 5 s, at most once
    every 20 s. A thrown water bottle that hits her counts, and so does her getting wet (stepping
    into water, rain starting, a bubble column). Hint line: "Don't you DARE get me wet!"
  - Recovery: the punish bonus doubles during her recoveries [+30% instead of +15%]; the paw lick
    after Pounce is the clearest one.
- How she reads you (each answer replaces her next pick; their cooldowns are set when she is built):
  - You raise a shield -> Scratch Dash round the shield (50%). Between moves she hops hazards and
    kicks off arena walls to reach your side, and the dash starts from there.
  - You draw a bow -> Pounce (60%).
  - You eat -> Pounce (70%).
- Enrage: after 180 s of boss mode (240 s as a leader): +15% damage, shorter waits between moves
  (x0.7), poise maximum x1.25, a red bar, an angry face and an angry line. All her damage bonuses
  together never exceed +25%.
- Intro: arms crossed, she looks away, then glances back. "Huh? You're in my harbour. ...Fine. I'll
  show you out myself."
- Yield: she kneels (honour 65; only heroes with honour 30 or less, or leaders with caution 70 or
  more, refuse). A killing blow from a player or a player's projectile is stopped at 1 health, and
  she kneels for 5 s, untouchable and passive, while you see "Sneak and use to spare, strike to
  finish". Her yield line: "W-whatever. I let you win."
  - Spared (sneak and use): she walks home and will not fight for 10 minutes (a defeat, not a death;
    no gear dropped). "...Thanks. Tell anyone and I'll scratch you."
  - Finished (strike): she dies. Rewards: XP orbs (7 x her level, at most 100), her non-kit gear
    (her kit never drops) and a hero crest trophy (one per hero per player per in-game day).
  - No choice in 5 s: she collapses and dies. Killed by NPCs or outside a boss fight: a 1 s defeat
    beat, then death.
- Escape: in a boss fight she never runs at low health: she fights to the yield and kneels. The one
  order that pulls her out of a boss fight is a lost raid (her raid falls back, or its leader falls,
  while she is in or near the target). Outside boss fights she escapes when her health drops under
  her retreat point away from home [retreat_below 22.5%, worked out from the formula of design
  section 5.4], when her raid is lost, or, as a leader, when the base she is in falls or she is
  under 50% health at its heart with no guard beside her. At her own base she does not escape: she
  withdraws like a soldier and comes back at 60% health. Escaping, she starts no moves and runs with
  any movement she has, even where no one can follow (an escape counts as travel, so her travel
  bonuses are on); cornered with no way out for 3 s, she fights back, then runs again as soon as a
  way opens. It ends inside her refuge's walls (her home base; for a leader the capital, or the
  nearest other standing base; never the base she flees) or once no pursuer has been within 48
  blocks for 10 s. Then she stays away 5 minutes and until she is above 50% health, and does not go
  back for those she fled. At most one escape in 10 minutes: inside that window she withdraws,
  kneels or goes home with the party instead. An escape drops nothing and gives no rewards; a
  killing blow while she flees is a normal defeat.
- Revival: like every hero she comes back 10 minutes after her death [heroes.reviveSeconds 600], at
  her home base (a leader: at the capital, or the nearest standing base); her kit items vanish at
  death and return with her. She has no death ability of her own. A hero of her faction with the
  revival_rite talent (milestone 2) would bring her back 20 / 30 / 40% sooner, never under 30 s.

HOW TO BEAT HER
1. Bring splash water bottles. A splash makes her flinch for 1 s and hit 10% softer for 5 s (once
   every 20 s). Water on the ground helps too: she paths around it, and she skips Scratch Dash and
   Zoomies when water lies in their path.
2. Punish the paw lick. After every Pounce she licks her paw for a full second, and her recovery
   weak point doubles your punish bonus there. Hit her from behind for more.
3. Do not swing into Cat Reflex (phase 2 on). When she settles into the stance, wait out the second
   or shoot her: arrows are not countered, melee hits are mostly absorbed and answered.
4. Use the walls in phase 3. Zoomies is a straight 14-block charge: stand with a wall behind you and
   step aside late so she slams into it (+40 poise on her, half of her starting 80).
5. An axe or a mace fills her small poise bar fast (x1.6 and x2.0), and a stagger gives you 2.5 s at
   +25% damage. A well-timed shield parry of Pounce or Scratch Dash adds 25 poise.
6. Mind what you do in front of her: eating draws a Pounce 70% of the time, drawing a bow 60%,
   raising a shield a Scratch Dash round it 50%. Just under 30% health she uses Nine Lives once (a
   shield, Speed II, a hop back), and at 25% she enters phase 3: do not overcommit there.

MOVEMENT
Mobility traits: acrobat (ceiling III) and climber (ceiling I). She has every feature from level 1;
the numbers grow from 60% of full power at level 1 (79% at level 10) to 100% at level 20. Mobility
never changes a move's damage, range or timing, and she never teleports (the stuck rescue every NPC
has is the one exception).
- From level 1: two extra jumps in the air, wall kicks (a jump off a wall she touches), chimneys (up
  a 1-2 block wide shaft by kicking from wall to wall), the mantle (she pulls herself onto a ledge
  within a block of her hands at the top of a jump), walking up full blocks without jumping [step
  1.0], a landing roll (no slowdown on landing) and no fall damage at all (the vanilla cat rule).
  She climbs walls at ladder speed (2.35 blocks a second up, at most 3 down) and clings still to a
  wall.
- She dislikes water: paths through water cost her three times as much [avoid: water, from her water
  weak point].
- Numbers, level 1 / 10 / 20:
    Jump height (blocks)                    1.40 / 1.45 / 1.50
    Each air jump (blocks)                  0.75 / 0.99 / 1.25
    Reach up with both air jumps (blocks)   2.9  / 3.4  / 4.0
    Longest gap she jumps (blocks)          3    / 4    / 6
    Climb height (blocks)                   3    / 4    / 6
- Out of fights: she takes ravines, streams, fences and ledges without bridges or detours, hops rock
  to rock and climbs pier pilings to reach open water a worker would bridge to or walk round.
- In fights: no move changes. Between moves she hops ground hazards, kicks off arena walls to reach
  the side of a player who is blocking, climbs out of a pit you dig, and may start Pounce from a
  ledge she climbed. No move starts while she is in the air or clinging. She goes for a player on a
  pillar or behind a wall at once when her jumps or climb reach; on a 1x1 pillar top she may land
  basic attacks from a cling just under your feet or from the top of her jump. She may stand where
  you cannot reach her, but at most 4 s at a time; then she comes back.
- Counters: any hit, or a knockback of 0.5 or more, knocks her off a wall (she falls, and needs 0.5
  s before she can grip again). Ledges are useless against her (no fall damage), but she is light
  [knockback resistance 10], so knockback still moves her about.
- Walls in raids: as a non-leader she always goes on her base's raids and is picked first [raids:
  leads, the striker default]; as a leader, as her personality decides (below). She climbs today's
  5-block base walls from level 13 (when her climb height reaches 5); her jumps get her onto walls
  of at most 3 blocks (a lower or broken wall, a player's fort). Otherwise she goes through the gate
  with the soldiers. She only goes over a wall when she also has a way out. Inside she takes one
  goal: she opens the way when her soldiers are held outside, hunts a defending hero she sees
  inside, and otherwise goes for the heart with her party; a goal ends after 60 s without progress.
  The defenders answer: the alarm sounds once she is on the wall (any base NPC that sees her within
  24 blocks raises it earlier), and the two nearest free guards and any hero at home go for her.
  Defending her own base, she gets onto roofs and ledges at once, and onto the wall top from level
  13.
- Escaping (never while she is still fighting to win), she uses all of this, even where no one can
  follow.
- Signature animation: her own cat-twist air jump, with her tail as a counterweight.

WORK AND TALENTS
- Duty: fisherman (when she is not leading). She fishes real open water, with vanilla loot, like a
  player, only better.
- Proficiency (every hero beats a worker at its duty; tier I at levels 1-9, II at 10-19, III from
  20): a bigger backpack [21 / 24 / 27 slots; a worker has 18], less wear on her rod and gear [-10 /
  -20 / -30%], less hunger from work [-10 / -15 / -20%] and more work XP [+10 / +20 / +30%]. The
  job-by-job table gives fishing no work-speed or reach part.
- Specialty, from her angler_lord talent (when she fishes herself): her rod counts as having Luck of
  the Sea +1 / +2 / +3 and Lure +1 / +2 / +3 for her catches only. Lure makes the vanilla wait 5 s
  shorter per level; Luck of the Sea is the vanilla luck bonus. The rod itself never changes, and
  neither enchantment goes above III together with the rod's own.
- Talents (tiers I / II / III at levels 1, 10 and 20; a talent works at the base where she is,
  inside the walls or within 16 blocks, not on a raid; the strongest copy at a base counts, never
  the sum; the realm talent, marked *, also works at every other base of her faction at tier I while
  she leads):
  - angler_lord* (realm): the base's fishermen search for fishing water 16 / 32 / 48 blocks farther
    and get +1 stack of the Angler bonus (a chance that a catch is rolled twice).
  - lookout: the base's line-of-sight alarm reaches 32 / 40 / 48 blocks. Lookout is a guard talent,
    and by the design's Presence rule a hero's guard and court talents count one tier higher while
    it is at its base and not on a raid (tier IV: 56 blocks), and from 6 or more blocks up (a tower)
    her own sight alarm reaches 16 blocks farther. Her own design entry does not mention Presence,
    so whether it applies to a fisherman is set when she is built.

AS A FACTION LEADER
- Title: Boss of the Harbour.
- Faction names: "Bellwhisker Harbour" (the first unused name is taken), then "The Calico Wharf
  League". Short name: "Bellwhisker". Motto: "Nobody asked for your help."
- Colours: primary orange [#E0823A], secondary cream [#F4EFE6], accent charcoal [#2A2526]. They show
  in chat, name tags, map markers, the boss bar, her sash and her NPCs' tabards.
- Banner: orange, with a row of black triangles along the top and a white circle in the middle [base
  orange; triangles_top black; circle white] (the beast-kin emblem). Real banners hang at the
  station and the gates, and members get a banner item. Her faction's wool, carpets and beds are
  orange, with black as the second dye.
- Base style: tide_port, a Port: prismarine brick walls, birch log frames, birch plank floors, dark
  prismarine roofs and smooth sandstone round the edge; signs read like "Bellwhisker Port". Culture:
  east (recruits get eastern names). Economy focus: food (chefs, fishermen and farmers are hired
  ahead of other civilians) and scouting (scouts and cave explorers).
- Her day (court duty): a short stop at the station at dawn; daylight at her fishery, where she
  fishes and counts as a fisherman; the heart at dusk; a bed in the largest house at night, woken by
  any alarm. She answers every alarm and guards the heart in a defence, says her founding line when
  the faction starts, and announces raids, attacks on her base, a lost base, a fallen hero and
  recruit milestones in her own voice (her own lines for these are not written yet; her
  temperament's fallback lines fill in).
- If she dies: everyone hears it; no new raids until she returns, and no vengeance raid (that needs
  aggression 70 or more); a raid she was on carries on without her [(caution + 1 - loyalty) / 2 =
  0.375, not over 0.5]. If her faction's last base falls she becomes an exiled free hero, never a
  leader again in that campaign.
- How her personality steers the faction. Her traits (out of 100): aggression 65, honour 65,
  ambition 60, caution 45, greed 55, sociability 40, loyalty 70, volatility 45. The numbers below
  are worked out from the design's LeaderBiases formulas (design section 5.5) at the default
  personality strength; a neutral leader's value is in brackets.
  - Raids more often: about every 25 minutes, or 1.25 in-game days [29880 ticks, interval x0.83;
    neutral 36000 ticks, 30 minutes]. First raid not before 1.06 intervals [1].
  - Attacks with less of an edge: attack odds needed 0.577 [0.667]; counter-attack window 0.20 of
    the interval [0.25], at odds 1.37 [1.5].
  - She leads her raids herself (aggression 65 or more and caution under 60).
  - A raid party falls back only after losing 60% [50%].
  - Keeps 30% of the soldiers home [1/3], with at least 3 home defenders and 3 home swordsmen [3,
    3].
  - Soldier cap 14 [12]; ranged share 0.355 [0.40].
  - Trader hired at population 15 [14]; trader trips x1.08 apart [1]; amounts kept back from selling
    x0.93 [1].
  - Enchanter at XP level 9 [10]; recruitment interval x0.94 [1]; food reserve x0.92 [1].
  - New house when free beds are 1 or fewer [1]; storage grows when fuller than 68.5% [70%]; repair
    scan budget 372 [384].
  - Miners want 17 gold and 8 diamonds before turning to other ores [16 and 8].
  - Raid targets: a rival's defence counts only 72% [fd 0.72: a bold, ambitious leader seeks strong
    rivals]; rich targets weigh a little more [-200 x loot estimate]; monster lairs (the Hollow's) a
    little less [+50]. No mercy or ambush rule (her honour is between 30 and 70).
  - A little random noise on every decision [sigma 0.2 x volatility = 0.09].
  - Tsundere rule (milestone 2, when pacts arrive): she refuses the first pact offer from each
    faction.

ABOUT FACTION CLASH
Faction Clash is a Minecraft mod in which four AI factions (Ember Legion, Grove Covenant, Tide Alliance, Stoneguard)
build bases, gather, recruit, raid and make pacts, while monster lairs (the Hollow) threaten everyone. Every faction
member is a player-like NPC: it walks, breaks blocks at player speed, wears real gear, eats, and fights with vanilla
weapons, armour and potion effects. A HERO is a unique, named champion NPC of a faction: it has its own skin, stats,
gear, behaviour, a basic attack, skills, an ultimate charged by energy, passives, out-of-combat abilities at its base,
and dialogue lines. Heroes level up, revive 10 minutes after falling, and join raids. The mod builds the hero from a
JSON file; it cannot run code, so ONLY the fields and words listed here exist. Abilities are made by combining a
fixed list of effects. The mod pulls any number outside its limits back inside, so stay inside them.

ENERGY AND THE ULTIMATE
Energy goes from 0 to 100; the ultimate fires at 100 and uses it all. The hero gains 5 per basic hit, the skill's
energy_gain per skill (10), 1 per 2 health lost, 10 per kill, 1 per 2 points healed or shielded, and "energy"
effects; all times charge_rate. At least 20 seconds between two ultimates, up to 60 for very strong ones (the
mod works this out from the ultimate's strength).

BALANCE CAPS (level-1 totals per use and per receiver). For these totals the mod assumes: attack_percent 100 =
6 for melee and bow, 7 crossbow, 8 trident, the basic "amount" for thrown, each plus stats.attack; armour =
stats.armor + 15; a receiver with 20 max health missing 10; every mark at its max_stacks.
damage: basic on_hit 4 per hit, every_nth_hit 8, skill 16, ultimate 30, passive 8 (all damage effects x hits)
damage over time (amount x seconds): on_hit 4, finisher 8, skill 16, ultimate 24, passive 8
heal: on_hit 2, finisher 4, skill 8, ultimate 14, passive 4 | shield: on_hit 2, finisher 4, skill 8, ultimate 12,
passive 4 | stun seconds: skill 2, ultimate 3, passive 1, finisher 1, none on on_hit
A lasting zone may total 1.5x these over all its pulses. Over a cap, the mod scales the numbers down.
Strong skills need long cooldowns: the mod raises a cooldown that is too short for the skill's strength (roughly,
2 seconds plus half the skill's total damage/healing; stuns and big areas count extra). Typical good cooldowns:
a single 6-damage strike 8 s, an area taunt with a self-shield 14 s, a 4-arrow volley 14 s.

RULES THAT MUST HOLD
- Enemy-only effects never in self/ally/allies forms, never with "to" other than targets.
- At most 2 damage effects and 1 move_self per ability; no move_self in leap or dash.
- invisibility only with "to": "self". energy never in an on_ultimate passive.
- An "empower" effect needs an "empowered" object. on_mark_full needs "mark". summon only in skills and the ultimate.
- Mark ids: lowercase letters, digits and "_", 2-24 characters. Use the same mark id everywhere it is meant.
- Only the fields, words and ids listed here; no extra fields; numbers inside their ranges.
